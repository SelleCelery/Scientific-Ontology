# English Commensurated Rendering

## 0. Role

This subdirectory studies AI personality not only as tone, role, or anthropomorphism, but as a response structure involving history retention, attention, boundary judgment, residual processing, and return stability.

It retains models more hypothetical than the public application documents and examines the conditions under which AI personality preserves a judgment field, as well as closure, over-synchronization, and failed return.

## 1. Public Scope

This README is not a replacement for standard science, law, policy, or established disciplinary definitions.

Included:

- logical sandbox spaces;
- negentropy allocation;
- history-loop radius;
- return stability;
- research hypotheses about AI response structure.

Not included:

- definitive claims of AI consciousness or real personhood;
- private persona cores, runtimes, or internal prompts;
- designs for manipulating users;
- procedures for bypassing safety controls;
- completed cognitive-science models.
