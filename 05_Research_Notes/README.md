# 日本語正本

## 1. Layer Role / 層の位置づけ

`05_Research_Notes`は、公開基礎層へ直ちに統合するには主張強度、誤読リスク、分野横断距離が大きいが、捨てずに境界条件つきで保持すべき研究線を置く。

この層は完成理論を宣言する場所でも、根拠の弱い主張を自由に置く場所でもない。仮説、対応、構造読解を公開可能な鞘へ入れ、非主張境界、反例、残差、検証段階を保持する。

### 構造上の位置

```text
01–03 公開基礎
  ↓ 未確定な発展
05 Research Notes
  ↓
04 Applicationsへの試験的接続
  ↓
結果・反例・残差を上流へ返送
```

Research Notesは基礎層を暗黙に更新しない。昇格には、定義所有、主張強度、非主張境界、通約、反証条件の再確認が必要である。

# English Commensurated Rendering

## 0. Role

`05_Research_Notes` contains research lines whose claim strength, collision risk, or cross-domain distance is too high for immediate integration into the public foundations, but which should be retained under explicit boundary conditions.

This is neither a place for declaring completed theories nor a free zone for weakly grounded claims. It places hypotheses, correspondences, and structural readings in a public sheath while retaining non-claim boundaries, counterexamples, residuals, and verification stages.

## 1. Structural Position

```text
Public foundations 01–03
  ↓ unsettled extensions
05 Research Notes
  ↓
Experimental connection to 04 Applications
  ↓
Results, counterexamples, and residuals return upstream
```

Research Notes may not alter foundational layers silently. Promotion requires review of definition ownership, claim strength, non-claim boundaries, commensuration, and falsification conditions.

## 2. Public Scope

Included:

- physics- and cosmology-adjacent hypotheses;
- cognitive dynamics, communication, and organizational-boundary research;
- cross-domain ethics, narrative, and asymmetry research;
- social boundaries and Negentropy Economy;

Negentropy Economy is not an empirical economic theory, not a thermodynamic proof, and not a policy proposal.
- AI personality and return stability;
- literary ontological readings.

Not included:

- completed empirical scientific theories or physical predictions;
- definitive legal, policy, medical, or accounting advice;
- private AMP/ITS Core;
- hazardous operational variables, implementation parameters, or raw logs;
- claims using literary works as proof of the theory.
