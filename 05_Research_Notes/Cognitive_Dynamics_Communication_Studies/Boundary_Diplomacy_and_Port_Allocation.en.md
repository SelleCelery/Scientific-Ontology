## 0. Positioning

This document presents a dynamic model of how a boundary system with finite cognitive and processing capacity receives multiple differences and existence phases through particular ports, how far it compresses them, what it holds, what it commensurates, and what it reopens.

This document owns one question:

> When multiple cognitive axes, existence phases, language games, and purposes come into contact, how can communicability be maintained without collapsing them into a single axis and without demanding infinite processing?

This document does not define the generation of cognitive axes themselves. That question is delegated to [`Entropy_Attributed_Difference_and_Cognitive_Axis_Formation.en.md`](../../02_Raj_Beauty/Entropy_Attributed_Difference_and_Cognitive_Axis_Formation.en.md).

The common structure that cuts a Cognitive Field into operational Ports is imported from the upstream intermediate model [`Cognitive_Dynamics_Communication_Model.en.md`](Cognitive_Dynamics_Communication_Model.en.md).

This document does not own the diagnosis of existence-phase compression itself. That question is handled by [`Compression_of_Existence_Phases.en.md`](Compression_of_Existence_Phases.en.md).

This document does not implement ports as responsibility, authority, audit, and stop conditions. That question is handled by [`Organizational_Boundary_and_Port_Model.en.md`](Organizational_Boundary_and_Port_Model.en.md).

This document addresses the control principles between those layers.

```text
differences / cognitive axes / existence phases
        ↓
port selection
        ↓
passage / holding / blocking / transformation
        ↓
response / residual retention / reopening
        ↓
port reallocation / boundary update
```

## 1. Finite Cognitive Capacity and Port Selection

Neither humans nor organizations can process every difference at the same time.

Cognition operates by foregrounding some differences through concern and sending others into the background through abstraction.

Restricting ports is therefore not itself a failure.

The problem begins when a temporary or local restriction of ports is mistaken for the only correct entrance to the world as a whole.

A hospital must prioritize a medical port, a court a legal-judgment port, and a research institution a verification port.

However, restricted operation becomes single-axis reduction when living circumstances that cannot be processed through the medical port are treated as irrelevant, pain that cannot be processed through the legal port is treated as nonexistent, or meaning that does not enter the verification port is treated as without value.

Port selection requires a purpose, duration, scope, processing capacity, return path, and reopening conditions.

---

## 2. Multi-Port Potentiality

Peace must not be defined as the permanent opening of every port.

Permanent openness exceeds processing capacity and dissolves boundaries.

What peace requires is not universal port openness, but **Multi-Port Potentiality**.

Multi-Port Potentiality is a condition in which currently unused cognitive forms, language games, responsibility pathways, and methods of dialogue are not erased and can be reopened or newly established when necessary.

Its minimum conditions are:

- plurality of ports;
- selectability;
- records of reasons for closure;
- reopenability;
- capacity to establish new ports;
- retention of untranslatable residuals; and
- a return path for appeal.

Being unable to process something at present is not the same as its nonexistence.

A state of Multi-Port Potentiality preserves this distinction.

---

## 3. Scoped Port Operation

**Scoped Port Operation** means restricting the communication openings currently used for processing according to purpose, situation, duration, and responsibility.

A scoped operation must make the following explicit.

| Item | Question |
|---|---|
| Purpose | Why is this port being prioritized? |
| Scope | What is included, and what is excluded? |
| Duration | How long will the restriction remain in effect? |
| Authority | Who decided the restriction? |
| Residual | Where will excluded differences be retained? |
| Return Path | Who can return an objection? |
| Reopen Condition | What occurrence will cause another port to open? |
| Stop Condition | What occurrence will stop the current operation? |

A restriction is justified only within its explicitly stated boundary.

When a scoped port is used beyond its applicable range, other existence phases are misclassified.

---

## 4. Criteria for Port Allocation

Port allocation is not a simple priority list.

At minimum, the following must be collated.

### 4.1 Primary Purpose

What must be protected, and what must not be lost?

### 4.2 Type of Difference

Is the difference information, pain, objection, danger, learning, relation, or an institutional demand?

### 4.3 Urgency

Will delay produce irreversible loss?

### 4.4 Reversibility

Can correction, compensation, or reopening occur later?

### 4.5 Residual and Residue

What will the current processing omit, and where will that omission accumulate?

### 4.6 Responsibility Boundary

Who bears responsibility for processing, holding, blocking, or transforming the difference?

### 4.7 Processing Capacity

Can the port understand the input, return a response, and retain exceptions?

### 4.8 Destination of Externalization

Who will bear the burden that is not processed?

### 4.9 Re-collatability

Even if the matter cannot be closed at present, can it later be reopened from another depth, port, or language game?

## 5. Cognitive Ports

Ports are not limited to organizational service windows.

Cognition also has forms through which differences are received.

Vision, hearing, bodily sensation, language, number, narrative, institution, ethics, and history can each be read as a Cognitive Port that foregrounds different kinds of difference.

### 5.1 The Visual Port as a Three-Dimensional Spatial Axis

In visual cognition, differences are readily configured as arrangements along a three-dimensional spatial axis.

Distance, direction, contour, front and back, and inside and outside form strongly stabilized cognitive configurations through visual and motor experience.

This spatial axis is an important port that makes physical action possible.

However, what cannot be placed in three-dimensional space is not therefore nonexistent.

History, meaning pressure, responsibility, relation, Intrinsic Time, and distance between language games require other ports.

This document treats the three-dimensional spatial axis not as the sole coordinate system of cognition, but as one powerfully stabilized Cognitive Port.

## 6. Logical-Depth Routing

Communication that can be processed on the current logical plane should be handled on that plane.

Some unresolved matters, contradictions, branches, residuals, residue, and meaning pressure, however, cannot be closed through a correct-or-incorrect judgment alone.

What is required in such cases is routing in the logical-depth direction.

A Depth Port is not a place that declares a problem solved.

It is an entry through which differences that cannot be closed on the current plane are retained so that they may later be re-collated with history, another concept, another experience, another institution, or another language game.

```text
unprocessability on the current plane
  ↓
Open Marker
  ↓
Depth Port
  ↓
retention of history / meaning pressure / branches
  ↓
re-collation with another port or language game
  ↓
return to the current plane or explicit retention as unresolved
```

Holding is not forgetting.

A Depth Port requires a reason for holding, a responsible actor, conditions for re-collation, and history.

## 7. Commensuration between Language Games

Different cultures, disciplines, institutions, organizations, and personalities operate through different language games.

This document does not independently define commensuration in general. Its canonical owner is [`Commensuration_as_Cross_Grammatical_Collation.en.md`](../Language_Meaning_and_Communication_Phase_Studies/Commensuration_as_Cross_Grammatical_Collation.en.md), while the upstream contract for Core Invariant and Operational Drift is imported from [`Optional_Axiom_Modules_as_Cognitive_Bridge.en.md`](../../03_Tam_Goodness/Optional_Axiom_Modules_as_Cognitive_Bridge.en.md).

Applied to Boundary Diplomacy, the canonical definition means that commensuration does not align different systems within a single vocabulary. It transforms a structure into a form reachable from another cognitive network while preserving its core structure, transformation residuals, and return paths.

This document distinguishes:

- **Core Invariant**: the core without which the same idea, purpose, or responsibility structure would no longer remain;
- **Operational Drift**: the expressive and operational layer that may change according to object, era, culture, and use; and
- **Untranslatable Residual**: difference that remains even after transformation.

Logical depth may also be read as the distance required to reach a Core across multiple language games.

Commensuration does not erase distance.

It creates a pathway across it.

## 8. Diplomatic Boundary Interface

Diplomacy is the work of maintaining communicability while preserving the boundaries of both sides rather than internalizing the other party.

A **Diplomatic Boundary Interface** detects communication, transforms it, and allocates pathways between different ports, protocols, language games, and responsibility boundaries.

Its functions include:

- identifying the other party's boundary;
- locating communicable ports;
- making non-shared domains explicit;
- transforming expression while preserving the Core;
- not concealing untranslatable residuals;
- enabling holding, escalation, and renegotiation; and
- retaining pathways for withdrawal and reconnection.

Diplomacy is not the manufacture of agreement.

It is boundary control that avoids destructive cutoff while retaining disagreement.

## 9. Asymmetric Communication

Communication is not always symmetrical.

Even between conscious agents, cognitive capacity, authority, language, body, time, and history differ.

Furthermore, nature, bodies, institutions, devices, and environments do not necessarily possess consciousness in the same sense as humans.

Even so, the side possessing cognition and authority bears responsibility for reading the other side's boundary conditions, resistance, damage, and returns.

Silence is not consent. The absence of a response in human language does not mean that no return exists.

If the attitude of Buber's "I-Thou" relation is borrowed structurally, it does not mean personifying the other. It means treating the other as a boundary that cannot be fully reduced to one's own use.

When nature is treated only as a resource, the body only as labor power, or an institution only as a command apparatus, the ports required to read returns are lost.

In asymmetric communication, the side possessing greater cognition and authority bears greater responsibility.

## 10. Processing Capacity and Meaning Pressure

Every port has a processing capacity.

Processing capacity is not merely the number of inputs that can be handled.

It includes the capacity to understand differences, collate them with history, hold exceptions, return responses, and route them to responsible actors.

Inputs that exceed capacity do not disappear.

They appear as queues, template responses, discard, exhaustion of responsible staff, informal pathways, and externalization.

**Meaning Pressure** is the pressure produced when differences that do not yet possess an adequate pathway, language, or return path accumulate at a boundary.

As Meaning Pressure increases, organizations and individuals tend to recede into a single axis instead of increasing their ports.

```text
reduced capacity
  ↓
fewer ports
  ↓
template processing
  ↓
expanded abstraction
  ↓
externalization of Meaning Pressure
  ↓
increase in enemies / noise / exceptions
```

What is needed is not unlimited acceptance.

It is the separation, expansion, holding, prioritization, suspension, and reopening of ports.

## 11. Boundary Control Cycle

Boundary diplomacy and port allocation operate through the following cycle.

```text
difference detection
  ↓
cognitive-axis collation
  ↓
port selection
  ↓
commensuration / transformation
  ↓
processing / holding / blocking / escalation
  ↓
response
  ↓
residual retention
  ↓
port reallocation
  ↓
boundary update
```

If a detected difference is not visible through the current cognitive axis, it is returned to the upstream cognitive-axis model.

If existence phases are being collapsed into a single metric, it is returned to the diagnosis of existence-phase compression.

If responsibility, authority, or audit is insufficient, it is returned to the organizational-boundary model.

## 12. Cognitive Weakening and Single-Axis Reduction

Cognitive weakening does not mean only a lack of knowledge.

It means a reduction in the differences, ports, language games, and time horizons that can be handled, forcing different existence phases into the same evaluative axis.

```text
decline in processing capacity
  ↓
reduction in available ports
  ↓
narrowing of concern
  ↓
expansion of abstraction
  ↓
contraction of meaning experience
  ↓
compression into a single metric
  ↓
antagonization of different existence phases
  ↓
decline in diplomatic capacity
```

Within this cycle, complexity itself is experienced as an enemy.

The other party's different language is treated as malice, exceptions as rule violations, holding as incompetence, and depth as ambiguity.

Work toward peace consists in reversing this cycle.

## 13. Failure Types of Diplomatic Interfaces

A diplomatic boundary node can acquire considerable power because it knows multiple languages and ports.

The intermediary itself must therefore be audited.

### 13.1 Gateway Capture

A particular intermediary monopolizes communication pathways, translation criteria, and connection destinations.

### 13.2 Diplomatic Interface Monopoly

The parties are prevented from communicating directly, and all information is reconstructed by the intermediary.

### 13.3 Concealment of Translation Criteria

The distinction between Core and Drift is not disclosed, making it impossible to inspect what was transformed.

### 13.4 Concealment of Residuals

Differences that could not be transformed are treated as though they never existed in order to manufacture agreement.

### 13.5 False Neutrality

The intermediary behaves as though it possessed no cognitive axis, interest, or authority of its own.

### 13.6 The Gray Witch Problem

As a literary alternative name, this document calls the condition in which a person capable of commensurating multiple boundaries privately controls the overall map and connection authority, and uses disagreement as a resource for preserving their own position rather than mediating it, the **Gray Witch Problem**.

The problem is not being gray.

The problem is that an intermediary possessing the overall map is not open to collation by anyone else.

## 14. Connection to the Organizational-Boundary Model

The output of this document is a port-allocation policy.

Implementing it within an organization requires:

- responsible actors;
- authority;
- input conditions;
- protocols;
- logs;
- audit;
- stop conditions;
- reopening conditions;
- appeal; and
- boundary update.

Diplomacy must not be left to goodwill alone.

It is necessary to implement who may open and close each port, who transforms communication, who retains residuals, and who audits the intermediary.

## 15. Connection to AI, DSSI, and Multi-Agent Systems

AI can function as a Diplomatic Interface that transforms multiple language games and connects different information sources.

At the same time, AI carries the risk of compressing differences into a single axis through its objective function, training data, and output format.

When AI is used as a diplomatic node, the following must be asked:

- Which cognitive axes does it amplify?
- Which differences does it abstract away?
- Does it display untranslatable residuals?
- Does it assist or block direct communication between the parties?
- Who audits the AI's transformation criteria?
- Can humans switch to another port?

DSSI can be read as a candidate implementation that supports port management in digital environments through pre-input warnings, holding, transmission control, history retention, cutoff, and reopening.

In a multi-agent environment, the purposes, authority, history, ports, and responsibility return paths of each AI agent must be separated.

## 19. Provisional Conclusion

Peace does not mean understanding everything at once or opening every port.

Under finite cognitive capacity, it means selecting necessary ports without erasing existence phases that are not currently in use, while retaining reopenability and untranslatable residuals.

Scoped Port Operation is necessary.

However, a restriction must not be transformed into the sole axis of the world as a whole.

Boundary Diplomacy creates passage, holding, transformation, and return paths between different cognitive axes, existence phases, and language games without assimilating the other party.

The intermediary is also a boundary and must therefore remain subject to audit and appeal.
