## 0. Position

Words can be close and still fail to communicate. Concepts from different fields can sometimes connect deeply through a single act of collation. Even within the same organization, language, or institution, mutual maps may not be transformable if participants read different things as gravity and expect different forms of return.

This cannot be explained by distance within a single semantic space alone.

As the central placement document for Language, Meaning, and Communication Phase Studies, this note addresses:

- what counts as a node;
- which relations count as edges;
- along how many axes distance should be read;
- which bandwidths are narrow and which directions alone are wide;
- whether a reply actually became a return path;
- how history changes the map itself; and
- how residuals that cannot be retained under the current computational rules demand a different computational optimality.

This note is not an authoritative source above the other eight documents. It is a central collational surface that places their concepts and makes conflicts and return paths visible.

At a deeper level of Scientific Ontology, however, the root structure of existence itself is provisionally rendered as a network. The concentration here of distance, bandwidth, return paths, gravity, phase transition, time, and entropy is therefore not treated as an editorial accident. Rather than divide the responsibilities, the document separates claim phases.

## 1. Central Thesis

> A semantic network is not a single graph consisting only of words or concepts. It is a configuration containing heterogeneous nodes such as words, speech units, propositions, concepts, subjects, documents, institutions, and language games, with typed relations such as grammar, reference, causality, history, authority, responsibility, affect, and commensuration, and including multilayer, temporal, and higher-order interactions.
>
> Commensurability is determined not by a single distance but by the overlap of a distance vector, direction-dependent bandwidths, port configurations, return expectancy, shared history, and the capacity to preserve residuals.

A semantic network must therefore not be reduced to “a map connecting similar words with lines.”

At the depth-hypothesis layer, nodes, distances, distributions, and recognition are read as emerging when separation and localization arise from dense relational possibility. This reading must not be promoted into an empirical fact of the public operational layer.

## 3. Communication Phase

This note does not define communication in general.

A **communication phase** is the local way in which, from the position of a particular cognition, language game, medium, or institution, communication is segmented as something, given paths and return paths, and assigned residuals.

The same contact foregrounds different things as communication in different phases.

- In a medical phase, symptoms and returns from intervention are foregrounded.
- In a legal-institutional phase, authority, procedure, evidence, and responsibility are foregrounded.
- In a literary phase, world conditions, narration, and unclosed histories are foregrounded.
- In an AI phase, input, context, generation paths, and post-output conditions of use are foregrounded.

A communication phase is not an independent world. It is a local language game that uses the same contact through different port configurations and grammatical frames.

## 4. Node Types

If different kinds of beings or objects are treated as the same type of node, the meaning of relations collapses.

This note distinguishes at minimum:

| Node type | Examples | Main persistence conditions | Caution |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sign unit | Token, word, sign | Repeatability, sign system | Does not own meaning as a whole by itself |
| Speech unit | Utterance, sentence, gesture, silence | Speech situation, order, medium | One segmentation of original pressure |
| Proposition | Relation structure available for judgment | Vocabulary, grammar, reference | Not identical with a speech act |
| Concept | Operational unit bundling multiple propositions, examples, and counterexamples | Definition, lineage, history of use | High-density terms cannot be exhausted by one definition |
| Subject | Judgment position of a human, AI, or group | Body, history, authority, continuity | Do not collapse them into one type |
| Document | Paper, narrative, rule, log | Fixed text, version, citation, reader | Separate author, content, and reader effect |
| Organization / institution | Company, administration, academic field, UI | Rules, roles, resources, audit | Do not identify with a personal subject |
| Language game | Research field, practice, creative work, religious practice | Vocabulary, operations, success conditions, form of life | Local operational system, not a closed system |

Node types may be increased or reduced according to the observational purpose. Because changing the type system can change conclusions, it must not be done implicitly.

## 5. Edge Types

The single phrase “connected” is insufficient.

| Edge type | What it connects | Example of return | Failure example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Grammatical relation | Order and roles among words, phrases, and speech units | Syntactic analysis, repair | Explaining effects through syntax alone |
| Semantic relation | Similarity, opposition, implication, usage | Concept revision | Turning similarity into grounding |
| Reference | Sign and object/history | Referent confirmation | Failed reference, same name for different objects |
| Causal / generative relation | Event and subsequent conditions | Consequence, prediction revision | Turning correlation into causation |
| Historical relation | Past contact and present response | Reactivation, learning | Erasing history and viewing only the present |
| Affective relation | Contact and qualitative response | Approach, avoidance, meaning pressure | Treating emotion as propositional truth |
| Authority relation | Accrediting and accredited nodes | Adoption, exclusion, credibility | Treating authority as grounds for truth |
| Responsibility relation | Return of judgment, action, and consequence | Correction, compensation, audit | Cutting the return path from a harmful node to consequences |
| Commensuration relation | Different grammatical or conceptual systems | Transformation, residual ledger | Pretending identity |
| Resource relation | Information, time, money, bodily load | Supply, metabolism, maintenance cost | Making the source of supply invisible |

Several edges can overlap between the same pair of nodes. A manager and subordinate, for example, can simultaneously be connected through command, evaluation, trust, salary, fear, and learning.

## 6. Binary Edges Are Not Enough

In actual meaning formation and social operation, interactions among three or more elements can form a single condition.

Speaker, recipient, rules, audience, past conversation, and future evaluation can act simultaneously on one sentence. Reducing them to pairwise relations can erase constraints carried by the field as a whole.

This note therefore permits higher-order relations where necessary.

- pressure of silence produced by an entire meeting;
- a definition established through a set of multiple documents;
- an evaluative field jointly produced by an article, comments, and algorithmic recommendation;
- burdens distributed across a family or organization.

This is not a claim that mathematical higher-order network models can be directly applied. It is a representational choice intended to preserve relations that pairwise reduction would erase.

## 7. Port Configurations and Language Games

A single port is a contact location.

Meaning, definition, and commensuration operate through configurations of multiple ports.

```text
input port
  ↓
grammaticalization / transformation port
  ↓
hold / residual port
  ↓
judgment / implementation port
  ↓
return port for results / objections / responsibility
```

Even within the same language game, meaning formation changes according to which ports are open.

There may be an input port for technical vocabulary but no port for counterexamples. There may be a citation port but no return path to the source. There may be a policy-proposal port but no route by which consequences from the field return.

A language game can then close locally while tending toward false closure.

If each Research Notes directory is designed as a local language game, it needs an object domain, vocabulary, permitted operations, conditions of collation, non-claim boundaries, residual storage, and upstream return paths.

## 8. Distance Vector

Semantic distance is not one-dimensional.

The distance between two propositions, subjects, or systems is provisionally read as the following vector:

```text
D = (
  d_lexical,
  d_grammatical,
  d_conceptual,
  d_historical,
  d_bodily,
  d_value,
  d_institutional,
  d_power,
  d_logical_depth
)
```

This is not yet a numerical metric. It is a diagnostic surface for distinguishing which distance obstructs commensuration.

### 8.1 Lexical Distance

Can the same terms be used? Surface vocabulary can be close while concepts remain distant.

### 8.2 Grammatical Distance

In what form are subject, time, causality, responsibility, and reservation segmented?

### 8.3 Conceptual Distance

How far do the intension, extension, and operational conditions of the same term overlap?

### 8.4 Historical Distance

What experiences, arguments, and revisions were passed through before reaching the same proposition?

### 8.5 Bodily-Experience Distance

Has the same explanation been received through comparable bodily load or practical contact?

### 8.6 Value Distance

What is protected, and what cost is considered acceptable?

### 8.7 Institutional / Power Distance

Who can speak, define, refuse, or withdraw?

### 8.8 Logical-Depth Distance

Not path length within one rule plane, but how many changes of observational position, conceptual redefinitions, or shifts in computational optimality are required to preserve residuals.

Even when lexical distance is large, commensuration can be easy if historical, bodily, and logical-depth distances are close. Even with identical vocabulary, communication can fail when value, power, or historical distances are large.

## 9. Bandwidth Vector

Communication bandwidth is likewise not a single quantity.

```text
B(A→B) = (
  b_signal,
  b_segmentation,
  b_meaning_preservation,
  b_residual_preservation,
  b_return,
  b_commensuration
)
```

### 9.1 Signal Bandwidth

How much sign sequence, sound, or data can be carried. This is the lower channel where collation with Shannon-type bandwidth is possible.

### 9.2 Segmentation Bandwidth

How finely can differences be distinguished at input and output?

### 9.3 Meaning-Preservation Bandwidth

How far can causality, reservations, responsibility, non-claim boundaries, and context pass without being lost?

### 9.4 Residual-Preservation Bandwidth

Can non-transformable differences be held instead of discarded as errors or noise?

### 9.5 Return-Path Bandwidth

Can objections, corrections, consequences, and responsibility return to the original judgment field?

### 9.6 Commensuration Bandwidth

Can Core and differences be preserved across different grammatical and conceptual systems while a return path is formed back to the original judgment field?

Bandwidth is directionally asymmetric.

Instruction bandwidth from manager to subordinate may be wide while objection bandwidth in the reverse direction is narrow. Requirements may flow abundantly from an institution to the field while the field lacks vocabulary for returning bodily load to the institution.

This asymmetry expresses a power structure, not merely communication volume.

## 10. Cognitive Bandwidth and the Commensuration Window

Even if the communication channel has bandwidth, commensuration does not occur if the recipient cannot preserve the difference.

Cognitive bandwidth is not fixed intelligence.

It changes with fatigue, fear, time pressure, trust, attention, vocabulary, bodily condition, and the quantity of unresolved residuals.

The region in which commensuration can occur is provisionally called the **commensuration window**.

```text
commensuration window
  = sender's expressive bandwidth
  ∩ channel capacity
  ∩ recipient's cognitive bandwidth
  ∩ shared history
  ∩ capacity to preserve residuals
  ∩ possibility of return paths
```

This is not a claim that the conditions have been measured through set operations. It is a display for checking the conditions one by one.

Differences outside the commensuration window move toward cutoff, simplification, misreading, conversion into authority vocabulary, or preservation as residuals.

## 11. Return Expectancy

Return expectancy is not merely the probability that a reply arrives.

This note treats **semantic return expectancy** as the prospect that a released difference will return as an interpretable response and reconnect to the original history or the next judgment.

The following should be distinguished:

- prospect that a response returns;
- prospect that it returns within a specified time;
- prospect that it returns in an interpretable form;
- prospect that it can be reintegrated into the original proposition;
- prospect that it leads to correction or withdrawal;
- prospect that it reaches the responsible subject;
- prospect that the same channel remains reusable.

Even massive reaction volume yields little semantic return if it cannot return to the original question. Where only formulaic responses return and objections never enter history, there are replies but no return path.

Return expectancy is currently a structural diagnostic concept, not an empirical probability.

## 12. Decomposing Communication Success

Communication success should not be judged through one word.

```text
arrival
→ formal parsing
→ proposition identification
→ speech-act understanding
→ historical collation
→ linguistic meaning formation
→ felt resolution
→ acceptance / agreement / refusal
→ action
→ return of consequences
→ re-collation
```

Record how far the sequence succeeded.

Following a command does not by itself mean that communication succeeded. A communication that is understood and refused can be more successful than a misunderstood communication that produces obedience.

## 13. History Changes Topology

After contact, a network does not return to its original state.

One receives an explanation. Is betrayed. Is helped. Is cited. Is ignored. Acquires a return path.

That history changes subsequent distance, bandwidth, and expectation.

- Accumulated trust can create tolerance for collation even when an explanation is incomplete.
- Repeated betrayal can make the same word arrive as intimidation.
- Learning terminology can reduce conceptual distance.
- Institutional exclusion can close return-path bandwidth.
- Literature and commensuration can open a route through different node groups back to the original history.

A semantic network is therefore not a static map. Contact history changes its terrain.

## 14. Communication Gravity Domain

When complex communications repeat, become history, and form beliefs and recognition, particular channels become easier to reuse.

This note provisionally treats this locally stable region as a candidate **communication gravity domain**.

> A communication gravity domain is a barrier-like communication region in which complex communication becomes history, forms belief and recognition, stabilizes internal communication, and at the same time makes inflow from outside and departure from within more difficult.

There is no single gravity source.

- trust;
- fear;
- dependence on resources;
- shared vocabulary;
- institutional sanctions;
- predictability of internal returns;
- narrow external commensuration bandwidth.

Stability does not imply correctness. Fear and dependence can stabilize a gravity domain.

The relations among communication gravity domains, belief gravity domains, and history gravity domains remain unresolved and are not canonicalized here.

## 15. Logical-Depth Distance and Shifts in Computational Optimality

Within the rules of the current language game, the number of steps required to move from proposition A to proposition B is a horizontal distance.

There are cases, however, in which the current node types, edge types, or distance function cannot preserve the residuals.

What is then required is not merely more computation.

- Change the unit of the problem.
- Change the subject.
- Change the time scale.
- Move from a binary opposition to a many-body problem.
- Change the surface on which responsibility is assigned.
- Separate documents that own different concepts.

This change in the operational position of computation is called a **shift in computational optimality**.

When a new computational optimality is established and node types, relations, and closure conditions are reorganized, the result can be read as a logical phase transition.

This does not mean that the process has already been formalized as an optimization problem in computer science.

## 16. Sublation and Closure Correction

Hegelian sublation provides a point of collation for moving contradictory terms into a new determination while preserving rather than simply deleting their difference.

If sublation alone is emphasized, however, there is a danger of reading every residual as material that will ultimately be integrated at a higher level, thereby making complete systemic closure appear possible.

Gödel's incompleteness theorems provide an external point of collation concerning limits of formal systems.

This note does not claim that Gödel's theorems directly prove incompleteness in natural language, society, or Scientific Ontology.

It does, however, receive as **closure correction** the formal warning that a sufficiently expressive local system need not be able to decide everything from within itself or establish its own completeness from within itself.

Residuals return to a system attempting closure with the message: “this is not closed yet.” At times, that return becomes a forcing pressure that makes the system search for another computational optimality.

## 17. Cognitive-Axis-Dependent Snapshots and Irreversibility

> Claim phase: Candidate connection from the public operational layer to the ontological-depth hypothesis layer

To discuss the progression of time, it is necessary to determine what counts as one state or snapshot.

Let the full state be `x_t`, and provisionally express the mapping by which cognitive axis `a` cuts out an observable snapshot as:

```text
s_t^(a) = π_a(x_t)
```

The same sequence of full states yields different snapshot sequences under different cognitive axes.

- In spatial thermodynamics, particle distributions, temperature, pressure, momentum, and similar quantities are placed among state variables.
- In a linguistic phase, words, propositions, grammatical relations, return paths, and residuals are segmented as states.
- In an institutional phase, authority, responsibility, burden, and historical attribution are segmented.
- In an emotional phase, objects, directionality, unclosed differences, and bodily responses are segmented.

Rather than defining entropy simply as “disorder,” this note proposes as a candidate reading the degree to which, under a given cognitive axis and state dimensionality, an earlier snapshot can no longer be reconstructed from the present snapshot.

Where a probabilistic process can be used, asymmetry between a path `γ` and a reverse path `γ^R` can be provisionally written as:

```text
I_a[γ] = log( P_a[γ] / P_a[γ^R] )
```

This expression does not transfer standard thermodynamic entropy production directly into a semantic network. It is a formalization candidate for checking how changing the cognitive axis changes the segmentation of states, probability distributions, and the way irrecoverability is counted.

In the Gate 3 auxiliary experiment, the same nonequilibrium Markov process was observed through several coarse-graining axes. Path asymmetry remained visible on an axis distinguishing all states, whereas axes observing only parity or two coarse regions lost directional information and produced apparent irreversibility of zero.

It therefore cannot yet be said that conceptual space and spatial thermodynamics “count the same entropy in different units.”

The more limited candidate claim is:

> When the same structure of irreversible distributional change is counted over different state spaces, cognitive axes, measures, and transition rules, the observed irreversibility differs.

The arrow of time can be read as the direction in which the next state is locked and reconstructability of previous states is lost.

Logical depth is not an axis that returns to a past physical state. It is an axis for re-collating previous segmentations, judgments, premises, and cutoffs and reconstructing return paths at another resolution.

## 18. Depth Interface to Entropy, Time, and Negentropy

> Claim phase: Ontological-depth hypothesis layer. This is not a replacement for standard thermodynamics, information theory, or cosmology.

Provisionally, the following correspondences are examined:

```text
structural surface
  distribution / separation / locking of the next state
  → entropy-like reading

functional surface
  before-and-after and sequential progression through distribution locking
  → temporal reading

depth surface
  residual preservation / reconnection / increased resolution / structural revision
  → negentropy-like reading
```

Time locks the next state and advances before and after. The Logical-Depth Axis opens the possibility of returning to a locked distribution, preserving residuals, and rearranging them into another configuration.

Negentropy does not reverse time.

What returns is not the past state itself but collatability with the past, severed causal relations, overlooked differences, lost return paths, and the ability to update judgment.

A history irreversible on the time axis can therefore acquire greater resolution and reconstructed return paths on the Logical-Depth Axis.

The fact that unsustainability generates corrective pressure is not the same as actual recovery of a local system. Some systems may fail to undergo a phase transition and collapse.

## 19. God and Creation as Network

> Claim phase: AMP / theological rendering layer. This is a private lineage for reading depth hypotheses in compressed form, not grounding for the public operational layer.

In this research line, the concentration of responsibility in the Network document is not treated as mere editorial overcrowding.

Here, God is not a personal agent that manages the world from outside, but the total structure within which existences can enter relations with one another.

Creation is not the addition of elements to an already completed world.

Null / Void perforates dense and undifferentiated relational possibility and interrupts continuity. Interruption produces segmentation and differentiation, through which nodes, distance, locality, distribution, and recognition arise.

```text
dense relational possibility
  ↓ perforation / interruption by Null / Void
segmentation
  ↓

  ↓
```

Within this rendering, time is the functional surface on which created distributions become irreversibly fixed as history, while entropy is read as the structural surface on which those distributions lose the ability to return to their previous snapshots.

This lineage connects to AMP Core, but it is not a claim of standard physics, standard cosmology, or theology in general. ## 20. Society as a Set of Many-Body Problems

Society is treated neither as the simple sum of individual intentions nor as one giant subject.

Individuals, groups, organizations, institutions, languages, media, AI, law, and money interact as a set of many-body problems, each with different bandwidths and histories.

The direction of a society can be diagnosed through questions such as:

- Where is communication concentrated?
- Where does compression loss occur?
- Which return paths are narrow?
- Which gateways are captured?
- Which histories have become unsearchable?
- Whose cognitive bandwidth is being physically depleted?
- Which gravity domains obstruct exit?

For example, in an organization where history-search bandwidth is narrow, transmission bandwidth for self-presentation is wide, and return paths from the field to upper levels are narrow, self-presentation is more likely to be mistransformed into ability.

This reading is not intended to erase individual responsibility. It is intended to examine which network conditions make many locally rational movements collectively destructive.

## 22. Failure Modes

0. **Metabolic-center adjudicatorization**: treating a central collational role as ownership of definitions or final judgment over other documents.

1. **Single-graph reduction**: flattening heterogeneous nodes, relations, and time into one graph.
2. **Single-number distance**: mixing lexical, historical, bodily, and power distances into one scalar.
3. **Physicalization of bandwidth**: directly equating meaning-preservation or return-path bandwidth with communication speed.
4. **Reply = return path**: treating a formulaic response as re-collatability.
5. **Stability = truth**: treating persistence of a communication gravity domain as correctness.
6. **Universalization of sublation**: consuming residuals as material for higher-order integration.
7. **Gödel generalization**: directly applying a formal theorem to natural language or society.
8. **Unmarked integration by the Network document**: absorbing concept ownership from the other eight documents without marking the three descriptive phases.
9. **Factification of depth hypotheses**: presenting entropy, time, or negentropy as empirically established mechanisms.
10. **Claim-phase conflation**: reading the public operational layer, ontological-depth hypothesis layer, and AMP/theological rendering layer with the same evidential force.
11. **Axis forgetting**: erasing the cognitive axis and state dimensionality under which irreversibility was counted.
