## 0. Position

Literature can be treated as a public site of worldmaking, as a device for temporarily inhabiting another world, or as a form of structural reading that contacts narrative truth.

This note treats none of these as the sole definition of literature.

The question addressed here is:

> When a reader contacts a work that compresses long periods, multiple events, bodies, judgments, failures, and prayers, how is that history redeployed on the reader's side, how does the difference from the reader's own history arise as qualia, and how does that contact alter subsequent conditions of recognition?

## 1. Central Thesis

A literary work does not preserve experience unchanged.

Experience is selected, compressed, and arranged through author, narrator, characters, language, form, and world conditions. The reader does not restore it as the original experience; the reader redeploys it using their own history, body, grammar, and understanding of the world.

> In one phase of literary contact, another's history compressed into a work is redeployed within the reader's history-field, and the difference between movement within the work and movement possible for the reader arises as an event with qualitative texture.

This note calls this operational surface **compressed-experience redeployment**.

## 2. How Is Experience Compressed?

A work does not contain the totality of events.

- Years of time may be folded into one scene.
- One person's conflict may be placed into a short line or a silence.
- Multiple social conditions may be gathered into one room, workplace, or family.
- Bodily sensation may be transformed into metaphor, rhythm, repetition, or line break.
- History that cannot be explained may remain as omission, foreshadowing, or restricted viewpoint.
- Conditions governing a world may be tested as narrative events.

Compression is not summary. It reduces the number of events while preserving a structure that another subject can redeploy.

This note does not define good compression through one metric. If the reader can still reconstruct differences, causality, injury, return paths, and residuals after compression, however, part of the experiential structure can be read as preserved.

## 3. The Reader Does Not Replicate Experience

The reader does not undergo the same experience as the author or characters.

Even when reading the same sentence, the world that arises differs with the reader's age, body, losses, occupation, worldview, and reading history.

The work is an input. It does not inject completed meaning into the reader.

The reader temporarily places themselves along a path presented by the work.

Where would I stop?
What would I fear?
What would I protect?
Why was this person able to go this far?
What can and cannot be seen in this world?

Through this self-placement, a difference arises between movement within the work and the reader's own history.

## 4. Qualia-Difference Contact

This note does not define qualia in general.

Here, **qualia-difference contact** refers to the condition in which redeployment of compressed experience makes the difference between the historical movement in the work and the reader's own history or possible action appear with bodily, emotional, and semantic texture.

“That is incredible.”
Tears come.
The body becomes hot.
It is frightening.
It is beautiful.
It is unforgivable.
One feels that some part of oneself can still move in that direction.

These need not be additions to propositional understanding. They can be one form of meaning experience arising from contact between the compressed history preserved by the work and the reader's own history.

Strong emotion alone, however, does not guarantee understanding of the work or contact with truth.

## 5. History Participates in the Formation of the Qualia Differences Addressed Here

The claim here is limited to the phase in which compressed experience forms a difference within the reader's history-field through literary contact. It does not impose history as a universal necessary condition for qualia in general.

A passage that once produced little response can become intensely present years later.

The reader has accumulated experience. They have read another work. Their body has been injured. They have lost someone. They have worked within an institution. That history opens a port that was previously closed.

Qualia are not simple stimulus-response events.

> Recognition bandwidth, meaning loops, and bodily history formed in the past are rearranged through contact with a work, changing the depth of texture that can be received.

Qualia once experienced also become history.

A history of encountering remarkable thought or action and trembling at the difference changes the conditions under which later works or real actions are received. Finer differences may become perceptible, and longer movements may become possible to hold.

In this sense, sensitivity is not a fixed capacity. It is formed through histories of qualia contact and collation.

## 6. Connection to Worldmaking

There is an existing reading of literature as a public site of worldmaking.

A work sets not only characters and settings but also what can count as an event, whose voice can be heard, what loss produces grief, how far responsibility returns, and what remains unresolved.

Compressed-experience redeployment in this note is one phase in which the reader contacts that worldmaking.

The reader temporarily inhabits the work's world and experiences its gravity, time, causality, and returns of meaning. Afterward, the reader may return to the actual world and build a bridge across the difference between the two worlds.

This note does not own worldmaking as a general definition of literature, nor does it claim that the world of the work and the actual world are directly identical.

## 7. Literary Form Is a Mode of Compression

Literary compression resides not only in content but also in grammar and form.

- Whose viewpoint is fixed?
- What is omitted?
- Which event is shown first?
- Which history is thickened by repetition?
- Which distant nodes are connected by metaphor?
- Which bodily time is produced by rhythm?
- Does silence close a return path or protect it?

A summary may fail to reach the qualia formed by a work because not only propositional content but also structures of time, distance, expectation, and silence have been compressed into the form.

## 8. Reading Is Not Unlimited

The reader's history contributes to meaning generation, but this does not make every reading equally available.

A work resists through its words, construction, viewpoint, repetition, and world conditions.

If the reader's associations have no return path to the work and the work is used only as support for a pre-existing thesis, the result can become false extension.

Conversely, if authorial intention is treated as the sole correct reading, residuals retained by the work and consequences the author themselves did not close can be erased.

Reading arises between the resistance of the work and the history of the reader.

## 9. Sensitivity and Port Management

As sensitivity increases, not only remarkable things but also contempt, disguised goodwill, careless language, and concealed injury can enter more strongly.

Lowering sensitivity can reduce injury but may also reduce the capacity to contact meaning deeply.

What is therefore required is not simple amplification of sensitivity.

- Select input routes.
- Select duration of exposure.
- Select depth of collation.
- Select where to cut off and where to return.

This does not reduce literary reading to a safety technique. It is cognitive port management for sustaining high-sensitivity contact with meaning.

## 10. Movement of Self-Definition

When the self is defined through external evaluation, titles, superiority, or self-presentation, it is destabilized whenever the evaluative apparatus changes.

Literary contact can open another form of self-definition.

> A self capable of feeling something remarkable when encountering remarkable action or thought.
> A self capable of placing itself within another's history and holding the difference.
> A self that has not lost the part that responds deeply to meaning even while inside anger or ressentiment.

This is not proof of moral purity.

It is the felt sense that the capacity to contact meaning has not yet died.

## 11. Collation with External Research

### 11.1 Husserl

The discussions of retention, primal impression, protention, and horizon provide a point of collation for reading as temporal experience rather than reception of isolated sentences, involving retention of what has just passed and anticipation of what follows.

### 11.2 Iser

Iser's account of indeterminacy in the work and concretization by the reader, in which the reader constructs a world while following textual directions, contacts redeployment and temporary inhabitation.

### 11.3 Eco

The Model Reader, encyclopedic competence, and limits of interpretation provide a point of collation for acknowledging reader history without falling into unlimited reading.

### 11.4 Derrida

Iterability and contextual severance provide a point of collation for considering how identity and transformation arise simultaneously when a work leaves its author and initial context and contacts another history.

These sources are not grounds for literary experience. They are used to identify which phase an existing concept illuminates and where differences remain.

## 12. Failure Modes

1. Defining literature exclusively as compressed experience.
2. Treating strong emotion as proof of understanding or truth contact.
3. Identifying author, narrator, character, and fictional world.
4. Treating the reader's self-placement as replication of the author's experience.
5. Using literature as empirical evidence for a philosophical or social theory.
6. Converting reader freedom into unlimited extension.
7. Valuing works by the magnitude of pain they produce.
8. Making continual openness of sensitivity an ethical obligation.

## 14. Definitional Residues

- The vocabulary of compression and redeployment handles literary time and form but does not sufficiently express creative impulse, play, musicality, or communal practice.
- Qualia difference can over-locate reading within the individual and background shared interpretation, criticism, and institutions.
- Emphasizing history risks retrospectively explaining accidental first-contact shocks or inexplicable beauty through causal narratives.
- Increasing sensitivity does not imply greater value in a work or superiority in a reader.

## 15. Non-Claim Boundary

- Literature in general is not uniquely defined as compressed experience.
- Strong qualia produced by a work are not treated as proof of empirical truth.
- The author's life, characters, and narrator are not identified.
- The reader is not claimed to reproduce the author's experience or emotion.
- Literature is not claimed to automatically produce therapy, education, or political change.
- Literary settings are not used as physical or cosmological evidence for Scientific Ontology.

## 16. Unresolved Questions / Depth Interface

- How should the preservation rate of compressed experience be evaluated?
- What is the relation between qualia contact and meaning-loop formation?
- Can changes in recognition bandwidth through repeated reading be described?
- Where is the boundary between resistance of the work and reader extension?
- How does this compare with film, music, ritual, and games?
- How can increased sensitivity and risk of injury be treated without closing the problem into individual responsibility?
