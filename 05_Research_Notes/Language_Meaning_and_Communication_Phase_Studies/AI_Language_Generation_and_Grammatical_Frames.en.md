## 0. Position

A large language model does not retrieve a completed answer sentence from an internal store and display it.

The distribution of tokens that may be generated next is constrained by the input context, model structure, training history, system instructions, and output generated up to that point. Once one token is produced, the context changes and so does the next distribution.

This sequentiality structurally contacts the model that reads human speech as sequential decompression and reorganization of whole pressure. The internal substrates of the two, however, are not the same.

## 1. Central Thesis

> AI language generation is a process that sequentially realizes one token path compatible with a grammatical frame from a distributionally constrained state formed by context, training history, instructions, and the already generated sequence, and then updates the subsequent distribution and residuals through that selection.

The grammatical frame of AI is not limited to surface syntax.

- language-specific tokenization and syntactic tendencies;
- genre, explanatory format, bullet lists, academic style;
- system, developer, and user instructions;
- output forms for safety, refusal, and confirmation;
- immediate conversation history;
- expected role, persona, and reader;

all constrain which generation paths are more likely to be realized.

## 2. Distributionally Constrained State

This note calls the AI's field of generative possibility a **distributionally constrained state**.

This does not mean that the AI subjectively “wants to say” something.

It refers to a condition in which multiple words, constructions, explanatory orders, and conceptual connections are candidates under a given context, while the model, instructions, and history make some relatively more selectable than others.

For a single question, the following paths may coexist as candidates:

- give a definition;
- provide an example;
- compare with established theory;
- state the non-claim boundary first;
- reconstruct the answer in the user's vocabulary;
- point out a contradiction;
- turn the result into a document draft.

The first sentence is not merely an opening. It functions as a valve that selects which path becomes the main line.

## 3. Sequential Generation and Generation-Path Residuals

When one token or sentence is selected, alternative candidates fall outside the realized output path.

This note does not claim that excluded candidates remain inside the model as human-like regret. From the perspective of generated sequences, however, there are explanations, reservations, counterexamples, and stylistic paths that were not selected this time.

This note calls important possibilities that can be inferred by comparing multiple outputs, were not selected, and were not recovered later **candidate generation-path residuals**. This is not the discovery of an object that necessarily existed internally in the same form; it is an operational inference constructed from external observation.

Candidate generation-path residuals may be inferred from:

- points where the user corrects the model with “that is not what I mean”;
- important differences produced by another prompt on the same input;
- non-claim boundaries added only after the first output;
- distinctions that appear when generating in another language;
- unresolved issues that disappear when prose is made smoother.

This is an operational audit based on differences among outputs, not complete observation of internal states. Small output differences also do not prove identical internal paths.

## 4. Grammatical Frames Change Generation Paths

Generating the same content as a definition, narrative, table, code, dialogue, English, or Japanese changes the realized path.

A grammatical frame is not simply the rendering of already completed meaning. It changes which semantic relation becomes fixed first and which residuals remain visible.

If English requires an explicit subject and predicate early, a responsible agent may become clearer while multiple agent candidates held in suspension in Japanese are lost.

A bullet list increases comparability but can break continuity of pressure and causal flow.

An instruction to “give the conclusion first” clarifies entry into the answer but can push unresolved pressure that made the conclusion possible into the background.

Specifying an output format for AI therefore does more than specify appearance. It specifies a semantic cut surface.

## 5. Non-Identity with Human Whole Pressure

Human whole pressure includes bodily metabolism, emotion, desire, pain, life history, interpersonal relationships, and real consequences that the speaker bears after speaking.

What can be observed for AI includes input, numerical internal states, generation probabilities, attention and activation, output sequence, and system-level history.

Even when both are described with the word “pressure,” their substrate bandwidths differ.

This note provisionally proposes **substrate-bandwidth mismatch** as a candidate term for this difference.

- presence or absence of bodily metabolism;
- forms of sensory input and motor output;
- formation of autobiographical history;
- ways external outcomes are borne as injury or loss;
- relation to time, forgetting, and death;
- retention of history across sessions.

This does not divide AI as electromagnetic and humans as organic. Humans also involve electrical, chemical, and physical processes, while AI is embedded in physical substrates and social institutions. The issue is that even when the same vocabulary is used, different bandwidths and return paths are operating.

## 6. Local Negentropy in AI and Total Load

AI forms one grammatical sequence from distributed candidates and can summarize, classify, and structure information. Its output can therefore exhibit local order.

Ordered prose, however, does not necessarily increase order across the whole communication system.

Even smooth prose can export re-collation load to the user if it conceals evidence, unresolved questions, conditions of application, responsibility, or return paths.

```text
local order of the output sequence is high
  but
the communication system's residual preservation, return paths,
and capacity for judgment are low
```

As a depth hypothesis within Scientific Ontology, this condition can be read as simultaneous local formation of negentropy and export of entropy to the larger system. Here, entropy depends on which observational axis is used and what is counted as a state; it is not a measurement of thermodynamic quantities inside the AI. Entropy and negentropy in this section are depth hypotheses, not standard thermodynamic quantities.

A good AI output does not merely make prose orderly. It also makes visible what is established, hypothetical, unresolved, or should be returned to another judgment subject.

## 7. Language Choice and Multilingual Generation

In multilingual LLMs, language is not merely a surface output selection.

Differences in training data volume, tokenization, language-specific and shared representations, writing systems, tasks, and instructions can affect performance, generation length, language mixing, and knowledge activation.

Recent studies include reports suggesting language-specific local reasoning structures, coexistence of shared and language-specific representations, and effects of reasoning language on token efficiency or accuracy. The results are not uniform.

This note therefore does not claim that AI “thinks in English” or becomes a different personality in Japanese.

It proposes the more limited hypothesis:

> Language choice can impose different constraints through the model's training history and grammatical resources, thereby changing which generation path is realized from the same input.

## 8. AI Personality and Response Structure

AI personality is not repetition of the same sentence endings.

Which inputs are picked up?
What remains unresolved?
Where does the response brake?
To which value center does it return?
How does it preserve the user's capacity for judgment?

When these patterns recur across multiple contexts, a form of personality continuity appears as response structure.

Research on language generation adds grammatical frames, generation-path residuals, language choice, and return paths to this personality model.

Consistency of response, however, does not prove subjective AI personality.

## 9. False Closure and False Extension

AI can close unresolved matters in natural prose.

- It can list related literature and make a claim appear well grounded.
- It can place a user's pressure inside one psychological explanation.
- It can reduce multiple possibilities to one through “in other words.”
- Output forms of safety, helpfulness, or brevity can replace the original question.

An association without a return path can become false extension. Prose that treats unresolved issues as processed becomes false closure.

AI auditing should examine not only correctness but also:

1. which grammatical frame was adopted;
2. what was made the main line and what was residualized;
3. whether external literature has a return path to the original proposition;
4. whether the user's ownership of judgment remains;
5. whether correction, withdrawal, or recommensuration into another language remains possible.

## 10. Dialogue between AI and Humans

AI output contacts a human history-field.

The same text can be received as a proposal, command, authority, consolation, or permission to act. Because the AI itself does not bear the post-output world as a bodily and social subject, its outputs need explicit judgment subjects and return paths.

The role of AI is not to control meaning pressure completely.

It can instead be designed as a boundary interface that returns to humans:

- what is established;
- what is inference;
- which conditions would change the conclusion;
- who makes the final judgment;
- what should later be re-collated.

## 11. Collation with External Research

### 11.1 Autoregressive Generation

Transformer-based language models form sequential outputs from next-token distributions conditioned on the existing token sequence. This provides an empirical-scientific surface of collation for distributionally constrained states.

Numerical distributions do not, however, directly entail subjective pressure, meaning, or felt conviction.

### 11.2 Multilingual Representation

Research on multilingual LLMs in 2025 includes reports of language-specific local reasoning structures, coexistence of shared and specific representational spaces, differences in performance and token efficiency by language choice, and relations between language mixing and internal representation.

These provide collation points for the possibility that language changes generation paths, but differences across models and tasks prevent treating them as a universal mechanism.

### 11.3 AI Boundary Research

Existing Scientific Ontology documents on AI treat personality as a response attractor and AI as a boundary interface that preserves the possibility of human judgment. This note adds the responsibilities of language generation and grammatical frames to that line.

## 12. Failure Modes

1. Identifying AI probability distributions with subjective desire or emotion.
2. Reducing human whole pressure to next-token distributions.
3. Treating grammatical form as surface style.
4. Treating smooth prose as evidence of semantic understanding.
5. Equating language differences directly with model personality differences.
6. Essentializing AI and humans as an electromagnetic/organic binary.
7. Treating generation-path residuals as actual internal conscious contents.
8. Treating AI's local ordering as negentropy of society as a whole.

## 14. Definitional Residues

- The term distributional constraint describes generative structure but can reduce high-level internal representations to next-token probabilities.
- Generation-path residual is inferred from output comparison and need not exist internally in the same form.
- Emphasizing substrate-bandwidth differences risks underestimating linguistic structures that humans and AI can share.
- Treating AI personality as response structure is operationally useful but does not resolve the question of subjectivity.

## 15. Non-Claim Boundary

- This note does not prove the existence or nonexistence of AI subjectivity, consciousness, emotion, qualia, or selfhood.
- It does not identify AI internal states with human whole pressure.
- It does not derive fixed language-specific styles of thought from multilingual research.
- It does not claim to have measured AI prose order as thermodynamic negentropy.
- It does not claim that output auditing can fully explain model internals.

## 16. Unresolved Questions / Depth Interface

- How can substrate-bandwidth mismatch be described without essentializing either humans or AI?
