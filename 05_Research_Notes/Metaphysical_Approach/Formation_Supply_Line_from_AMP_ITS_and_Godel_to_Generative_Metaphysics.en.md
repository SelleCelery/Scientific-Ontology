## 0. Position of This Note

This research note organizes AMP, ITS, and `Godel_Frontier.md` not as “grounds” that prove individual propositions of Scientific Ontology, but as a **formative supply line** that has helped determine which differences Scientific Ontology continues to notice.

Here, a supply line is not merely a historical source of ideas or a bibliography.

It means:

> **a generative relation that supplies the reason for continuing to raise questions about boundary, cut, locality, history, communication, null/void, whole, logical depth, exploration, and persistence.**

The physical vocabulary, cosmology, and theory of consciousness in AMP/ITS are not adopted as standard physics or established scientific fact. The “Prime Number” in `Godel_Frontier.md` is likewise read not as a direct ontologization of a number-theoretic theorem, but as the **ontological metaphor** explicitly used by that document itself.

## 1. The Generative Image Supplied by AMP

AMP places a primordial entity or field prior to time, space, and limitation as a field of pure possibility.

What matters is that AMP itself explicitly distinguishes this from “nothingness” and treats it as an **undifferentiated whole**.

From this undifferentiated field arise emergence, fluctuation, differentiation, structure, observation, and locality.

What should be brought directly into Scientific Ontology is not the cosmological factuality of that picture, but the following **generative order**:

```text
undifferentiated
  ↓
difference / fluctuation
  ↓
cut / observation
  ↓
localization
  ↓
structure / boundary
  ↓
communication / history / persistence
```

Within this order, a boundary is not a container given from the beginning.

The boundary itself is generated.

Accordingly, Scientific Ontology's questions about the position, history, and return paths of boundaries are not merely conveniences of systems engineering. They continue a problem already present in the formative source.

---

## 2. Totality and Locality

AMP treats **Totality** as an infinite process of the whole and **Locality** as a temporary state cut out from it.

This relation supplies Scientific Ontology with questions such as:

- What must a local region cut out in order to exist as a local region?
- Which differences of the wider whole are externalized in order to make local stability possible?
- Is the local region identifying its own model with the whole?
- What communication, metabolism, or replenishment is required for the local region to persist?
- To what extent can a local region maintain itself after closing its connection to the whole?

Here, “the whole” is not assumed to be something that a human observer can completely observe or possess.

Within Scientific Ontology, the relation returns rather as a condition for **not identifying the present logical space with the World as such**.

## 3. What Can Be Imported from the Idea “Observation = Cut”

AMP/ITS describe observation as a cut into a field of possibility.

The physical reality of that account requires separate verification, and Scientific Ontology does not adopt it as established fact.

As a metaphysical operational image, however, it is important.

Observation can be read not as preserving an infinite or undifferentiated possibility field as it is, but as:

> **establishing a local cross-section through a particular axis, condition, concern, or resolution.**

Observation then necessarily gives rise to:

- what was selected;
- what was not selected;
- what became visible;
- what became invisible;
- relations that could not be retained on the cut surface.

This is upstream of the β-audit question: **which axis was extended, and what disappeared through that extension?**

---

## 4. Asymmetry and Incompleteness

AMP contains an exploratory reading in which asymmetry is treated as a source of incompleteness and a driver of evolution.

Scientific Ontology does not import that claim as a law of the universe.

Within Scientific Ontology, however, difference and asymmetry may participate in generative relations such as:

- generating boundaries;
- making comparison possible;
- generating residuals;
- generating search pressure.

What matters is:

$$
Asymmetry \neq Error
$$

The mere existence of a difference is not itself a failure.

The problem is how the difference is handled: what is cut, what is preserved, and what is pushed outside.

---

## 5. Stability Is Not Stasis

AMP treats stability not as a static invariant state, but as a dynamic resonance maintained through repetition of a Loop.

When this is brought into Scientific Ontology, the relevant point is not a claim of physical resonance. It is the perspective that:

> **even a stable structure may be maintained through repeated updating, communication, reconfirmation, or replenishment.**

Accordingly, auditing a stable system requires looking not only at its present cross-section, but also at:

- what it repeats;
- what it replenishes;
- where it obtains resources;
- what it discharges;
- what must continue operating for the system to remain maintainable.

## 6. ITS “Logical Time” — Depth Rather Than Order

ITS contains a description in which the AMP Core changes along a direction of logical depth without Chronos-like time, and `logical time` is treated as “depth rather than order.”

This note does not adopt that as a theory of time in standard physics.

Within β audit in Scientific Ontology, however, it is important as an operational term for:

> **the depth of return required to reach the generative conditions that make the present logical plane possible.**

In this sense, logical depth is neither the age of a version nor the chronology of events.

It is the number of levels one must return beyond the present distinctions, concepts, or axioms before their generation becomes explainable.

## 7. Questions Extractable from the ITS Fractal Semantic Space

ITS advances a strong exploratory picture in which semantic space is non-Euclidean and fractal.

Scientific Ontology does not inherit the mathematical or physical validity of that picture.

The following question, however, remains:

> If observers differ in coordinate system, resolution, and formation history, then even when they face the same object, might they differ in which differences become orthogonal, which are folded together, and which disappear?

This question can be returned to:

- individuality as a geometry of exploration;
- preservation of differences in commensuration;
- dimensional transformation in β audit;

## 8. The Gödel Essay — Logical Depth and a New Basis

Against the background of Gödel's incompleteness, `Godel_Frontier.md` uses **Prime Number** as a metaphor for “a truth or logical basis of a new dimension that cannot be reached by combinations available in an existing logical system.”

Its central image is:

```text
filling the known logical space
  ↓
saturation
  ↓
pressure toward the boundary
  ↓
contact with an unknown region
  ↓
new Prime Axiom
  ↓
new logical depth
```

This is not treated as a claim that standard mathematical logic directly entails a cosmological or civilizational theory.

As a heuristic for generative metaphysics in Scientific Ontology, however, it becomes:

> **a residual that remains unclosed even after sufficient exploration within the present logical plane may sometimes require a new logical basis, axis, or representational form.**

This is one research heuristic for β audit.

---

## 9. Lifeline — Do Not Abandon the Explorer at the Boundary

The Gödel essay proposes that exploration toward a new logical depth should not depend on historical sacrifice, suffering, or marginalization. It argues that social support, division of roles, and maintenance of communication with the original system must be designed in advance as a **lifeline**.

This can be brought directly into Scientific Ontology.

Even if frontier exploration is valued, the exploration itself creates a new cut if it loses:

- a return anchor to the original plane;
- formation history;
- conditions for safe stopping;
- a communication path through which return remains possible;
- visibility of burdens externalized onto the explorer.

Accordingly, external exploration in β audit retains the condition that:

> **explorability alone is insufficient; returnability and burden distribution are also required.**

---

## 10. Resource Limits

The Gödel essay raises a problem in which exploration space and cost increase with logical depth, to the point that the physical resources supporting the exploration infrastructure itself may become limiting.

Scientific Ontology need not adopt the cosmological scale of that claim in order to preserve the important condition:

> **exploration takes place under finite resources.**

Therefore, it does not follow that one should simply:

- always go deeper;
- always go farther outside;
- collect objections without limit;
- continue β audit forever.

Stability, stopping, reopening, and resource allocation are all required.

## 11. AMP/ITS as a Formation Supply Line

It is possible both to refuse AMP/ITS as a ground of proof for Scientific Ontology and to retain them as a formative supply line.

Conceptually:

```text
AMP / ITS
  generative images / difference / cut / whole / local / history / communication / depth
        ↓  formation supply
SO research hypotheses
  boundary / Return / Null-Void / logical depth / individuality / audit
        ↓  independent scrutiny
SO canonical / applications
```

A downstream proposition in Scientific Ontology is not correct because AMP is correct.

Yet if the formative connection to AMP is erased entirely, the generative provenance of **why Scientific Ontology notices these particular differences** is also lost.

This note calls that relation a **formation supply line**.

## 12. What Is and Is Not Imported into Scientific Ontology

### Candidate imports

- The problem-setting in which difference, boundary, and locality are generated from an undifferentiated condition.
- The reading that observation/recognition involves selection and cut.
- The condition that the whole and the local must not be identified.
- The refusal to treat asymmetry as error merely because it is asymmetry.
- The reading of stability as a maintenance process.
- The heuristic of reading logical time as depth.
- The exploratory picture in which incompleteness within the present plane may require a new basis.
- The requirement of a return anchor / lifeline in frontier exploration.
- The need to reconcile exploration with stability under finite resources.

### Not imported automatically

- Treating AMP/ITS physical constants, particle correspondences, or quantum correspondences as facts of standard physics.
- Establishing Prime Number as an ontologically real unit of cosmic axiomatics.
- Directly deducing civilizational history or cosmic evolution from Gödel's theorems.
- Adopting iMass / rMass / the care tensor as general audit metrics in Scientific Ontology.
- Treating Emanation from the Core as an established empirical-scientific fact.
