# 0. Aim

Modern science describes change with high precision through observable targets, measurements, reproducibility, mathematical relations, mechanisms, statistical dependence, conservation laws, symmetry, and interaction.

Scientific Ontology does not deny those achievements.

The added question lies one step upstream or outside the usual descriptive frame:

> **Why does a relation not end as a one-off accident, but persist as a repeatable structure?**

> **Why do differences not merely dissipate, but remain as boundaries, histories, and structures?**

> **Why does a structure that realizes some function arise, and why can that structure generate new functions and new differences?**

> **Why are there relations stable enough to be describable as laws?**

The proposal is not a rejection of modern causal explanation, but an **extension of its explanatory range**.

A first compression is:

```text
typical scientific question
  given a state, by what mechanism or law is the next state produced?

additional AMP question
  why did that mechanism, law, or structure itself persist as a repeatable relation?
```

Thus this note does not replace

$$
Law + State_t \rightarrow State_{t+1},
$$

but adds upstream a generative reading:

$$
Difference
\rightarrow
Relation
\rightarrow
Stabilization
\rightarrow
Repeatability
\rightarrow
LawLikeForm.
$$

The latter is not presently a physical law. It is an AMP research hypothesis.

# Part I. What is being extended in causality?

## 1. Do not oversimplify modern science

It would be inaccurate to say that modern science deals only with efficient cause. It contains mechanistic explanation, statistical causal inference, structural causal models, functional and evolutionary explanation, cybernetic explanation, conservation-law explanation, symmetry-based explanation, and more.

This note therefore does not criticize science by compressing it into one crude causal doctrine.

A particularly powerful operational form nevertheless remains:

> **Given a current state and rules, describe which changes occur under which conditions.**

Conceptually:

$$
X_{t+1}=T(X_t;\theta),
$$

where $X_t$ is current state, $T$ is transition rule/mechanism/dynamics, and $\theta$ contains parameters, boundary conditions, constants, or related constraints.

AMP returns the stability of $T$ and $\theta$ themselves to the problem surface:

> Why is $T$ repeatable?

> Why does $\theta$ appear fixed within some range?

> Why does the state space itself permit repeated cutting-out of the same kinds of entities?

That is where the proposed extension begins.

---

## 2. Separate descriptive and generative causality

### 2.1 Descriptive causality

Given already formed objects, laws, state spaces, and measurement rules, descriptive causality asks how $A$ gives rise to $B$:

$$
A \xrightarrow{T} B.
$$

### 2.2 Generative causality

AMP additionally asks why $A$, $B$, and $T$ became repeatable distinctions or structures at all:

$$
\{variation,difference,history\}
\rightarrow
\{stable\ relation,boundary,Lock\}
\rightarrow
\{repeatable\ form\}.
$$

Generative causality is not declared superior to descriptive causality. They address different targets. Descriptive causality works with a formed world at high precision; generative causality returns "already formed" to the question.

$$
GenerativeCausality \neq Replacement(ScientificCausality).
$$

---

## 3. Approximation to the Galileo / Aristotle contrast

This note does not make the historical claim that Galileo simply accepted efficient cause and rejected Aristotle wholesale.

As a research contrast, however, it is useful to note that modern science achieved enormous success by concentrating on measurement, mathematization, and reproducible change while questions analogous to formal and final cause receded from direct physical explanation.

AMP does not reverse this by returning to pre-modern physics. Its position is:

> **Retain the facts, mathematics, and measurement constraints won by modern science while reopening the question of why those stable forms became possible.**

The question is Aristotelian in direction, but the proposed answer is closer to modern generative, persistence, and selection systems.

# Part II. Repositioning the four causes on an AMP surface

## 4. Do not simply revive the four causes

Material, efficient, formal, and final causes are used here as a commensuration surface, not as an authoritative interpretation of Aristotle.

| Cause | Provisional AMP rendering | Central question |
|---|---|---|
| material | available carriers, resources, fields, present states | from what can it be constituted? |
| efficient | contact, transition, transformation, action | what produced the change? |
| formal | structures, boundaries, or Lock forms that preserve difference | what form can persist? |
| final | persistence / possibility of continuation | what conditions must be met for a next state to remain possible? |

The most important move concerns final cause.

---

## 5. Final cause is persistence

The central hypothesis is:

$$
FinalCause_{AMP}=PersistenceCondition.
$$

This does not mean that a future goal-agent pulls the present toward itself, nor that the universe is assigned intention, desire, or plan.

It means:

> **A structure incapable of continuing into a later state cannot continue its history in that form. Persistence conditions therefore act as constraints that differentially retain some continuations.**

Final cause is read not as

$$
Goal\ from\ Future,
$$

but as

$$
Constraint\ on\ Continuation.
$$

The "purpose" is not a pre-existing answer. It is the constraint that only structures capable of continuation can form continuing histories.

---

## 6. Persistence is not goodness

Calling persistence a final cause risks treating persistence as morally good. This note rejects that move:

$$
Persistence \neq Goodness.
$$

Parasitic structures, violent institutions, and systems sustained by externalization can all persist.

Persistence is first a generative descriptive variable. Audit theory must then ask:

> Whose persistence is constituted by whose non-persistence?

Final cause as persistence therefore does not provide ethical justification.

# Part III. Two AMP hypotheses

## 7. Root is one

As a human-side AMP research hypothesis:

$$
H_1:\quad Root=One.
$$

"One" does not mean one particle, one material, or one spacetime point. It means that before distinctions such as subject/object, mind/nature, object/space, primordial activity is not pre-split into multiple independent substances.

The public AMP notion of primordial entity is compatible with this direction, but the explicit axiom-like form `Root=One` is a research-level formulation of this note and is not retroactively declared public AMP canon.

---

## 8. Symmetry persists

A second human-side hypothesis is:

$$
H_2:\quad Symmetry\ persists.
$$

"Symmetry" cannot simply mean spatial uniformity, equal temperature, or bilateral visual symmetry. What the argument more minimally requires is closer to:

> **Some relation remains invariant through change.**

A weaker intermediate proposition is therefore:

$$
Persistence \Rightarrow Invariance_{some\ relation}.
$$

Persistence does not require everything to remain identical. Something is retained as identity or relation while other aspects change.

Symmetry is one major candidate formalism for describing such invariance:

$$
Persistence
\Rightarrow
Invariance
\dashrightarrow
Symmetry,
$$

where the last relation remains a research question.

---

## 9. Symmetry breaking is not negation of symmetry

Local structure requires difference:

$$
\Delta \neq 0.
$$

If "symmetry" is restricted to complete absence of difference, it becomes incompatible with world formation.

This note therefore permits:

```text
higher-order invariance
  + local symmetry breaking
  + Lock that preserves the break
  -> persistent structure
```

or conceptually:

$$
GlobalInvariance + LocalAsymmetry + Lock
\rightarrow PersistentStructure.
$$

Local asymmetry may therefore be retained under broader invariance rather than interpreted simply as failure of symmetry.

# Part IV. Asymmetry has conditions of persistence

## 10. Difference is not automatically existence

A difference that occurs once does not necessarily persist as an existence.

Even where

$$
\Delta_t \neq 0,
$$

if

$$
\Delta_{t+1}=0,
$$

it is transient.

AMP is interested in differences that acquire history:

$$
\Delta_t \rightarrow \Delta_{t+1} \rightarrow \cdots \rightarrow \Delta_{t+n}.
$$

The same number or shape need not be preserved. It is enough that the difference remain traceable through transformation as relation, boundary, or history.

---

## 11. Persistent asymmetry requires a Lock

A central hypothesis is:

$$
Persistent(\Delta) \Rightarrow Stabilization(\Delta),
$$

or operationally:

$$
Persistent(\Delta) \Rightarrow Lock(\Delta).
$$

A Lock does not freeze difference forever. It is:

> **a structure that enables a difference to pass into later states as some history even in an environment where it could otherwise transform, dissipate, or reintegrate.**

Depending on domain, Lock may be instantiated through boundary, coupling, feedback, conservation-like relation, activation barrier, record, reproduction, metabolism, institution, language, memory, or another mechanism. The list is not a claim that these are identical. It is a functional commensuration around carrying difference into later states.

---

## 12. Do not define Lock from persistence

This is a central falsification boundary.

If the theory merely says "whatever lasted must have had a Lock," Lock becomes post hoc and unfalsifiable.

Therefore:

$$
Lock \not:= Persistence.
$$

Candidate Lock conditions must be observable, calculable, or structurally describable independently of persistence duration. Candidates include:

1. return toward a relation after small perturbation;
2. a barrier suppressing a transition;
3. retention of historical information after state change;
4. a relational invariant across a range of variation;
5. ability to reconstruct structure through input/output exchange;
6. describable release or collapse conditions;
7. reproduction of a higher-order relation despite replacement of components.

These are not a final definition. They are a minimum defense against circularity.

---

## 13. "Change continues until Lock" remains a hypothesis

A strong human-side formulation is:

> **While an asymmetry fails to satisfy persistence conditions, structure continues to vary; when a Lock-capable structure forms, the difference stabilizes as history.**

Provisional notation:

$$
Lock(S_t,\Delta_t)=0 \Rightarrow Variation(S_{t+1})>0,
$$

$$
Lock(S^*,\Delta^*)=1 \Rightarrow Retention(\Delta^*)>0.
$$

The universal wording "must continue to change" is too strong because stopping, annihilation, freezing, or loss of observability may also occur. A safer statement is:

> **An unlocked difference is not guaranteed to persist in that form.**

# Part V. Function, structure, and difference

## 14. Separate function from structure

Let function be $F_t$ and structure be $S_t$.

Function is an action, relation, or possibility required to satisfy some persistence condition. Structure is the arrangement, boundary, or coupling realizing that function at a given point.

One function can be realized by multiple structures:

$$
F \leftarrow \{S_1,S_2,S_3,\ldots\}.
$$

One structure can generate multiple functions:

$$
S \rightarrow \{F_1,F_2,F_3,\ldots\}.
$$

Therefore:

$$
F \neq S.
$$

---

## 15. Difference appears between function and structure

As a deliberately abstract notation:

$$
\Delta_t = Requirement(F_t) - Capacity(S_t).
$$

This need not be numerical. It includes cases in which:

- a required function cannot be realized by the current structure;
- a structure unexpectedly creates a new possibility of action;
- external conditions change so the old structure cannot persist;
- a structure remains while the function for which it formed becomes unnecessary;
- a new boundary creates a new functional demand.

Difference is not necessarily defect. It may be an entry point for new structural formation.

---

## 16. Co-generation rather than a fixed order

The theory does not assert only a cycle of

```text
function -> structure -> difference -> function -> ...
```

Possible relations include function -> structure, structure -> new function, structure -> difference, difference -> functional demand, difference -> direct structural change, and function -> another function.

A better minimal representation is:

$$
F_t \leftrightarrow S_t \leftrightarrow \Delta_t.
$$

Adding history:

$$
X_t=(F_t,S_t,\Delta_t,H_t),
$$

and next state:

$$
X_{t+1}=\Phi(X_t,E_t),
$$

where $E_t$ denotes environmental conditions or contact with other systems.

---

## 17. Structure generates function

Function need not be completely specified first with structure merely implementing it.

Once a structure exists, it may generate functions that did not previously exist:

$$
S_t \rightarrow NewFunction_{t+1}.
$$

A boundary can make inside/outside exchange possible; a record can return the past into later states; language can transmit experience to another subject.

AMP generative theory is therefore not design-from-purpose. Final cause as persistence constrains formation, but concrete functions and structures can proliferate within the generative process.

# Part VI. How to read laws

Modern science describes stable regularities. AMP does not deny them.

A candidate internal reading is that a law-like form is:

To avoid confusing this with the standard scientific definition of law, AMP provisionally calls it a **law-like heuristic**:

$$
RepeatedRelation + Stability + Transferability
\rightarrow LawLikeHeuristic.
$$

"Heuristic" does not mean the universe consciously searches for an approximate solution. It means that among many possible changes, relations capable of being repeatedly carried into later states can appear rule-like.

# Part VIII. Interference zone with thermodynamics

## 24. Why thermodynamics matters

If AMP claims that persistent asymmetry requires persistence conditions, thermodynamics is an important destructive test.

Temperature, concentration, pressure, and other macroscopic gradients often move toward equilibrium under appropriate conditions. This contacts the AMP intuition that difference does not persist unconditionally.

It does not establish a universal AMP law

$$
AllAsymmetry \rightarrow Dissipation.
$$

Self-gravitating systems, open nonequilibrium systems, phase transitions, and dissipative structures resist a simple homogenization picture. Thermodynamics is therefore a hard collation surface, not proof of AMP.

---

## 25. Nonequilibrium systems are closer to AMP

AMP includes structures that persist while exchanging with the outside.

A conceptual balance for a difference measure $\Delta$ can be written:

$$
\frac{d\Delta}{dt}=G-D+L-E,
$$

where $G$ is generation of difference, $D$ dissipation/homogenization, $L$ Lock/re-stabilization, and $E$ destruction/externalization/loss.

Even where

$$
\frac{d\Delta}{dt}=0,
$$

the system need not be static. Difference may persist through a dynamic balance:

$$
G+L=D+E.
$$

Thus:

$$
Stabilization \neq Stop(Change),
$$

and a better AMP reading is:

$$
Stabilization \approx PreserveDifferenceAmidChange.
$$

AMP therefore must not define "return to symmetry" as simple spatial homogenization. It must distinguish whether asymmetry dissipates, transforms into another asymmetry, becomes Locked under a higher-order invariance, or generates new asymmetry:

# Part IX. Reading time and space as stabilization forms

## 28. Time as manifestation of committed difference

The public AMP introduction says that time appears as histories accumulate and irreversible differences arise.

This note takes a stronger research step: rather than place time as a container prior to the world,

> **the structure in which a difference is committed and retained as history into later states manifests as time.**

Conceptually:

$$
Difference
\xrightarrow{Commit}
RetainedDifference
\xrightarrow{History}
TimeManifestation.
$$

"Commit" does not mean conscious decision. It means that a difference is carried decisively into conditions of the next state, regardless of whether the implementation is physical, biological, cognitive, automatic, or non-automatic.

AMP therefore does not oppose physical time and intrinsic time as two separate time substances. Physical time is one stable rendering of intrinsic, commit-derived history in the physical world:

$$
PhysicalTime = Render_{physical}(CommitHistory).
$$

Clocks, coordinate time, proper time, and their mathematical definitions remain governed by standard physics. AMP asks upstream why such stable time renderings can exist.

---

## 29. Time rendering and logical compression

A human-side hypothesis allows the same history to be rendered at different "time densities" by different structures:

$$
T_i=Render_i(H_{commit}).
$$

A short interval in one rendering does not automatically mean few AMP commits:

$$
Short(Render_i(H)) \nRightarrow FewCommits(H).
$$

Inflation is used as a strong research example. The note does not deny the time scales of standard cosmology. It asks whether a formation phase in which repeatable relations of the later physical world stabilized could contain logically dense structural commits while being rendered as physically brief.

Thus the claim

> the physical-time rendering of inflation is short

is not equated with

> AMP generative time was structurally sparse.

This remains an AMP time-generation hypothesis, not an established physical proposition.

---

## 30. At equilibrium, can time cease to appear as time?

A human-side hypothesis asks whether, in an extreme equilibrium limit, the possibility of committing new differences into history degenerates so strongly that the condition for **time to appear as a category** is lost, rather than time merely "slowing down."

The phrase "equilibrium is extreme asymmetry" is misleading in standard thermodynamic language. The intended AMP claim is instead:

> **the distinguishable transition possibilities required to commit new historical differences may degenerate toward a limit.**

Conceptually:

$$
AvailableNextCommit \rightarrow 0
$$

could accompany

$$
TimeManifestation \rightarrow NonTemporalFlatness.
$$

"Phase transition" here is an AMP operational term, not a claim of a standard physical phase transition. It names a possible loss of the conditions under which time-category description remains applicable.

The note therefore rejects a two-clock model in which physical time continues forever while intrinsic time alone reaches zero. Because physical time is also read as a rendering of commit history, disappearance of commit differences moves the question from the rate of time to the conditions under which time manifests at all.

The intuition of "being stretched forever until zero" may be retained as a formation metaphor for category loss, but not as a formal proposition.

One can imagine a flat state becoming another generative source, a present physical universe inheriting residuals from a prior world of another category, or existences outside the time category. Such imagery may emerge naturally from AMP, but it is not an active hypothesis here.

Even saying "after" a state without time reintroduces temporal order. The current conceptual apparatus does not yet define the relation adequately.

The material therefore remains at the **fiction frontier / contemplative boundary** until an independent difference, support path, and Return path are formed.

The legitimate research question is narrower:

# Part XIII. Connection to audit theory

## 41. Why audit asymmetry?

The most important upstream proposition passed to audit theory is:

$$
PersistentAsymmetry \Rightarrow PersistenceConditions.
$$

Asymmetry is not itself treated as bad. The question is:

> **What makes this asymmetry persist?**

Transferred into social, institutional, or knowledge systems, the audit asks what enters, what is consumed, what is externalized, what is replenished, who is treated as an exchangeable carrier, what would release the Lock, and whether the difference returns to the affected agent.

---

## 42. Separate persistence of a system from persistence of its constituents

A system $S$ can persist while its components $x_i$ do not:

$$
Persist(S) \nRightarrow \forall i\,Persist(x_i).
$$

Long-lived systems can rely on replacement, replenishment, reproduction, externalization, or repair.

Any social use of final cause as persistence must therefore specify:

> **Persistence of whom or what?**

Without that specification, longevity of a violent system can be mistaken for justification.

---

## 43. Audit searches for Lock mechanisms

Questions passed to detectors include:

1. What is asymmetric?
2. Through which Lock is that asymmetry retained?
3. What is the maintenance cost of the Lock?
4. Where does that cost flow?
5. Does the Lock preserve or replace constituents?
6. Does it have a Return path?
7. Is there a release condition?
8. When the Lock breaks, does the difference disappear or transform into another structure?
9. What stabilizes the Lock itself?

Audit therefore becomes not the elimination of asymmetry, but **exposure of the mechanism by which asymmetry persists**.

The concepts are not identical. Scientific falsification has domain-specific logics, measurements, and experimental conditions. SO Return can include objection, unresolved difference, cut, self-reference failure, semantic difference, and institutional burden more broadly.

# Part XVII. Falsification routes

## 56. Where can this note break?

If the note is to function as a backbone for audit theory, its failure points must be explicit. Falsification is layered rather than reduced to one Yes/No flag.

---

## 57. Internal-logical falsification candidates

### 57.1 Persistence does not entail any invariance

If systems can legitimately be called the same persistent system while every element and every relation is replaced without any traceable invariant, the bridge

$$
Persistence \Rightarrow Invariance
$$

fails and pressure reaches the symmetry hypothesis.

### 57.2 Asymmetry persists without any independently defined Lock

If long-lived asymmetries systematically exist where no independently defined Lock can be detected, then

$$
Persistent(\Delta) \Rightarrow Lock(\Delta)
$$

must be revised.

### 57.3 Lock does not contribute to persistence

If candidate Locks can be detected but bear no relation to persistence rate, the definition is empty or wrong.

### 57.4 Final cause as persistence is circular

If "what persisted" is simply renamed "what fulfilled its purpose," and purpose is then defined as persistence, no explanatory increment is produced. Persistence conditions must operate as independently discriminating constraints during formation.

---

## 58. Formal-model falsification candidates

### 58.1 AMP model is indistinguishable from comparison models

If AMP Lock Model cannot be distinguished on major observables from random drift, explicit optimization, Darwinian replicator, or related models, the generative hypothesis may be redundant.

### 58.2 No predictive difference

If every result can be explained after the fact as "another kind of Lock," the model is overfitted. Lock classes and their predicted ranges need prior restriction.

### 58.3 Works only through narrow parameter tuning

If the AMP sequence appears only under narrow parameter choices, universal claims must be weakened.

---

## 59. Falsification of concrete physical correspondences

Not every metaphysical proposition in the note can be falsified by physics alone. But concrete physical claims must be testable when made.

Claims such as a specific interaction forming at a specific epoch, a physical law being derived from a proposed formation mechanism, one-to-one mapping of AMP variables to observables, or inflation necessarily generating a particular Lock must face standard physics and observational cosmology.

If such a claim conflicts with established facts, the response cannot be "this is metaphysics." The local physical claim is corrected or withdrawn.

---

## 60. Thermodynamic falsification pressure

Thermodynamics asks at least:

1. Which asymmetries dissipate?
2. How far can open-system inflow maintain them?
3. How are cases such as self-gravitating systems handled, where structure formation and entropy increase coexist?
4. If new order at a phase transition is called Lock, what independently defines that Lock?
5. Does the AMP hypothesis of time-category loss at equilibrium add anything beyond low change rate, unobservability, or formal stationarity?

Unresolved differences remain on the AMP side.

---

## 61. Darwinian falsification pressure

If AMP generalizes Darwinian structure, it must distinguish persistence without heredity, selection without replication, stabilization without selection pressure, and Locks unrelated to fitness.

Renaming all natural selection "persistence filtering" would add no explanatory value to evolutionary theory.

---

## 62. Self-application falsification candidates

This note is itself a persistent knowledge structure. It must therefore audit whether it externalizes counterexamples to protect AMP, neutralizes scientific objections as "another language game," turns final cause or Lock into universal gap-fillers, assimilates every result into the function-structure-difference loop, or silently projects human-side intuition into canon.

If the criteria applied to other systems cannot be applied to this note, it fails as an audit-theory backbone.

The correct statement is therefore:

## 64.1 Verification scope depends on present exploration resources

Whether a proposition is "currently verifiable" is not a property of the proposition alone. It depends on available sensors, measurement systems, formal language and mathematics, comparison targets, experimental design, computation, records, history, and contact with other systems.

Let current verification surface be:

$$
\mathcal{V}_t=V(B_t,\Pi_t,H_t,E_t),
$$

where $B_t$ is available exploration resources, $\Pi_t$ available verification/search operators, $H_t$ formation history, and $E_t$ currently available observation/fact candidates.

Then

$$
Q \notin \mathcal{V}_t
$$

implies neither $\neg Q$ nor $Q$. It only means that under present resources the proposition has not entered the adjudication surface.

This connects with the SO view that the current logical space $\Omega_t$ is not the world itself and that engineering correctness can only be treated as resource- and history-relative search saturation.

## 64.2 Layered-world hypotheses are examples outside current verification scope

If AMP is not restricted to the physical world, one can imagine human-world, cat-world, dog-world, worlds formed from different observation/commit histories, other persons as overlaps of independently formed worlds, or existences that treat a human time category as an object.

This note does not assert those as facts. There are not enough data, operational definitions, sensors, comparison models, or invalidation conditions.

The correct boundary is:

> **Not presently verifiable does not mean nonexistent, and not presently falsifiable does not mean existent.**

These strong layered-world images remain close to Pure Unknown and at the fiction frontier until a return path is formed.

---

## 64.3 Verification is not the whole of correctness

Conversely, verifiability or success in one verification cannot be elevated into the whole of correctness.

Verification depends on current $\mathcal{V}_t$; weak operators may fail to reach important defeaters; agreement with one observation strengthens a support path but does not erase competing explanations, unexplored regions, scope limits, or design bias.

SO engineering correctness is therefore not one verification flag but a compound state such as:

$$
Correct_t
\sim
InternalViability
+ ActualSearch
+ Saturation
+ DefeaterAudit
+ Return/Reopening,
$$

where `+` is not numeric addition.

Verification can be a strong support/search operator without replacing the whole. None of this weakens empirical constraint: when AMP makes a physical factual claim, empirical science remains one of the strongest external constraints.

---

## 64.4 Separate "not currently verifiable" from "no return path in principle"

Resource-relative verification must not become a license to hold every claim indefinitely.

At least three cases must be separated:

### A. Not reached under current resources

Required observations, mathematics, comparison models, records, or computation are presently insufficient. Changes in $B_t$ or $\Pi_t$ may later bring the claim into the verification surface.

### B. No operational definition yet

The research has not yet specified what observation would change the claim. It may remain a research hypothesis, but not with the strength of an empirical claim.

### C. No return path is admitted in principle

The claim is constructed so that no observation, logic, or objection can change it. This is not merely "not yet verifiable"; it may be structurally cut off from audit and falsification.

Strong layered-world imagery must move from A or B toward an actual future Return path before re-entering active research. If it remains C, it stays explicitly in fiction/contemplation rather than being strengthened as knowledge.

# Part XXII. Unresolved points, falsification candidates, residuals

## 75. Is final cause as persistence actually causal?

If persistence is merely a new name for what happened to remain, no cause has been added. To merit causal language, persistence conditions must be formalized as differential constraints during generation.

It remains unclear under what observational or logical state $Root=One$ could fail. If it is made purely definitional, it becomes unfalsifiable. A comparison with multiple-root models is required.

Physical symmetry, mathematical invariance, identity preservation, and historical continuity may be conflated. The proposition should first be weakened to Invariance, after which the justified range of the word Symmetry can be tested.

## 78. Do differences really disappear when left alone?

The strong older statement that unattended differences dissipate or homogenize may be too general. Isolated systems, open systems, gravitating systems, symbolic systems, and institutions require separate treatment.

## 79. Is one concept of Lock enough?

Calling gravity, atomic bonding, cell membranes, records, and legal institutions all "Lock" may over-abstract. Types such as dynamical, boundary, historical, reproductive, or institutional Lock may be needed.

and co-generation remains unresolved.

## 81. Is space a stabilization form for separation?

## 82. Is gravity a first Lock?

The existing research hypothesis requires clarification of what "first" means, the order relative to time and space, description of gravity in the early universe, and relations to other interactions. Ontological reading order and chronological cosmic formation order must be separated.

## 83. What explanatory increment comes from reading inflation as a formation phase?

Merely saying "it was early, so nothing was fixed yet" adds little. The formation-phase model must produce at least formal differences about which relations later fix, which differences disappear, and what changes across Lock formation.

## 84. Equilibrium and disappearance of the time category

No measure yet exists for $AvailableNextCommit \rightarrow 0$. Loss of commit possibility must be separated from low rate of change, sensor resolution, or formal stationarity.

# Part XXIII. Final compression

## 88. Extending causality in modern science

> **Where modern science describes with high precision what produces what from one state to the next, AMP retains those results and additionally asks why those interactions, structures, and law-like forms became repeatable enough to persist into later states.**

## 89. Final cause

> **Final cause in AMP is not intention supplied from the future. It is the persistence condition a structure must satisfy to continue its history. Persistence does not mean goodness.**

## 90. Function, structure, and difference

> **Existence formation is not a fixed one-way design from function to structure. Function, structure, and difference alter one another: structure can create new functions; functions can create new differences; and differences can induce later structure formation.**

## 91. Asymmetry and Lock

> **A difference does not become history merely by occurring. The metaphysical question of why law-like form became sustainable does not close under exactly the same adjudication rules. Differences are kept in an interference zone rather than converted into a winner/loser result.**
