## 1. Directory Role

This directory retains the generative images supplied by formative materials in the AMP, ITS, and Gödel lines without silently projecting those earlier materials back into present Scientific Ontology. It functions as a **research surface for cross-collating generative metaphysics, history, physics-adjacent correspondence, and the engineering of audit and correctness**.

The documents here do not constitute one completed authoritative metaphysical system. Strong claims inherited from earlier AMP/ITS materials, current candidates for generative metaphysics, physics-adjacent hypotheses, audit theory, and implementation specifications are placed beside one another while preserving their distinct claim strengths and formation histories. The purpose is to examine what, if anything, can later be returned to a candidate authoritative text.

Two layers are especially important.

1. **Generative metaphysics, formative sources, and physics-adjacent research**
   - Examines generative routes through differentiation, persistence of relation, formation of logic, fixation and recovery, time, observation, and self-reference, together with formative sources, historical computation, and physics-adjacent correspondence.

2. **Engineering Research on Correctness**
   - Commensurates correctness, closure, contradiction, Spine, surplus, logical plane, and α/β audit toward logical, structural, and computational forms, while examining both implementability and the differences lost in implementation.

The latter is not automatically derived from the former, nor does it prove the former. Successful audit implementation would not by itself demonstrate the validity of generative metaphysics or of the canonical Scientific Ontology texts.

### Structural position

```text
earlier AMP / ITS / Gödel-line formative materials
  ↓ referenced as formative sources
Metaphysical Approach
  ├─ generative metaphysics / history / physics-adjacent research
  └─ Engineering Research on Correctness
       ↓ commensuration toward logic / structure / computation
       implementation candidates / toy models / machine-readable audit surface
  ↓
counterexamples / mismatches / residuals / implementation failures / external findings
  ↓
return to the relevant canonical text, research line, or future v6 reconstruction
```

This directory does not pre-emptively establish the v6 Applied Metaphysics authoritative text. It makes visible the route currently leading toward an authoritative candidate while retaining unresolved regions, the strength of physical correspondence, and the distance between theory and audit implementation.

## 2. Public Scope

### 2.1 Included

- Research that reads AMP, ITS, and Gödel-line documents not as grounds of proof but as formative supply lines.
- Research that re-examines differentiation, persistence, function, structure, difference, fixation, recovery, time, observation, and self-reference in generative order.
- Historical-computation models that distinguish irreversible event history from dependencies that can be reconstructed in the present.
- Physics-adjacent hypotheses that bring light, causality, distance, space, and time into contact without identifying Scientific Ontology with standard physics.
- Overview and review drafts leading toward the candidate authoritative text *Applied Metaphysics System*.
- Research on the metaphysical conditions under which audit becomes necessary.
- Research on α audit, β audit, logical plane, Spine, surplus, and finite-resource exploration.
- Candidate implementation specifications dividing audit work between LLMs and deterministic computation.
- Research that retains mismatches produced by implementation, commensuration, or contact with external fields as residuals and returns them upstream.

### 2.2 Not included

- A declaration that the v6 Applied Metaphysics authoritative text has already been established.
- Direct adoption of earlier AMP/ITS physical correspondences into present canonical Scientific Ontology.
- Rewriting definitions belonging to standard physics, cosmology, mathematical logic, mathematics, or computer science.
- Treating generative hypotheses placed in research notes as empirically established facts.
- Promoting `Spine` or other research vocabulary into public foundational vocabulary before adjudication.
- Treating successful audit implementation or machine audit as proof that a theory is correct.
- Registering `000*` formative materials, earlier drafts, or local working files in the public index or Manifest.
