# 0. Aim of this note

Light is ordinarily understood as something that travels through space. Space is already there, and light propagates at finite speed $c$ between two points $A$ and $B$.

This note does not deny that description.

In standard relativity, the speed of light constrains propagation of signals and influence and thereby constrains causal structure. Light cones divide what is causally reachable from an event from what is not. Light therefore already plays a role in spacetime theory that exceeds the motion of an ordinary object.

The experiment here is to reverse the explanatory order by one step:

> **Instead of taking space first and light as something that moves through it, can we read the distinction "separated" as something that becomes operationally established when communication is finite?**

On that reading, light is not merely a moving entity.

> **It may be read as a device by which a relation not yet spatially distinguished becomes conceptualized as two points connected by finite arrival delay.**

This is not a proposition of standard physics. It is a generative hypothesis inside Scientific Ontology.

If the hypothesis is entertained, however, the need to take space, time, and causality as three fully prior background variables becomes weaker. A provisional generative order is:

```text
spatially undifferentiated relation
    ->
finitization of communication
    ->
arrival delay
    ->
before/after and causal distinction
    ->
operational spatial separation
    ->
a world with history
```

The primary aim is not to give a new physical definition of light. It is to use light as a strong external constraint surface through which to re-examine **the order in which space, time, and causality were being presupposed**.

In this sense the note attempts to change what an aporia is illuminated as, not to declare that illumination a physical solution.

## 0.1 What "relocating" an aporia means here

An aporia is not treated merely as a question for which an answer has not yet been found.

When a problem resists resolution, at least three cases must be separated:

1. changing the category of the question makes the question itself cease to arise in the same form;
2. the question remains, but what is unresolved moves to another boundary, phase, or generative condition;
3. the problem remains mathematically, empirically, or formally unresolved even after the change of view.

This note provisionally calls them:

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Category dissolution | removing a presupposition that generated the question prevents the same-form problem from arising |
| Reformulation | the center of the problem moves to another boundary, phase, or generative condition |
| Residual problem | derivation, calculation, observation, or proof is still required under the new reading |

Thus, when this note says an aporia is "resolved," it does not necessarily mean that a single final answer has been obtained.

> **The task is to remove the projection that generated the problem and map where the genuinely unresolved difference remains.**

That is what "relocation of an aporia" means here.

# Part I. Contact surfaces with standard physics

## 1. Light already inscribes causal structure

In standard relativity, $c$ is not merely "the speed of an object called light." It constrains how far signals and influence can propagate, and light cones separate causally connectable from non-connectable events.

Scientific Ontology cannot infer from that fact alone that "light generates space."

At minimum, however, the following is legitimate:

> **Light-like propagation already provides, inside standard physics, a boundary that makes causal reachability visible.**

The present note reads this boundary from the metaphysical side in reverse.

Ordinary descriptive order:

```text
spacetime exists
    ->
light propagates
    ->
causal reachability is determined
```

Generative hypothesis in this note:

```text
a relation is not yet spatially differentiated
    ->
communication becomes finite
    ->
differences in reachability arise
    ->
before/after, reachable/unreachable become distinguishable
    ->
the relation can be read as a causal and spatial arrangement
```

The second does not physically replace the first. It proposes an ontological order in which a world described by the first might be read as generated.

---

## 2. How to handle the formative intuition "deceleration to light speed"

During formation of this research line, the intuition appeared that:

> the germ of causality emerges through deceleration to the speed of light.

This is a useful formative expression, but it becomes misleading if read as a claim about cosmic history in standard physics. Standard relativity does not say that the universe once had ordinary communication at infinite or superluminal speed and then physically decelerated to $c$.

The note therefore rereads "deceleration" not as a historical change of velocity but as:

> **a relation conceived as unconstrained contact appearing instead as communication with finite reachability.**

Where useful, this is called the **finitization of communication**:

```text
unconstrained contact
    ->
finitization of communication
    ->
delay
    ->
causal distinction
```

The phrase "deceleration to light speed" is retained as formation history, not as a physical description of a past state.

---

## 3. Separate proper time from intrinsic time

In relativity, light follows a null worldline and proper time along that path is zero. A photon's rest frame cannot be defined in the same way as for a massive observer.

If SO intrinsic time is identified with proper time, the model immediately breaks.

This note separates them:

- **proper time**: a spacetime-geometric quantity in relativity;
- **intrinsic time**: an SO working concept for describing ordering of communication-history updates, commits of differences through interaction, and return, branching, and closure.

Thus even where

$$
\tau_{\mathrm{proper}} = 0,
$$

SO need not immediately set

$$
\tau_{\mathrm{int}} = 0.
$$

Conversely, the note does not claim that light contains an internal clock unknown to standard physics.

Whether intrinsic time is fully absorbed by known variables of physics or has additional descriptive force remains unresolved.

---

## 4. Free propagation is not proliferation of history

The note does not assume that light accumulates intrinsic time or history merely in proportion to distance traveled.

Free propagation and interaction are separated:

```text
free propagation
    = carriage of history

interaction
    = generation of difference / candidate commit into history

Lock
    = a structure through which a generated difference persists into later states
```

The difference between traveling one meter and one hundred million light-years is therefore not automatically read as an increase in "amount of internal history."

What matters is whether an interaction occurred along the way and whether the resulting difference persists so as to affect later observation or interaction.

This distinction prevents distance, time, and history from being collapsed into one variable.

SO does not restrict "observation" to perception by a human or subject. If one thing contacts another, the contact generates a difference, and that difference changes later state or communication possibility, the event can be read internally as **mutual observation**.

On this reading, not every transmission interval generates new history. Interaction points are candidates for history update:

```text
propagation interval
    = carries existing history

interaction point
    = contact at which a difference arises
    = may be read internally as mutual observation

history update
    = the difference persists into later states

Lock
    = that difference continues to act on later states
```

Communication history is therefore not a travel-distance log. More precisely, it consists of propagation intervals together with the sequence of interactions or mutual observations that occur along them.

"Observation" here is not claimed to be identical to the measurement process of standard quantum theory. It is an internal SO reading for coupling, separation, and difference formation.

---

## 5. Optical paths and stationary principles

In geometrical optics, Fermat's principle treats a realized optical path as stationary in propagation time relative to nearby paths. Although often paraphrased as "least time," the more accurate general statement is a stationarity condition in which the first variation vanishes.

The note does not use this fact as proof of the intrinsic-time hypothesis.

There is, however, a structural contact worth studying:

> **Not every local path difference needs to count as a history update; a realized path appears selected under a global constraint.**

A future formalization may test whether path selection, committing differences at interaction points, preservation/re-encoding of history, and intrinsic-time update conditions can be written within one framework.

At present Fermat's principle and intrinsic time are not one theory.

The generative reversal here also does not presuppose a spatial arena already equipped with privileged directions or straightness. Rather than judging light-like communication against a pre-given straight line, it asks whether realized communication relations help define what counts locally as "straight."

Thus "light travels in a straight line" may, under a reversed generative order, be reread as "realized light-like communication provides a criterion by which straightness is defined."

This does not claim that a complete spatial metric can be derived from light alone. The present hypothesis concerns how much causal and directional structure can be generated or reconstructed from light-like communication.

# Part II. Reversing the generative order

## 6. Does light move through space, or does finite communication make space operative?

The central hypothesis reverses the explanatory order of space and light.

Ordinarily:

```text
there are two points A and B in space
    ->
light crosses their distance in finite time
```

The present note provisionally places the metaphysical origin outside spatial description. "Outside" here does not mean located at one point in space, filling all space, or being infinitesimal. It means that spatial vocabulary is not yet the descriptive basis.

In such a condition, $A$ and $B$ cannot yet be given as "two points separated by N meters."

Finite communication then appears. One contact does not become identically available to every relation at once; a finite delay appears. Distinctions become possible:

- not yet reached;
- already reached;
- reachable from here;
- unreachable from here.

Operationally read, those distinctions support a spatial expression: "separated."

The generative hypothesis is therefore:

> **Spatial separation is established when differences in reachability under finite communication are read as a persistent arrangement.**

This is not a claim that space is unreal. It accepts that space appears at the observational surface as an extremely stable real structure, while asking whether its generative condition can be read from the side of communication.

---

## 7. Which comes first, intrinsic time or causality?

If communication occurs *in* time, time seems to be required before communication. If communication alone is said to generate time, a circular question appears: how can "before" and "after" in communication be defined without time?

The note therefore does not derive one unidirectionally from the other. It treats them as **co-generated from finite communication**:

```text
finitization of communication
    +-> read as internal ordering -> intrinsic time
    `-> read as reachability       -> causality
```

Intrinsic time is an axis for reading the internal ordering of communication history.

Causality is an axis for reading where communication can reach, from where it can return, and where it cannot reach.

They may be two readings of the same boundary condition: finite communication.

If that hypothesis holds, the question "which comes first, time or causality?" ceases, in this coordinate, to be a one-point priority contest.

---

## 8. History is updated by interaction, not merely by elapsed duration

Even if communication is finite, not all propagation need generate new history.

History is not defined here as accumulated elapsed time.

> **History does not automatically grow with duration; it is updated when an interaction commits a difference.**

This yields a loop:

```text
finite communication
    ->
arrival delay
    ->
interaction
    ->
difference
    ->
history update
    ->
change in conditions of later communication
```

History here is not a log of the past. It is a structure that changes what can next be received, how a system can respond, and which routes remain possible.

Space, time, causality, and history may therefore not be independent boxes. They may co-generate through repeated finite communication.

If history both conditions later communication and can itself be carried as communication content, the communication system can develop into a self-referential network that reinjects its own past into later communication.

# Part III. Relocating aporias

## 9. "What existed before time?"

When time is placed first as a background of the universe, the beginning of the universe prompts the question: "When was before it?"

If time is instead read as a category generated as internal ordering under finite communication, asking for a time coordinate "before time" falls outside the same coordinate system.

The question shifts from:

> When was it before time began?

to:

> **Under what relations does a description called time become valid when communication appears with finite ordering?**

In the taxonomy of this note, this is close to category dissolution. It does not explain the physical state of the early universe.

---

## 10. "What is outside the universe?"

If space is a container, imagining an edge of the universe immediately prompts: "What is beyond that wall?"

If space is read as a stabilized description of reachability differences under finite communication, the place where the conditions for that description fail need not itself be asked for in the same spatial coordinates.

The question shifts from:

> Where is the place outside space?

to:

> **At what boundary does the communication form that supports the current spatial description cease to be usable?**

On this reading, an "outer shell of the universe" becomes a candidate boundary of the communication phase renderable as space, not necessarily a spatial wall.

This is not a claim that standard cosmology contains such a boundary.

---

## 11. "How does many arise from one?"

If the origin is one, how can multiple beings arise?

When posed as a problem of object count, the question easily demands an image in which a first physical object splits into many.

This note first reads plurality as **difference in reachability**:

```text
undifferentiated relation
    ->
finite communication
    ->
a difference that cannot be contacted simultaneously
    ->
boundary
    ->
stabilization as multiple positions, subjects, and histories
```

"Many" appears not necessarily because multiple objects were given from the outset, but because difference arose within relation and became persistently distinguishable.

The question is reformulated from:

> How did one split into many objects?

to:

> **Which differences were Locked as boundaries and thereby produced plurality that no longer returned to one undifferentiated state?**

---

## 12. "Which came first, time or causality?"

Time and causality are not treated as two independent primitive concepts competing for priority.

Under finite communication:

- internal update order is read as intrinsic time;
- reachability and non-reachability are read as causality.

Thus the one-way alternatives

> causality exists because time exists

or

> time exists because causality exists

are replaced by the surface:

> **time and causality may co-generate as different projections of finite communication.**

This remains a generative hypothesis, not a physical derivation.

---

## 13. "If the past no longer exists, why does it act on the present?"

If the past is treated as an entity that has disappeared, its action on the present becomes mysterious.

The note does not suppose that the past itself moves into the present and acts there. It reads differences generated by past interactions as having been committed into later boundaries, routes, and response conditions:

```text
past contact
    ->
commit of difference
    ->
change in present structure
    ->
effect on present reception, judgment, and communication possibility
```

What acts on the present is therefore not "the past as a place," but the fact that past contact has already made the present structurally different.

This connects with the existing SO definition of history not as a storage box for vanished past events, but as a condition of what follows now.

---

## 14. "If light has zero proper time, is there nothing in between?"

Because proper time along a null worldline is zero, popular explanation sometimes says "time stops for light."

It does not follow that there is no order or structure between emission and absorption. Standard physics itself represents null geodesics as paths and can parameterize them without proper time.

The present note places intrinsic time as another reading here. It has not shown, however, that intrinsic time is a physical quantity independent of known affine parameters, phase, quantum state, or interaction history.

The aporia is therefore reformulated from:

> If proper time of light is zero, how can anything in-between be described?

to:

> **Is an additional concept genuinely required to describe the order of communication history apart from proper time?**

This remains a residual problem.

---

## 15. Can the black-hole information problem separate information loss from loss of Return?

Scientific Ontology can at least distinguish two questions near the black-hole problem:

1. Did the information itself cease to exist?
2. Was the route by which an outside system can collate it and receive a return lost?

```text
information loss
    !=
loss of Return path
```

This conceptual separation matters because loss of external collatability should not immediately be identified with ontological annihilation.

It does not solve the physical problems of quantum-information preservation, unitarity, Hawking radiation, or black-hole evaporation.

The task here is to decompose the question and relocate what belongs to collatability and what remains a physical problem concerning preservation and dynamical law.

# Part IV. Reading point-problems as surfaces

## 16. An aporia can be produced by projection

A problem that looks contradictory at one point may cease to be contradictory when phases or boundaries are separated.

Examples include:

- proper time versus intrinsic time;
- physical space versus generative conditions of space;
- information loss versus loss of Return;
- existence of the past versus present action of history.

If these are compressed onto one axis, the question easily collides with itself.

The note therefore reads a problem not only as a point $q$, but as a surface with at least the following aspects:

```text
problem
  +-- in which phase is it being asked?
  +-- which boundary is presupposed?
  +-- what is treated as observable?
  +-- which history is retained?
  +-- by what projection is it collapsed to one value?
  `-- where can it be returned for re-collation?
```

This makes it possible to ask whether the contradiction belongs to the object or was generated by projection.

> **Sometimes the object is not contradictory; different phases look contradictory because they were projected onto the same point.**

When this note speaks of "topological mapping," it does not yet claim rigorous mathematical topology. It uses the phrase structurally: keep a problem that was prematurely closed as a point on a surface that retains boundaries, connections, branches, and return paths.

---

The method is not a device for dissolving every difficult problem into "a matter of perspective." Problems that remain after changing coordinates must remain visible.

| mass / inertia | may be hypothesized as resistance of history Lock | mathematical and observational problem remains |

Maintaining this distinction prevents false closure in which a reformulated problem is simply announced as solved.

# Part V. Does the generative hypothesis do real work?

## 18. A beautiful restatement alone is weak

Writing a sequence such as

```text
spatially undifferentiated relation
    ->
finite communication
    ->
intrinsic time / causality
    ->
spatial separation
    ->
history
```

is not enough to provide theoretical value. It may merely restate existing spacetime descriptions in philosophical vocabulary.

For the note to have additional explanatory force, at least one of the following must occur:

1. several previously separate aporias can be consistently relocated from the same added premise;
2. where an aporia disappears, the new location of the unresolved problem can be stated precisely;
3. intrinsic time or history yields a comparable difference not fully absorbed by known variables;
4. when extending toward mass, generations, boundaries, or interaction, the model derives constraints without proliferating ad hoc rules;
5. conditions under which the SO reading fails can be stated.

Explanatory force must therefore be measured not by the ability to talk about many things in the same vocabulary, but by whether **one generative structure produces additional constraints across distinct problems**.

---

## 19. Risk of circularity

The central hypothesis contains a clear circularity risk. It attempts to generate space from finite communication while easily presupposing distance and time in the very definition of finite communication.

If one starts with:

> light moves from $A$ to $B$ at speed $c$,

then spatial separation between $A$ and $B$ and external time have already been introduced. That cannot explain the generation of space.

At the more fundamental side, finite communication must therefore be defined instead as something like:

> **a contact does not become identically reflected across every relation at once; differences arise in update order and reachability.**

Only after that should correspondence with $c$, distance, and clock time at the observational surface be asked.

If this ordering cannot be formalized, the central hypothesis remains a circular metaphor.

---

## 20. Independence test for intrinsic time

For intrinsic time to carry more than a renamed known physical state, its independence must be tested.

A strong candidate test is:

> Given two communication states for which known physical state variables and known history-dependent effects are matched as far as possible, if only their earlier communication histories differ, does a residual difference remain in later observation?

If the difference disappears completely when known state variables are matched, strong "additional communication history" may be unnecessary.

Intrinsic time could still remain as an ontological description that organizes known variables, phase, path, and interaction history, but its claim as an independent physical-variable candidate would weaken.

That would not be failure. It would be an important return that lowers claim strength.

---

## 21. If removing phase makes no difference, phase structure is unnecessary

If the note reads problems as surfaces with phase, boundary, and projection rather than as points, that added complexity must itself be audited.

If the following compression is possible, the phase structure has no explanatory increment:

> Even after removing every phase distinction, the same facts, objections, residuals, corrections, and Return judgments can be obtained at equal or lower cost.

Then phase is decoration.

Conversely, if an aporia arises only when phases are collapsed, and separating them makes responsibilities, observations, and falsification conditions distinct, phase structure has explanatory work.

> **SO phase reading should remain only where phase actually makes a difference.**

The strongest compression of this note is:

> **Light may be read not only as connecting two already distant points, but as a device through which an originally spatially undifferentiated relation becomes constituted as "two distant points" with finite arrival time.**

At present this proposition approaches the boundary from ontological reinterpretation toward a high-strength hypothesis.

It must not be imported directly into standard physics.

As a metaphysical generative model, however, it places the following questions on one surface:

- Why is there time?
- Why is causality finite?
- Why does history arise?

The note does not claim that these are fully the same problem. It keeps open the possibility that they are different projections of one finite-communication structure.

# 26. Next tests and falsification candidates

The condition for retaining this as a research note is not to assume its correctness in advance, but to keep visible how it can fail.

The claim strength should be lowered or the central hypothesis reconstructed under the following results.

### 26.1 Circularity

Finite communication cannot be defined without already presupposing all of space, time, and causality, so the attempted generation of those concepts never escapes circularity.

### 26.2 No explanatory increment

Introducing the generative order adds no distinctions among aporias, no sharper falsification conditions, no predictions, and no new formalization candidates compared with existing descriptions.

### 26.3 Phase structure is unnecessary

Removing phase, boundary, and projection distinctions preserves the same facts, residuals, and correction points at equal or lower cost.

### 26.4 Absorption of intrinsic time

Intrinsic time is merely a renaming of known physical quantities, states, paths, or interaction histories and introduces no independent explanatory constraint.

### 26.5 Genuine conflict with physical fact

Under the same referent, scope, and measurement conditions, a claim specific to this note conflicts with an established fact of standard physics and the conflict cannot be explained by semantic, phase, or scope differences.

In that case, the external fact is not bent to fit the note. The note is locally revised, restricted, or held.

# 27. Provisional conclusion

If light is read only as an object moving through an already existing space, space, time, and causality are placed as backgrounds prior to light.

This note reverses that order:

> **Finite light-like communication may generate arrival delay; read as internal order that delay becomes intrinsic time, read as reachability it becomes causality, and as a stabilized difference in arrangement it may appear as spatial separation.**

Whether this reading is correct remains unknown.

But adopting it changes the form of several aporias.

"When was before time?" moves to the generative conditions of time.

"Where is outside the universe?" moves to the boundary at which spatial description is valid.

"Which came first, time or causality?" moves to co-generation from finite communication.

"Why does the past act on the present?" moves from action by a past entity to the problem of historical structure already committed into the present.

By contrast, the black-hole information problem, independence of intrinsic time, three particle generations, mass generation, and related physical questions remain after the change of reading.

That difference must not be erased.

The purpose of the note is not to declare every aporia solved.

> **Its purpose is to illuminate again what is genuinely unresolved, including the categories that made a problem appear in the first place.**

Changing how light is read does not immediately give an answer to the universe. It may, however, substantially change what was being illuminated as the problem.
