## 0. Aim

The correctness-topology line defines engineering conditions under which a finite subject may currently treat a judgment as correct: internal viability, search saturation, Return/Reopening, null, cut, residual, and self-reference.

Its central question is roughly:

> **May this judgment be maintained now, and what arrival would require it to reopen?**

This note moves one step upstream:

> **Why is audit needed at all? Why must self-reference and null be detected? Why must a system that acts outward permit effects to return to itself?**

The starting point is not an abstract ideal of fairness, humility, or openness. AMP reads the world not as a finished collection of objects but as a generative process of differentiation, boundary formation, history, communication, retention, and semantic closure from a primordial entity.

Separation, difference, and asymmetry are not bad by definition. Subject and object, mind and nature, observer and observed, inside and outside all require some difference to become distinguishable.

Therefore:

$$
Asymmetry \neq Violation.
$$

The audit question is instead **which asymmetry persists by retaining what, wearing down what, externalizing what, and replenishing what**.

A knowledge system also acts on the world when it observes, classifies, explains, criticizes, and predicts. Action itself is not denied.

But if the consequences of that action do not return to the acting system; if the acting system alone exempts itself from revision; and if it maintains its external form by consuming histories, resources, subjects, or future generations as replaceable carriers, then persistence of the system and persistence of its constituents diverge.

Audit then becomes more than error detection. It asks:

> **Whose persistence is constituted by whose non-persistence?**

> **Who acts, who is acted upon, and where does the result return?**

> **Which residuals, burdens, nulls, or high-disorder regions are externalized so that the current order can appear stable?**

> **Does the structure continue by retaining the same beings, or by wearing out, replacing, and reproducing them?**

The theory for re-collating these questions is what this note calls audit theory.

### 0.2 Responsibility boundary between adjudication and audit

This note separates **adjudication** from **audit**.

Adjudication addresses local proposition-level validity:

$$
Adjudicate(p,\phi_t),
$$

asking how far proposition $p$ holds in current phase $\phi_t$ under factual constraints, support, scope, defeaters, and search history.

Audit addresses the larger arrangement containing that proposition:

$$
Audit(D,S),
$$

asking of document $D$ or system $S$:

- Is placing the proposition there appropriate?
- Does claim strength match support?
- Are fact, theory, hypothesis, and residual mixed without labels?
- Do expression, structure, or chapter placement conceal objections, Return paths, or reopen conditions?
- Has a lower-layer research hypothesis been silently promoted into higher-layer canon?
- Has a locally adjudicated proposition been over-applied beyond its scope?

Therefore:

$$
Adjudicated(p,\phi_t) \not\Rightarrow Audited(D,S).
$$

Audit may call multiple proposition-level adjudications, but it is not reducible to truth judgment. It also examines **where a locally viable proposition is placed, at what strength, with which history and Return path**.

# Part I. Existence, difference, and asymmetry

## 1. Asymmetry is not the enemy of existence

AMP does not begin from a set of already independent finished objects. From a primordial entity, cuts, differentiation, and boundary formation generate distinctions such as subject/object, mind/nature, observer/observed.

Without difference, boundaries do not arise. Without boundaries, observation, contact, and communication do not arise.

Thus asymmetry is not itself breakdown or evil.

For two distinguishable beings or fields $A$ and $B$:

$$
\Delta(A,B) \neq 0.
$$

If that difference remains traceable through history, $A$ and $B$ can be treated as distinct beings, roles, fields, or states. Persistence is therefore deeply related to retention of difference.

A conceptual candidate is:

$$
Persist(X)
\Rightarrow
Retention(\Delta_X,H_X,B_X),
$$

where $\Delta_X$ distinguishes $X$, $H_X$ is the history by which it reached its present state, and $B_X$ is the boundary condition that retains difference while permitting contact.

This is not a theorem. It is a formalization candidate connecting AMP to audit theory.

The key reading is not that existence is preserved because the world is symmetric, but that a differentiated being persists because it can retain its difference in some form.

## 2. The issue is not the existence of asymmetry but its mode of persistence

Many legitimate relations are asymmetric: parent/child, teacher/learner, physician/patient, driver/passenger, administrator/user, researcher/research subject, present/future generation.

Demanding complete interchangeability would destroy responsibility, expertise, safety, and temporal difference.

Audit theory therefore does not set:

$$
Asymmetry = Wrong.
$$

It asks instead:

1. By what is the asymmetry retained?
2. Where do burdens and benefits flow?
3. To whom do the consequences of action return?
4. Can that Return alter the actor itself?
5. Whose history is retained, and whose history is treated as replaceable?
6. Where are residuals, wear, and uncertainty discharged to preserve the current order?
7. Does the asymmetry continue by preserving constituents, or by wearing them down, replacing them, and reproducing their roles?

This creates a need to distinguish **sustainable asymmetry** from **externally sustained asymmetry**. The latter may be locally destructive while appearing globally stable because it continuously imports new carriers.

# Part II. Intrusion, violence, and force

## 3. Contact can be intrusive

SO does not treat contact as a colorless event. Contact between different histories and boundaries can alter later conditions for one or both sides.

This broad action is called **intrusion** here:

$$
I_{A\to B}(t).
$$

Where

$$
I_{A\to B}(t) \neq 0,
$$

A's action changed some audit-relevant aspect of B's state, boundary, history, meaning, options, or later conditions.

Intrusion may include observation, classification, criticism, teaching, translation, diagnosis, persuasion, institutional processing, physical contact, economic exchange, or legal judgment.

> **If intrusion itself were prohibited, contact, learning, observation, and repair would also be prohibited.**

SO does not aim at zero intrusion. It asks where the difference generated by intrusion returns and whether that Return can alter the actor.

### 3.1 Separate contact, transmission, intrusion, and intended effect

A signal or utterance may reach B without creating the relevant difference being audited.

Let successful transmission be:

$$
Tr_{A\to B}(t)=1.
$$

Let an audit-relevant difference on B caused through A's action be:

$$
\Delta^{rel}_B[A\to B].
$$

Then:

$$
Tr_{A\to B}=1
\not\Rightarrow
I_{A\to B}\neq 0.
$$

Mere transmission does not guarantee an audit-relevant change in B's later conditions.

Conversely, for intrusion in the present technical sense:

$$
I_{A\to B}\neq0
\Rightarrow
\Delta^{rel}_B[A\to B]\neq\varnothing.
$$

This does not count every microscopic physical change as intrusion. The audit target, scope, and observation conditions must define which differences are relevant.

A further distinction remains between changing B and achieving A's intended effect. An action can change B while failing to produce intended persuasion, understanding, trust, compliance, or repair, and may generate an opposite difference.

> **Contact, successful transmission, actual change in the other side, and intended effect are four different things.**

## 4. "Violence" without privileging the physical

The operative definition is:

> **Violence is a persistent condition in which unilateral intrusion does not effectively return the difference it generated to the acting subject.**

First separate the existence of a Return path from its effectiveness.

$$
R_{B\to A}=1
$$

means there is some path or response surface through which B can return a difference to A.

That does not guarantee the difference reaches a place capable of reopening A's judgment or action conditions.

Define effective Return as:

$$
R^{\mathrm{eff}}_{B\to A}(\Delta^{rel}_B)=1,
$$

meaning the relevant difference reaches a position from which A's judgment, action rule, boundary condition, resource allocation, stopping authority, or revision authority can actually be re-examined.

Thus:

$$
R^{\mathrm{eff}}_{B\to A}=1
\Rightarrow
R_{B\to A}=1,
$$

but not conversely. A complaint desk, record, response form, or appeal process may exist while remaining disconnected from the responsibility boundary.

A minimal logical form is therefore:

$$
\boxed{
Violence_{A\to B}
:=
I_{A\to B}
\land
\neg R^{\mathrm{eff}}_{B\to A}
\left(
\Delta^{rel}_B[I_{A\to B}]
\right)
}
$$

What returns is not the intrusion itself but the relevant difference produced on B. Effective Return does not require A to comply with B's demand; it requires that the difference enter A's rejudgment space and be able, where warranted, to alter, stop, or revise action.

An intrusion with a functioning effective Return path is not necessarily "violence" in this technical sense.

### 4.1 Why retain a strong word?

The word is intentionally strong. If it is completely neutralized, cognitive, institutional, economic, translational, or classificatory action can be declared harmless merely because it is nonphysical.

The note therefore generalizes beyond physical violence while simultaneously limiting the term: not all intrusion is violence.

The point is to keep visible that nonphysical one-way action can also alter histories, boundaries, and later possibilities with destructive intensity.

## 5. Force as a counter-action that stops or returns one-way action

Ordinary language associates "force" strongly with physical or military power. The present note generalizes it structurally:

> **Force is a counter-action that stops, attenuates, blocks, or returns unilateral intrusion so the acting side becomes re-examinable.**

The audit-theory aim is not destruction of the other side's existence. The minimum purpose is either:

$$
I^{unilateral}_{A\to B} \rightarrow 0
$$

or

$$
R^{\mathrm{eff}}_{B\to A}:0\rightarrow1.
$$

An audit claim can be a non-destructive form of such counter-force in an epistemic domain:

```text
one-way epistemic action
        ->
audit claim
  what is the basis?
  what is the scope?
  through what transformation did it pass?
  is the same rule self-applied?
  can returned difference change the actor?
        ->
Return restored / action stopped / binding authority refused
```

The audit claim is itself an intrusion and must therefore remain auditable in basis, scope, method, and consequence. If counter-force refuses Return to itself, it can become violence under the same definition.

# Part III. Self-reference and null

## 6. Self-reference: return an acting principle to oneself

To avoid fixing asymmetry outward, a principle applied to another must be able to return to the acting system.

If A applies principle $p$ to B as $p(B)$ while refusing $p(A)$ in principle, the operation becomes one-way.

Self-reference is therefore not merely "being able to talk about oneself." It is:

> **the ability to reapply to oneself or one's own action the rules, classifications, evaluations, and boundary conditions used on others.**

Represent this as:

$$
SelfApply_A(p)=1.
$$

If SO demands of other systems "return to your basis," "do not exceed scope," "do not close unresolved issues," "receive objections," or "show transformation paths," those demands must be returnable to SO itself.

## 7. Self-reference failure is not a necessary condition of violence

Violence can exist without logical self-contradiction.

A system might axiomatically state "A may use B unilaterally" and apply that rule consistently. Formal self-consistency does not eliminate asymmetric load.

Therefore:

$$
SelfContradiction=0
$$

can coexist with

$$
Violence_{A\to B}=1.
$$

Audit must separate at least:

- **logical self-reference failure**;
- **ontological asymmetric load**.

Self-reference audit alone does not detect all unilateral intrusion.

## 8. Null: observe what action caused to disappear

Self-reference alone is insufficient. A system can apply the same rule to itself and still fail to observe what has been lost or made unreachable.

AMP can read null not only as absolute emptiness but as a state in which connection to history is severed. The correctness line further separates Expected Missing, Cut, Resource Boundary, Deliberate Seal, and related states.

For audit theory, null is:

> **a negative observation surface showing that a history, support, Return path, distinction, or response expected to be retained, connected, or returned is absent after action.**

With expected structure $E(v)$ and observed structure $O(v)$:

$$
N(v)=E(v)-O(v).
$$

$N(v)$ is not a physical quantity. It marks difference from expected connection.

Examples of the structure include a system that criticizes others while lacking a path for criticism back into itself; imports an external concept while dropping source-domain limitations; produces benefit while erasing the history of wear that supports it; or shifts future burden outside present decision surfaces.

## 9. Relation between self-reference and null

Self-reference and null are not independent supreme principles. They are two major audit surfaces for detecting and recovering asymmetry internally:

```text
asymmetry
   +-- self-reference
   |    can the rule applied outward return inward?
   |
   `-- null
        what disappeared, failed to arrive, or became unable to return through that action?
```

Self-reference without null can preserve formally consistent external harm. Null without self-reference can always assign missingness to the other side.

> **Self-reference returns the acting principle to the actor. Null returns the difference erased by the action to the actor.**

Both connect to Return/Reopening.

# Part IV. Deriving the need for audit from AMP

## 10. Persistence of existence and asymmetry

The word "persistence" must be handled cautiously.

Even if some $X$ passes from $t$ to $t+1$:

$$
X_t \longrightarrow X_{t+1},
$$

it does not follow that the same constituents, history, or boundary were retained. A system can preserve name, function, or outward form by destroying and replacing its components.

At minimum:

$$
Persist(System) \neq Persist(Constituents).
$$

> **Outward persistence of a system and persistence of the beings constituting that system are not the same.**

## 11. Asymmetry sustained through replenishment

Let $C$ be a core, $P$ a burden-bearing region, and $E$ a replenishment source. Load $L_t$ flows from $C$ to $P$. $P$ is worn down, but new carriers $G_t$ arrive from $E$:

$$
P_{t+1}=P_t-L_t+G_t.
$$

If

$$
G_t \ge L_t,
$$

the **role** $P$ can persist for a long time even though the beings occupying it do not.

The structure therefore persists not by ending destruction, but by replenishing what can be destroyed.

Audit must ask not merely whether the system has survived, but:

> **What is it consuming as replaceable carriers in order to survive?**

## 12. Existence-persistence audit

Persistence can be decomposed as:

```text
persistence
  +-- retention
  +-- repair
  +-- replacement
  +-- externalization
  `-- reproduction
```

### 12.1 Retention

The same beings, histories, and boundaries are retained to some degree.

### 12.2 Repair

Damage occurs, but Return enables repair while history remains connected.

### 12.3 Replacement

Function is maintained by replacing worn carriers.

### 12.4 Externalization

Residuals, wear, uncertainty, or responsibility are sent elsewhere to keep an internal order stable.

### 12.5 Reproduction

New burden-bearing carriers are continually generated or acquired so the same asymmetric relation can be reproduced.

### 12.6 Separate manifestation, generation, transmission/conversion, and responsibility boundaries

#### 12.6.1 Do not promote the manifestation point into the cause point

Where a phenomenon becomes visible need not be where its generative conditions arose.

Audit separates:

- **manifestation boundary**: where a difference, symptom, failure, loss, or objection becomes observable;
- **generation boundary**: where conditions producing that difference or load were established;
- **transmission/conversion path**: where the generated difference traveled, accumulated, amplified, attenuated, or changed form;
- **responsibility boundary**: where authority, obligation, or effective capacity to change, stop, or repair those conditions was located.

These can coincide, but must not be assumed identical. Responsibility boundary is not an instant accusation label; it traces changeability and where burden can be taken up.

#### 12.6.2 The canary in the mine

If a canary used as a warning system dies in a mine, it would be false closure to stop at "the canary had an individual illness."

The canary is the manifestation boundary. Upstream conditions generated or accumulated dangerous atmosphere: a generation boundary. Movement and concentration of the danger define a transmission/conversion path. Where authority and ability to detect, ventilate, evacuate, stop work, or otherwise change conditions existed defines a responsibility boundary.

High-resolution diagnosis of the canary and identification of mine-wide generative conditions are different tasks. Audit that refines only the manifestation boundary can lose the upstream generator.

#### 12.6.3 Causal false closure through personalization

A part breaking where stress appears does not prove that the defect originated there. A department concentrating failures is not necessarily the structural cause. Likewise, pain, symptoms, failure, or refusal manifesting in a person does not by itself close the generative cause inside that person.

A human-side research proposition is:

> **A person is not necessarily the source of a phenomenon. A phenomenon may pass through, accumulate in, transform within, and become visible through a person. Therefore do not close the cause boundary inside the person merely from where the result manifested.**

The reverse false closure is also prohibited. Finding a structural condition does not erase individual difference, individual action, bodily state, or local factors from the causal chain.

#### 12.6.4 Traceback and externalization

Audit is not required to choose "person" or "structure" in advance. It asks whether it can **trace back from manifestation to generative conditions, transmission path, changeability, and responsibility**.

Promoting manifestation into cause hides upstream conditions. If the Return that should reach the structure is pushed back onto the manifestation point, personalization itself can become externalization.

This yields a foundational question:

> **Whose persistence is constituted by whose non-persistence?**

### 12.7 Do not exclude the acting side from audit

The same four-way distinction returns when SO acts outward:

```text
theory / proposition
  ->
commensuration / interpretation
  ->
institutional design / implementation
  ->
operation
  ->
consequence manifests in humans / environment
```

A theory can contribute to a generation boundary, commensuration or制度 design can form transmission/conversion paths, implementers or decision surfaces can occupy responsibility boundaries, and humans/environment can become manifestation boundaries.

Conversely, manifestation of a bad result does not by itself prove "SO caused it"; the actual path must be traced.

Thus saying "it was misused" does not by itself exempt the acting theory. Audit asks whether implementation actually departed from theory, whether wording or commensuration invited misuse, whether stopping conditions were missing, whether the load was foreseeable, and whether foreseeable burden was externalized.

Return also means more than passively waiting for objections. As the strength of action rises, the responsibility to actively recover results, failures, burdens, refusals, exits, and residuals can also rise, and to return them upstream into judgment, implementation, and canon.

`Return != Assimilation` still applies: converting the affected side's difference into the theory's own vocabulary and thereby erasing the original objection is not successful Return.

SO itself remains inside this audit.

# Part V. Internal/external surfaces and entropy bands

## 13. Do not define inside and outside spatially alone

A member formally "inside" an institution can have no path through which wear or objection returns to decision. A physically external actor may have a strong path that changes the institution.

A provisional functional definition is:

> **The functional inside is the range from which differences generated by action can effectively return to the system's self-change.**

Let effective Return from subject $x$ to the decision surface of $S$ be:

$$
R^{\mathrm{eff}}_{x\to Decision(S)}.
$$

If that route is closed in principle, $x$ can be organizationally inside while remaining outside in terms of burden processing.

This motivates the idea of **return-path externalization**.

## 14. Low-H and High-H bands

AMP treats entropy-like direction as diffusion of history and increasing difficulty of reintegration. Related research treats differences as acquiring attributes such as diffusion, aggregation, forgetting, persistence, value, cost, externalization, and Return formation.

For audit, this note provisionally uses **Low-H band** and **High-H band** as structural working terms, not as thermodynamic entropy quantities.

### 14.1 Low-H band

Relatively predictable state, preserved history, repair resources, stable decision authority, ability to send exceptions or wear elsewhere, and an environment that can perceive its own order as order.

### 14.2 High-H band

Rapid state change, frequent replacement, fragile historical continuity, concentration of uncertainty/exception/accident/wear, preference for replacement over repair, and weak Return from that region to the whole-system decision surface.

### 14.3 Externalization of order

A core $C$ appearing low-H does not mean it produces order internally by itself. Residuals and burden may be pushed to $P$:

$$
Q_C\downarrow,
\qquad
Q_P\uparrow.
$$

A conceptual externalization flow is:

$$
J_Q(C\to P)>0.
$$

Audit must not look only at local order and assume that order is internally closed. It asks where uncertainty, wear, historical cut, and replacement cost flow in order to sustain the visible order.

# Part VI. Temporal externalization and generations

## 15. Future generations lack direct Return

Intergenerational action contains asymmetry different from contemporaneous interaction.

If present A acts on future B:

$$
A_t \to B_{t+n},
$$

B may not exist or have a voice at A's decision time. Therefore:

$$
R_{B_{t+n}\to A_t}=0
$$

can be nearly unavoidable as a temporal structure.

Future generations therefore have a strong Return deficit relative to present action.

## 16. Proxy Return

Where direct Return from the future is impossible, the present may need proxy Return structures such as records, long-range forecasting, legal constraints, trusts, future-impact assessment, prohibition rules, intergenerational representation, or conservation boundaries.

The note does not rank specific制度 designs. It retains the structural proposition:

> **Sending burden to a future that cannot answer and hiding that burden from the current decision surface creates strong audit asymmetry.**

## 17. Structures that use the future as replenishment

If a system wears down present burden bearers while assuming future bearers will continue to arrive, the future is integrated as replenishment source $E$.

The system may persist not because the same people survive, but because **the burden-bearing role keeps being reproduced**.

Audit asks:

> **Are future generations being presupposed as replacement material for a burden structure they could not choose?**

This is a detector question applicable to any intergenerational institution, not a conclusion about one economic or political order.

# Part VII. Reproduction of asymmetry

## 18. One act is not yet a制度 of violence

A one-time unilateral intrusion and a persistent violence structure are different. At institutional or civilizational scale, the issue is a loop:

```text
intrusion
  ->
wear
  ->
replacement / externalization
  ->
new carriers replenished
  ->
intrusion again
```

This is an **asymmetry reproduction loop**.

## 19. Minimal reproduction expression

With burden-bearer set $P_t$, wear $D_t$, and replenishment $G_t$:

$$
P_{t+1}=P_t-D_t+G_t.
$$

The role structure may appear stable as long as:

$$
G_t \ge D_t.
$$

Therefore never infer:

$$
Stable(Role) \Rightarrow Stable(Bearers).
$$

That is an important prohibited inference.

## 20. Replaceable burden bearer

When a being is treated not as a history-bearing individual but as a replaceable node for system function, the system prioritizes persistence of the role over persistence of the being.

The term **replaceable burden bearer** names the structural relation, not the essence of any person or group.

Audit asks whether the system preserves a group as history-bearing subjects or reproduces them as interchangeable carriers of a role.

## 21. Violence structures can look stable

Unilateral intrusion need not collapse quickly. A system that closes Return, externalizes burden, and replenishes worn carriers can convert local non-sustainability into global outward persistence.

Thus the proposition "violence is unsustainable, therefore it quickly disappears" is rejected.

A more careful statement is:

> **If unilateral intrusion does not integrate, return, or repair the load it produces, its persistence cost must be transported somewhere.**

Possible destinations include other groups, regions, species, environments, future generations, lower organizational layers, future repair costs, or unexplained residuals.

Audit searches for those destinations.

# Part VIII. Collapse, fragility, and non-teleology

## 22. Do not turn "the prosperous must decline" into a law

Closed Return can produce positive feedback:

```text
Return closure
  ->
error / residual accumulation
  ->
maintenance cost rises
  ->
further externalization / intrusion
  ->
further Return closure
```

Conceptually:

$$
I_t\uparrow
\Rightarrow Q_{ext,t}\uparrow
\Rightarrow Cost_{maint,t}\uparrow
\Rightarrow I_{t+1}\uparrow.
$$

This resembles historical intuitions about brittle dominance, but the note does not assert a universal law that flourishing systems must collapse.

A system may collapse, reform, enlarge its externalization range, transform into another institution, or acquire new replenishment sources.

The auditable target is therefore not inevitable decline but **the conditions under which brittleness increases and where that brittleness is externalized**.

## 23. The world does not punish

The note does not need a world with moral purpose that punishes unsustainable structures.

A weaker proposition is sufficient:

> **A structure that cannot meet the conditions required to carry itself into later states will not reproduce itself indefinitely in the same form.**

But externalization, replacement, and replenishment can import those required conditions from elsewhere, allowing long persistence.

Therefore post hoc statements such as "it collapsed, so it was unsustainable" are circular by themselves.

## 24. What to observe before collapse

Candidate pre-collapse detectors include decreasing Return paths, failed self-application, increasing Expected Missing, rising residuals, rising maintenance cost, increasing turnover of burden bearers, increasing externalization flow $J_Q$, proliferation of exceptions and auxiliary rules, replacement preferred over internal repair, growing dependence on new replenishment sources, and more operations that neutralize counterexamples or objections.

These are detector candidates, not universal empirical laws.

# Part IX. Seven propositions supporting the need for audit

## Proposition 1. Contact entails action; perfectly non-intrusive cognition is not required

Cognition, classification, translation, criticism, explanation, and institutional processing all act at boundaries. The presence of intrusion alone does not invalidate an action.

SO asks for **retention of intrusion history and Return paths**, not absence of all intrusion.

## Proposition 2. If action is allowed only in one direction, asymmetry between subject and object becomes fixed

The issue is not every difference in role or authority. It is a structure in which A alone is preserved as acting subject while B alone is preserved as object or replaceable burden bearer.

Externalization and replenishment can institutionalize that asymmetry.

## Proposition 3. Self-reference and recognition of null are major internal audit surfaces for detecting asymmetry

Self-reference returns outward rules inward. Null observes differences erased, cut, or made invisible through action.

But formally self-consistent violence remains possible, so load flow, replacement, externalization, and reproduction require separate audit.

## Proposition 4. Intrusion without effective Return must transport its persistence cost elsewhere

If consequences do not return for integration or repair, they need not disappear. They can remain as residual, wear, replacement cost, or future burden.

The issue is therefore not only whether the actor contradicts itself, but who carries the cost of the actor's persistence.

## Proposition 5. SO need not accept binding one-way action and may demand comparable auditability from the actor

When another system classifies, criticizes, denies, explains, or constrains SO, SO can issue an **audit claim** asking for basis, transformation, scope, unresolved points, load, externalization, self-application conditions, and Return.

The "force" in this note names the counter-action that stops one-way authority and restores a re-examinable surface.

## Proposition 6. SO need not erase a system that rejects audit, but it need not accept that system's one-way authority

A system may define a boundary such as "we do not accept external audit." SO does not need to prohibit its existence.

But if the same system exercises one-way classification or authority over SO while exempting the corresponding action surface from audit without explanation, SO need not accept the claim as binding knowledge inside its own boundary.

This is not a universal revocation of epistemic status. It is a reception condition at the SO boundary.

## Proposition 7. The same audit principle applies to SO

If SO audits others but refuses equivalent audit of itself, it reproduces the asymmetry it problematizes.

SO must remain able, in principle, to have its basis questioned, transformations traced, scope limited, externalization identified, burden-bearer relations examined, null/cuts/self-reference failures pointed out, audit claims audited, and its current state reopened by the Return.

> **SO can claim a right to audit only insofar as it remains itself auditable and the resulting audit can effectively reach its own conditions of action.**

# Part X. Knowledge systems in general

## 25. Knowledge acts on the world

A knowledge system does not merely mirror the world. It cuts what counts as fact, importance, measurement, noise, explanation target, or exception.

Knowledge is therefore boundary action. Let a theory $T$ act on target world $W$ as:

$$
I_{T\to W}.
$$

The issue is not that theory acts, but whether Return from the world can alter the theory.

## 26. Read falsifiability as a Return path

If observations, counterexamples, replication failures, or criticism can return from $W$ to $T$:

$$
R_{W\to T}>0.
$$

If those differences can actually reopen $T$ and alter proposition, scope, support, or structure:

$$
R^{\mathrm{eff}}_{W\to T}>0.
$$

The second condition captures effective Return. A theory with a nominal response channel but no possibility of modification has a weak Return path.

Popperian falsifiability may therefore be reread from SO as:

> **preservation of a path through which an external difference capable of breaking the knowledge system can return into it.**

This is not a historical claim about Popper's philosophical foundations. It is an SO-side metaphysical commensuration:

$$
Falsifiability \approx PreservedEffectiveReturnPath.
$$

## 27. Theoretical immunization as suppression of Return

If every counterexample is neutralized by auxiliary hypotheses, exception routing, observer disqualification, moving definitions, or principled invalidation of counterevidence in ways that leave $T$ unchanged, Return is suppressed.

Conceptually:

$$
Immunization \approx ReturnPathSuppression.
$$

Adding auxiliary hypotheses is not itself wrong. If the added hypothesis is independently testable and can reopen the whole theory, Return may remain healthy. The pathology is using added structure only to make theory unchangeable.

## 28. Knowledge systems also have reproduction loops

A knowledge system can preserve its external form by sending burden outward:

```text
counterexample
  -> marginalization
  -> auxiliary concept added
  -> core unchanged
  -> new supporters / cases acquired
  -> new counterexample
```

This is a general detector model, not an accusation toward a specific school or discipline. Audit asks where counterexamples and unresolved issues go, what can alter the core, whether new supporters or cases substitute for revision, and whether local repair proliferates indefinitely.

# Part XI. External reference and commensuration

## 29. External reference is not the highest principle

SO does not cite external systems primarily to receive certification from authority, nor to claim complete reproduction of an original author's meaning.

External reference matters because SO imports facts, concepts, and interpretations into its own system and acts on them.

At minimum preserve:

```text
source statement / external fact
        ->
SO reading
        ->
transformation / abstraction / commensuration
        ->
SO integration
        ->
SO-side claim
```

Audit asks what the source actually supplied, how SO read it, where it transformed it, which claim SO now owns, and whether SO has projected its own conclusion back into the source system.

## 30. What to show instead of "we did not misread it"

Rather than claim ownership of an external system as a whole, show:

> **This is the statement or interpretation submitted by the source; this is how SO read it; this transformation was applied; and this is where the result was integrated.**

Then if an external Return says the source understanding is wrong, scope was lost, a transformation erased an important distinction, or integration does not follow, the return point is visible.

External reference is not the highest audit principle, but it is an important boundary where self-reference, null, Return, and asymmetry are tested concretely.

## 30.1 Source-domain definitional authority and receiving-system reconstructive authority

Two kinds of authority must be separated.

**Source-domain definitional authority** means that disciplines such as science, mathematics, law, or history retain authority over how a term is defined and tested inside their own domain. If SO borrows terms such as `entanglement` or a Feynman diagram, it must not claim ownership of the scientific definition, reverse-prove a scientific claim from an SO analogy, or return an SO-specific use as if it were standard science.

**Receiving-system reconstructive authority** means that respecting the source definition does not prohibit SO from repositioning a public concept into a different structural question while preserving provenance.

Thus:

$$
SOUse(x) \nvdash ScientificClaim(x),
$$

and also:

$$
\neg ScientificUse(x) \nvdash Meaningless(x).
$$

The first says that SO use does not make a scientific claim. The second says that exclusion from scientific definition or verification does not by itself prove a structural reuse meaningless.

## 30.2 Interference zone: a contact surface not fully recoverable into either side

When systems A and B contact one another, agreement is not necessarily the goal.

If A borrows B's term, B objects, A responds, and disagreement remains, collapsing the whole history into either A or B loses the contact event.

The note therefore places a provisional **interference zone** on the boundary:

$$
Z_{AB}
=
\{Claim_A,Interpretation_B,Objection_B,Response_A,Residual_{AB},ReturnPaths,Refusal\}.
$$

$Z_{AB}$ is neither A nor B. It is a third historical surface generated by contact.

Retain which term or claim made contact, what the source system objected to, what the receiving system accepted or retained, where agreement stopped, what residual remains, and whether either side paused or refused engagement.

Agreement is not required for the contact history to matter.

## 30.3 Return is not Assimilation

When an objection returns, SO must receive it. Receiving it is not the same as absorbing it completely into SO vocabulary:

$$
Return \neq Assimilation.
$$

If science says "that term is not used that way in this scientific field" and SO responds only "this is a boundary reaction in SO," the objection may simply have been captured.

Return therefore requires, where possible:

1. retaining the other side's wording and reasoning in its own vocabulary;
2. recording SO's reading separately;
3. retaining disagreement as Residual;
4. recording which SO support, scope, or term choice the Residual reopens.

This is a central boundary against SO Capture.

## 30.4 Reciprocal contestability

If SO says something about another system, it does not in principle exclude that system from requesting re-collation of the action surface. Likewise, when another system classifies or limits SO, SO can question basis, scope, transformation, and self-application of that action.

This state is called **reciprocal contestability**.

It is not an obligation to answer forever or reach agreement. A party may respond, respond partially, hold, refuse, or exit for reasons of scope, secrecy, resources, or safety.

> **When engagement ends, record the point as a boundary rather than converting refusal into defeat.**

A system that exercises epistemic authority outward while making the corresponding surface in principle unauditable may nevertheless lose binding authority inside SO's own reception boundary if it cannot account for that asymmetry.

## 30.5 Preserve an interference log instead of win/loss

A minimal log can retain:

```text
Interference Log

contact:
  term / claim / diagram at which contact occurred

source-domain meaning:
  meaning and scope retained in the source domain

receiving-system use:
  what SO retained and what it transformed

objection:
  what the other side objected to

response:
  what SO accepted and what it maintained

shared:
  agreed portion

residual:
  unresolved difference

return:
  which support / scope / vocabulary choice was reopened

engagement-status:
  continuing / paused / declined / closed-by-boundary
```

`declined` is not refutation or defeat. It records that the current contact path is open only that far.

A strong research hypothesis is that commensuration may be understood partly as:

$$
Commensuration \approx TraceableInterference,
$$

without reducing the whole concept of commensuration to that formula.

## 30.6 Boundary model using contact with science

If SO borrows a scientific term or diagram and science returns "that use is outside this field's definition or verification form," that can be a legitimate source-domain boundary response.

SO must clarify whether it claims identity, structural borrowing, correspondence hypothesis, or metaphor.

Conversely, moving from "science does not use the term this way" to "therefore this structural use is meaningless" does not follow automatically. SO may ask from which rule that additional judgment follows.

Science can close its boundary with "we do not treat that beyond the point of scientific testability." SO can respond "we will continue the SO-internal structural use while clearly labeling it non-scientific."

Neither side needs to subsume the other. The visible difference between the source definition boundary and receiving-system reconstruction boundary can itself be retained as an outcome.

## 30.7 From low-resolution proposal to high-resolution reconstruction

SO does not assume it can enter every target domain at the target domain's own resolution from the outset.

Initial contact may use a **low-resolution probe** whose claim strength and boundaries are explicit. Low resolution does not mean careless. It must state what distinctions have not yet been made, which terms are borrowed metaphorically, where the target domain must take over, and which facts SO does not yet own.

Conceptually:

$$
P_0^{\phi_0}
\rightarrow Contact(D)
\rightarrow Return_D
\rightarrow P_1^{\phi_1}.
$$

After Return, SO incorporates facts, definitions, counterexamples, scope, and methodological constraints from domain $D$ and reconstructs the proposal at higher resolution.

High resolution is not agreement:

$$
HighResolution \neq Assimilation.
$$

It means preserving the distinctions and constraints returned by the other domain with enough detail that SO can reconstruct them internally without flattening them.

## 30.8 Separate rebuttal construction from immunization

A theory need not revise its root every time an objection arrives. It can first test whether current theoretical paths already answer the objection.

Let available paths for proposition $p$ be:

$$
\mathcal{R}_{SO}(p).
$$

For high-resolution objection $o$, existing paths are used first to determine whether SO can rebut it, limit scope, withdraw part of the claim, or retain residual disagreement.

Using existing paths is not immunization by itself; it localizes where explanation succeeds and fails.

But if a new auxiliary path $r_{new}$ is added for the rebuttal:

$$
r_{new}\notin\mathcal{R}_{SO}^{old}(p),
$$

that path is a new theoretical commit and must not be retrospectively counted as old explanatory capacity.

Where existing and explicitly added paths still cannot reconstruct the external constraint at adequate resolution, record a candidate **path exhaustion** point.

A residual after path exhaustion is not merely "something not yet considered"; it is a difference that remains after the system has used the paths it claims to possess, and therefore approaches root-revision status.

## 30.9 Audit fact, theory, and residual with phase

Do not force a target into a phase-less fixed label.

The same $x$ has different audit meaning as an externally supplied fact candidate, SO interpretation, proposition under adjudication, challenged proposition, scope-limited reconstructed proposition, residual after path exhaustion, or root-revision candidate.

Represent provisionally:

$$
Finding(x,\phi_i).
$$

Thus:

$$
Finding(x,\phi_i) \neq Finding(x,\phi_j)
$$

may hold.

This is not relativization of fact. It preserves how the same factual constraint is connected to interpretation, proposition, residual, and revision paths.

## 30.10 Lossless single-phase compression is a root falsification candidate for SO

If SO introduces phases, those phases must add real adjudication or audit capability.

Compare phase-aware model $M_{\Phi}$ with single-phase model $M_1$.

If for SO's core target class $M_1$ can preserve the same factual constraints, local adjudications, responses to objection, residual detection, root-revision points, Return/Reopen decisions, and do so at equal or lower resource cost, then phase structure has no explanatory increment.

$$
LosslessQuotient(M_{\Phi}\rightarrow M_1)=1
$$

systematically across core targets would make multiphase structure a candidate for root revision or withdrawal.

Simple tasks being solvable in one phase is not a problem. The falsification pressure applies where phase distinctions make no substantive difference to residual discovery, Return, rebuttal, or root revision.

# Part XII. Connection to history, institutions, and economics

## 31. Separate broad structural reading from replacement of specialist disciplines

The audit structure may look applicable to history, economics, politics, organizations, families, education, and institutions of science. Applicability does not grant empirical ownership of those domains.

SO cannot replace source criticism in history, models/statistics in economics, or institutional analysis in political science.

Strong statements about human history or specific economic orders therefore cannot be derived directly as facts from this note. They require external-domain evidence.

## 32. What SO can bring into those domains

SO can first bring detector questions:

- Whose stability depended on whose wear?
- Where was burden externalized?
- Were burden bearers repaired or replaced?
- Could resistance and objection return to decision?
- Was load sent into the future?
- Did proxy Return exist for the future side?
- Which High-H band supported a core's apparent Low-H state?
- How were burden-bearing roles reproduced?
- Did outward persistence of the system match persistence of its constituents?

These questions are then taken to records, data, and source-domain theory. Results return to SO as pressure on structural hypotheses, not as substitute conclusions for history or economics.

## 33. How to read resistance

Resistance from the acted-upon side is not necessarily noise. It may be an attempt to create a Return path where the ordinary route is closed.

But the note does not say all resistance is correct or sustainable. Resistance is also intrusive and can itself become violence.

The same audit returns to the resisting side:

> **What is being stopped? What is being protected? Which Return path is being created? Is the counter-action creating a new one-way structure?**

This keeps the audit claim from becoming an unexamined weapon of righteousness.

# Part XIII. Logical and mathematical formalization candidates

## 34. Minimal notation

There is no established common quantitative scale yet, so the note prioritizes logical expressions and state transitions over pseudo-precision.

### 34.1 Transmission, intrusion, and B-side difference

$$
Tr_{A\to B}=1
$$

means an utterance, signal, classification, instruction, or similar item reached B.

But:

$$
Tr_{A\to B}=1
\not\Rightarrow
I_{A\to B}\neq0.
$$

For intrusion, require an audit-relevant change:

$$
I_{A\to B}\neq0
\Rightarrow
\Delta^{rel}_B[A\to B]\neq\varnothing.
$$

### 34.2 Existence of Return versus effective Return

$$
R_{B\to A}=1
$$

means a path exists. Effective Return is:

$$
R^{\mathrm{eff}}_{B\to A}(\Delta^{rel}_B)=1,
$$

meaning the difference reaches a position capable of reopening A's judgment or action conditions.

Thus effective Return implies a Return path, but a Return path does not guarantee effectiveness.

### 34.3 Violence

$$
\boxed{
Violence_{A\to B}
=
I_{A\to B}
\land
\neg R^{\mathrm{eff}}_{B\to A}
\left(
\Delta^{rel}_B[I_{A\to B}]
\right)
}
$$

The temporal condition that the structure persists is conceptually part of the definition but is not yet encoded in this minimal expression.

### 34.4 Self-application

$$
SelfApply_A(p)=1.
$$

### 34.5 Null / Expected Missing

$$
N(v)=Expected(v)-Observed(v).
$$

### 34.6 Externalization flow

$$
J_Q(A\to E)>0.
$$

### 34.7 Reproduction of burden bearers

$$
P_{t+1}=P_t-D_t+G_t.
$$

## 35. Candidate formalization of force

Let audit-theory counter-force be:

$$
F^{def}_{B\to A}:

\rightarrow

An audit claim can be written:

```text

did an audit-relevant difference arise on B?
```

A formula such as

C_{B_t}^{(k)}(S_t)

# Part XVI. Residuals, falsification candidates, unresolved points

## 41. Can the problem of asymmetry really be derived from AMP?

Public AMP contains difference retention, history, communication, null, and entropy-like structures, but propositions such as "sustainable existence requires Return" or "unilateral intrusion must externalize its persistence cost" are not simply explicit public AMP statements.

They remain human-side working hypotheses under derivation and require separate comparison with AMP Core, counterexamples, and limiting conditions.

## 42. Complete symmetry is not required

The theory does not idealize complete symmetry. Legitimate asymmetries of parenting, medicine, education, justice, safety, confidentiality, and time would be destroyed by such a demand.

The more plausible requirement is not role identity, but auditability of the reason, burden, stopping condition, objection path, and responsibility where action is one-way.

## 43. A Return path is not enough

A formal channel for comment may exist while decision never changes. Return may need to be separated into ingress, transmission, consideration, and possible modification.

A feedback form is not equivalent to effective Return.

## 44. If externalization is part of the larger system, what counts as outside?

A burden externalized from a company to a region may still lie inside a national or planetary system. "Outside" should therefore be defined functionally relative to the decision surface being audited, not as absolute space. The inside/outside cut changes with scale.

## 45. A violence structure can persist for a very long time

With sufficient replenishment, unilateral systems can persist. Violence must not be identified with short-term instability.

SO must identify what that long persistence consumes, where it sends wear, and what it must reproduce.

## 46. Is persistence good?

Persistence is descriptive here, not ethical. Long-lived systems are not automatically good and short-lived systems bad. Ethical evaluation requires separate contact with Tam/Goodness, boundary ethics, responsibility, consent, and history preservation.

## 47. Danger of entropy vocabulary

High-H / Low-H are working terms for uncertainty, wear, diffusion of history, and replaceability. No quantitative identity with thermodynamic or information-theoretic entropy is claimed. More neutral terminology may eventually be preferable.

## 48. How publicly usable are the words "violence" and "force"?

Their generalization preserves intuitive pressure but invites legal, political, and military misreading.

Public use should keep operational definitions adjacent, explicitly deny automatic physical/military justification, avoid concrete coercive recommendations, and foreground non-destructive implementations such as audit claims, refusal, exit, and boundary defense.

## 49. Is SO producing burden bearers of its own?

More rigorous audit can require more confirmation, records, explanation, and human review. If humans become burden bearers sustaining SO's auditability, the theory reproduces the problem it describes.

> **Increasing auditability must not be equated with increasing audit labor.**

This is especially important for AI-assisted design.

## 49.1 Who owns the interference zone?

If SO alone stores an interference zone inside its repository, it may monopolize the right to edit, summarize, and classify the other side's objection.

Future implementation should separate raw objection, SO summary, SO interpretation, and unresolved residual. Where the other side cannot verify the record, label it as an **SO-side record of external objection** rather than the objection itself.

## 49.2 Reciprocal contestability includes a right to exit

Reciprocal contestability does not give a right to compel endless dialogue. A party may answer, limit its answer, hold, refuse, or exit. SO may record the structural fact of that choice but does not thereby gain authority to infer motive or personality from refusal.

## 49.3 Authority boundary around borrowed terms

Source-domain authority over a term inside its own domain is different from control of all secondary reuse. Conversely, receiving-system reconstructive freedom is different from freedom to overwrite the source meaning.

The general rule needs further work with external norms of citation, intellectual property, and specialist terminology.

## 49.4 SO's Persistence Bias

SO strongly values persistence, history retention, Return, Reopening, and branch preservation. This is an important theoretical individuality and should be visible.

But three things must be separated:

1. **AMP persistence hypothesis**: a generative hypothesis that continued structures require persistence conditions.
2. **SO methodological persistence preference**: a design preference to preserve history, objection, Return, and reopenability where useful.
3. **ethical claim that persistence is good**: not derivable automatically from the first two.

Some processes should end; some hypotheses should be discarded; some branches cost too much to preserve; some information should be forgotten or compressed; some persistence depends on wearing others down.

SO must therefore ask itself:

> **Are we preserving this because it genuinely increases future possibility, or because SO itself fears non-persistence?**

Possibility preservation also has cost:

$$
CostOfPreservation>0.
$$

An excessive Persistence Bias can itself become a pseudo-lock that consumes exploration resources.

## 57.5 Exploration-resource / Lock-health detector

Even where a target looks stable, ask whether maintaining that stability continuously drains search, audit, or repair resources.

Observe whether the same exceptions and local patches recur, tracking/monitoring costs rise, resource input reduces residuals, Return/Reopening improves, Lock maintenance reduces reconstruction capacity, or obtaining reconstruction resources creates new externalization.

High cost is not automatically pathology. Difficult targets can legitimately be expensive. The question is whether cost is converting into residual localization, improved Return, and increased reconstructability.

# Part XVIII. Returning to ordinary human life

## 58. What it means for the result of action to return

People change their surroundings whenever they speak, teach, decide, classify, command, help, criticize, design institutions, or write theories. Living with others does not permit a fantasy of never affecting anyone.

The issue comes after the action.

Suppose someone is harmed, refuses, breaks down, behaves unexpectedly, or presents something the actor cannot explain. If that consequence can return far enough to make the actor ask whether the method itself should change, there is still a loop.

Receiving Return does not require obeying the other side. Re-examination may still produce the same decision. What matters is that what happened to the other side was not discarded as structurally irrelevant.

The opposite case is common: the actor can change the other side, but the consequences cannot return to a place that can change the actor.

There may be a complaint channel that never reaches the decision-maker. Failures may repeat in practice while policy treats them as exceptions. People may burn out and leave while replacement makes the organization look stable. A problem manifesting in one person may be personalized without tracing the conditions that generated it. A fact inconsistent with a theory may be routed to "exception" so the theory need not reopen.

The surfaces differ, but the structure is similar:

> **Action reaches the other side, while the difference generated by that action does not return far enough to change the actor's decision.**

Such systems can persist for a long time by replacing worn people, importing resources, routing problems elsewhere, shifting costs to the future, or calling unexplained cases exceptions.

Longevity alone therefore does not establish that a system works well.

Ask instead:

> **What returns? Does it reach a place where decisions can actually be remade? What cannot return, where does it accumulate, and who carries it?**

And the place where a problem becomes visible is not necessarily the place that generated it. A symptom appearing in a person does not imply that the whole cause lies in that person. An accident in a frontline unit does not imply that only the frontline should be examined.

Where did the result manifest? What generated it? Through what path did it travel? Who could stop or change it?

Without that traceback, a system can repair only the visible point and reproduce the same condition.

The ordinary human version of audit is therefore:

> **Do not exempt yourself from the results of the actions you send outward.**

It does not mean accepting every demand or giving the other side final authority. It means returning what happened, refusing to locate cause only where manifestation occurred, tracing upstream where necessary, and preserving a state in which one's own action can be decided again.

Find where the loop broke; reconnect it where appropriate.

# Part XIX. Final compression

## 59. Asymmetry

> **Asymmetry is not the enemy of existence. Difference and boundary make asymmetry part of the conditions under which distinguishable existence forms. The audit question is what the asymmetry retains, wears down, replaces, and externalizes in order to persist.**

## 60. Intrusion

> **Intrusion is action through contact that changes another side's later conditions. Denying intrusion altogether would deny observation, learning, criticism, and repair as well.**

## 61. Violence

> **Violence, in the operational sense of this note, is not limited to physical force. It is a persistent structure of unilateral intrusion in which the relevant difference produced on the affected side does not effectively return to the actor's judgment, stopping, or revision surface. A nominal Return channel is not enough.**

## 62. Force

> **Force is a counter-action that stops, attenuates, blocks, or returns unilateral intrusion so the acting side becomes re-examinable. An audit claim is one non-destructive form of such counter-action in an epistemic domain.**

## 63. Self-reference

> **Self-reference is the ability to return to oneself the rules one applies outward. Formally self-consistent one-way load can still exist, so self-reference audit alone is not a complete violence audit.**

## 64. Null

> **Null is a negative observation surface appearing when history, support, Return, or distinction expected to be retained or returned is absent after action.**

## 65. Persistence

> **Persistence of a system is not persistence of its constituents. Systems can continue by replacing damaged carriers, externalizing burden, and transferring cost into the future.**

## 66. Reproduction

> **Unilateral intrusion becomes a persistent制度 when intrusion, wear, replacement, and replenishment reproduce as a loop. Audit examines the reproduction mechanism, not only one act.**

## 67. Audit

> **Audit is not merely error hunting. It returns the acting side to a position where differences returned from the world it acted upon can change it again.**

## 68. Knowledge

> **If knowledge makes claims about the world, it must be able to reopen through Return from the world. Falsifiability may be reread from SO as one strong empirical-scientific implementation of that Return structure.**

## 68.1 Commensuration

> **Commensuration is not averaging different systems into one. It preserves traceability among source meaning, receiving-system transformation, objection, response, and unresolved difference.**

## 68.2 Interference zone

> **An interference zone is a third historical surface generated through contact between different systems and fully recoverable into neither. It preserves traceability of contact, objection, response, refusal, and residual rather than forcing agreement.**

## 68.3 Reciprocal contestability

> **A system that acts epistemically on another does not in principle exclude re-collation of that action surface. Reciprocal contestability is not an obligation of infinite response; refusal, hold, and exit remain legitimate boundary states.**

## 68.4 Detector and judgment

> **A detector is not a judge. It exposes a difference and hands the finding to adjudication as a defeater candidate. `Finding != Verdict`.**

## 68.5 Manifestation and generation boundaries

> **Do not automatically promote where a phenomenon appears into where it was generated or who bears responsibility. Separate manifestation, generation, transmission/conversion, and responsibility boundaries and preserve traceback.**

## 68.6 Adjudication

> **Adjudication asks whether a proposition is locally supportable under current phase, factual constraints, support, scope, and defeaters. Successful local adjudication does not imply that the surrounding document has passed audit.**

## 68.7 Phase-aware audit

> **Contact a low-resolution proposal with the outside, reconstruct it at higher resolution through Return, test whether existing paths can rebuild it, and record paths used, paths added, and residual difference with phase. Residuals that remain after path exhaustion approach root-revision status.**

## 68.8 Single-phase falsifier

> **If removing phase distinctions still permits the same adjudication, residual discovery, rebuttal, Return, and root revision at equal or lower resource cost, SO's phase structure loses explanatory increment.**

## 69. Final sentence

> **Audit in Scientific Ontology does not mean eliminating asymmetry. It means finding whether someone's order is being supported one-way by someone else's null, wear, replacement, silence, or future; returning that difference to the acting side; and reopening the structure toward a form that can still persist after the Return.**
