## 0. Position of This Note

This research note reconstructs α audit and β audit in Scientific Ontology in light of the current research conversation, `第七章　正しさを、実際に壊しに行く`, the AMP/ITS line of logical depth, `Godel_Frontier.md`, and experimental results from the Dual-Closure / Structural Commensuration line.

It does not rewrite the current canonical definition of audit.

In particular, the terms “completeness,” “incompleteness,” “logical plane,” and “Gödel” as used here include research vocabulary internal to Scientific Ontology. They must not be identified without qualification with the mathematical claims of Gödel's completeness theorem or incompleteness theorems in standard mathematical logic.

---

## 1. Logical Plane

At a given logical depth $d$, let the current set of propositions, definitions, inference rules, support relations, scopes, and unresolved differences that are handled together as one system be called, provisionally, the logical plane $L_d$.

$$
L_d = \{P_d,\Pi_d,S_d,Q_d,B_d\}
$$

where:

- $P_d$: propositions, definitions, and relations handled within the plane;
- $\Pi_d$: inference, transformation, and search rules permitted within the plane;
- $S_d$: support, establishment conditions, and scope of application;
- $Q_d$: unresolved differences, residuals, and undetermined states;
- $B_d$: search resources currently available.

This plane is not “the World.”

It is the range that the present system can treat as one plane under its present cognition, rules, history, and resources.

---

## 2. α Audit — Validity within the Logical Plane

The object of α audit is the interior of the currently posited logical plane $L_d$.

At minimum, α asks:

1. Does each proposition hold under the support and rules adopted on that plane?
2. When propositions are retained simultaneously, is a known contradiction being hidden?
3. Is there an Unsupported Join between grounds and conclusion?
4. Does word meaning, scope, or object change without marking along the way?
5. Has an undetermined state been closed as though already determined?
6. Were defeaters constructible within the present plane actually searched for?

Accordingly, α attempts to break the following claim:

> **Does this logical plane actually hold together as the plane it is currently being treated as?**

In this note, “completeness” is used only as a working term for roughly:

> a state in which individual propositions hold on the plane under its own conditions and the system, when taken together, is not falsely closed while retaining a known contradiction.

This usage is not intended to redefine `completeness` as a technical term of standard mathematical logic.

---

## 3. β Audit — Following the Generation of a Logical Plane along Logical Depth

β audit does not directly adjudicate the internal validity of the current plane $L_d$.

It examines the generative transition:

$$
L_{d-1} \longrightarrow L_d
$$

Here, $d$ is not Chronos-like time.

It is:

> **an axis of logical depth indicating how far one must return toward the generative conditions that make the present logical plane possible.**

A theory written ten years ago is therefore not necessarily shallower than a theory written today.

Even descriptions formed on the same day may stand in a relation such as:

$$
L_0 \prec L_1 \prec L_2
$$

β asks, for example:

- What held on the previous plane?
- What did not hold there?
- What remained undetermined, unexpressed, or unreachable?
- What search pressure did that residual generate?
- Which search instrument, external contact, or other individuality opened a new direction?
- What was added or changed so that the new plane became possible?
- What became visible through the extension?
- Conversely, what disappeared, was compressed, became presupposed, or was externalized?
- What newly became undetermined on the new plane?

β therefore audits:

> **Why did the present logical plane become necessary in this form?**

## 4. Logical Depth as Intrinsic Time Rather Than Mere “History”

The history examined by β is not simply version history or date order.

What matters is the intrinsic ordering:

```text
previous plane
  ↓
residual that could not be processed on that plane
  ↓
search pressure
  ↓
new distinction / relation / representation / axis / Path
  ↓
new plane
```

An ITS trial describes `logical time` as “depth rather than order.” This note does not adopt the associated physical or cosmological claim. It uses the idea only as an operational concept for reading the formation history of logical planes.

Traceback in β does not simply mean searching for older documents.

It means:

> **returning to a plane on which the present distinction did not yet exist, and finding the unclosed difference that made that distinction necessary.**

## 5. Incompleteness May Become Extension Pressure Rather Than “Error”

`Godel_Frontier.md` uses the metaphor of a “Prime Axiom” for a new basis that cannot be reached through combinations within existing logic, and presents a generative image of saturation of known logical space, pressure toward a boundary, and Lock of a new axiom.

This is not treated here as a cosmology directly derived from the theorems of standard mathematical logic.

As a research heuristic for Scientific Ontology, however, the following configuration is useful:

```text
propositions that hold on the present plane
  ↓
search within the plane reaches saturation
  ↓
undetermined / unexpressed / residual remains
  ↓
frontier unreachable with the present axioms, axes, or representation
  ↓
introduction of a new logical basis or representational direction
  ↓
new logical plane
```

Here, incompleteness does not mean that the present plane is “wrong.”

The plane may be sufficiently coherent internally while still leaving something that cannot be closed on that plane alone.

β traces whether that residual genuinely required the next plane.

## 6. Transformation of Dimensions and Axes

Generation of a new logical plane is not necessarily simple inclusion:

$$
L_d \subset L_{d+1}
$$

Transition to a new plane may involve at least the following transformations.

### 6.1 Axis Addition

Add a direction of discrimination that did not exist on the earlier plane.

### 6.2 Axis Extension

Extend the reachable range, resolution, or scope of application of an existing axis.

### 6.3 Axis Split

Separate what had been treated as one axis into multiple independent differences.

### 6.4 Axis Merge

Combine previously separate axes through a deeper common generative relation.

### 6.5 Folding / Projection

Fold higher-dimensional differences into a lower-dimensional representation needed for present operation.

### 6.6 Suppression / Dormancy

Leave something unused on the present plane without deleting it, keeping it dormant.

### 6.7 Erasure / Cut

A distinction, return path, or relation that existed on an earlier plane becomes unreconstructable from a later plane.

### 6.8 Externalization

A burden, residual, or condition previously handled internally is pushed outside.

β examines the entire transformation, not merely “what was added.”

## 7. Do Not Reify Axis Too Strongly

In the Structural Commensuration line, Axis was treated as a first-class object through v0.3, but the center of the model shifted toward **Persistent Path** in v0.4.

Given that correction, the “axis” seen by β need not be a fixed coordinate that existed in advance.

Among multiple Persistent Paths, Loops, Branches, and Cross-constraints, the structure currently activated may appear observationally as an Axis.

β should therefore not close with the description:

> “an axis increased.”

It should return to:

> **Through which Path formation, Branch reactivation, or relational transformation did that direction become observable?**

## 8. Fractal Audit — Look at Dimensional Transformation, Not Only Scale

Fractal audit should not be limited to the simple image of the same shape repeating at different sizes.

The relevant question here is what happens when a logical plane is examined at different granularities, depths, and axes:

- Which generative types persist?
- Which Paths survive?
- Which Loops close?
- Which Branches reactivate at another depth?
- Which Return structures are preserved?
- Where do local repairs proliferate?
- Where does downstream explanation increase without returning upstream?
- Which axis disappeared as the result of extending another axis?

Fractality is therefore treated as:

> **a heuristic for reading the persistence or collapse of generative signatures across different logical depths and dimensional transformations.**

Even if a mathematical fractal dimension is measured, that measurement must not by itself be promoted into an index of structural health or truth.

## 9. Finite Resources and Stability

β must not continue descending through logical depth without limit.

Search resources in Scientific Ontology are finite.

If:

$$
B_d < \infty
$$

then it is impossible to search every axis, every past plane, and every external World at the same time.

Stability is therefore necessary.

But:

$$
Stability \neq Correctness
$$

Here, stability means:

> **a state in which, under the present resources, search instruments, and formation history, the current logical plane can be provisionally maintained and used for the next action.**

It must remain reopenable when a new difference arrives.

β audit itself therefore requires both stopping conditions and reopening conditions.

## 10. α and β Are Not Two Independent Checkers

Under the current hypothesis, α and β are not simply two parallel inspection engines. They address different topological positions in logical formation.

Conceptually:

```text
β
examine how an unclosed difference or incompleteness on an earlier plane
generated a new plane
  ↓
α
attempt to break validity inside the new plane
  ↓
Stability
provisionally retain the plane under finite resources
  ↓
external contact / new observation / other individuality / residual
  ↓
β reopens
```

This is a current research hypothesis, not a settled specification.

## 11. Boundary from the Current Repository Machine Audit

The current Repository checker / machine audit examines such matters as:

- agreement between Manifest and actual files;
- broken links;
- consistency of declared relations;
- missing contract fields.

These checks are important, but they are neither α audit nor β audit as defined in this note.

Their position is better described as:

> **surface-level consistency inspection of the research substrate required to perform α/β audit.**

An empty payload field in a Manifest does not, by itself, establish a metaphysical Cut.

To raise the finding into α or β audit, the logical plane, support, residuals, formative relations, and depth transitions must be reconstructed separately.
