# 日本語正本

## 1. Layer Role / 層の位置づけ

`06_Visual_Materials`は、`01`–`05`までの概念・研究を、**別の知覚形式へ写すレンダリング研究面**である。現在の公開成果物は主として画像、概念ポスター、読解注記だが、役割は画像置き場に限定されない。

レンダリングでは、ある構造が見えやすくなる一方で、主張強度、留保、時間順序、依存関係、非主張境界が圧縮される。そのため06は、単に「分かりやすくする」だけでなく、**何が保持され、何が失われ、どの条件を本文へ返さなければならないか**を確認する。

`07_Creative_Offshoots`との違いは、06が原文書との対応を保ちながら別形式へ写すことを主目的とする点にある。07は共有文法を前提にせず、異質な語彙・世界観・反論へ元型を曝露する。06で生じた圧縮損失はsourceへ返し、07で生じた異質系との衝突は再照合・研究へ返す。

## 2. Public Scope and Claim Profile / 公開範囲と主張強度

このREADMEの公開プロファイルはP0-P1であり、視覚資料そのものを理論証明として扱わない。

レンダリングの成功は、原理の真理性や実証性を保証しない。図が理解を促進しても、本文の条件・限定・反証可能性を置換しない。

# English Commensurated Rendering

## 0. Role

`06_Visual_Materials` is the **rendering-research surface** that projects concepts and research from `01`–`05` into another perceptual form. Its current public artifacts are mainly images, conceptual posters, and reading notes, but its role is not merely to store images.

Rendering makes some structures easier to see while compressing claim strength, reservations, temporal order, dependencies, and non-claim boundaries. The task of 06 is therefore not only to make material easier to understand, but to ask **what is preserved, what is lost, and which conditions must be returned to the source text**.

The difference from `07_Creative_Offshoots` is functional. Layer 06 attempts to preserve correspondence with source documents while changing form. Layer 07 does not assume a shared grammar and instead exposes the archetype to heterogeneous vocabularies, worldviews, and objections. Compression losses in 06 return to source texts; collisions in 07 return to re-collation and research.

## 1. Public Scope

The public profile of this README is P0-P1. Visual material is not theoretical proof.

Successful rendering does not guarantee the truth or empirical warrant of what is rendered. Even when a diagram improves understanding, it does not replace conditions, limitations, or falsification routes stated in the text.

Included:

- public Japanese and English posters;
- conceptual arrangement diagrams;
- notes for reading visual materials; and
- checks on what is preserved or lost when structures from `01`–`05` are projected into another perceptual form.

Not included:

- complete replacements for theoretical texts;
- proof by image alone;
- theoretical approval based on expressive attractiveness;
- private implementation diagrams, persona-OS diagrams, or internal evaluation maps;
- image-generation prompts or raw production logs.
