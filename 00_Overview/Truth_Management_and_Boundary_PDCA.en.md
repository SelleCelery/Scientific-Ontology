## Redefining PDCA through First Purpose, Sharing, World Formation, and Boundary CA

## 0. Positioning

This document examines the process by which a purpose is selected from a recognized world, shared, used to reorganize institutions, actions, and evaluation in reality, and then re-examined through returns from boundaries.

This note owns one central question:

> When purpose is placed as the First Purpose of truth, how should that purpose be shared, what kind of world does it form, and through which returns from boundaries should it be revised or abandoned?

This note does not generate the cognitive axis itself.

The formation of cognitive direction—what becomes perceptible, what becomes intelligible, and what becomes invisible—is treated in [`Entropy_Attributed_Difference_and_Cognitive_Axis_Formation.ja.md`](../02_Raj_Beauty/Entropy_Attributed_Difference_and_Cognitive_Axis_Formation.ja.md).

This note does not directly commensurate ideologies, worldviews, disciplines, and institutions.

The boundary protocol that collates the attention, abstraction, value, cost, Core Invariant, Operational Drift, and untranslatable residuals of candidate worldviews is treated in [`Optional_Axiom_Modules_as_Cognitive_Bridge.ja.md`](../03_Tam_Goodness/Optional_Axiom_Modules_as_Cognitive_Bridge.ja.md).

This note receives their outputs.

```text
Cognitive-Axis Formation
  What becomes visible, and what becomes invisible
        ↓
Cognitive Bridge
  How a worldview, value, or method becomes explicit
        ↓
Truth Management
  What is made into the First Purpose, shared, and examined
        ↓
Organizational Boundary and Port Model
  How it is implemented as responsibility, authority, communication, audit, and return paths
```

Accordingly, this note is not a complete theory of truth.

It does not deny ordinary questions of truth and justification, such as whether a proposition corresponds to fact, whether an inference is valid, or whether the evidence is sufficient. These remain necessary.

What this note adds is the domain in which purpose-setting itself changes the world being addressed.

In corporations, public administration, states, international orders, education, AI adoption, and research planning, it is not enough to describe what is correct. What is placed as the purpose changes personnel, budgets, authority, evaluation, visibility, exceptions, and future options.

In such domains, purpose must itself be examined.

## 1. Why Read Truth from Purpose?

Truth is commonly discussed in terms of correct propositions, correspondence with fact, logical consistency, and verifiability.

These are important.

However, in domains such as organizations, institutions, states, AI, and social design, where the describer also intervenes in the object, they are not sufficient.

Once a purpose is set, the following change:

- what is measured as an achievement;
- what is counted as failure;
- who is treated as a contributor;
- whose burdens are externalized as costs;
- what is processed as an exception;
- what is recorded and what is left unrecorded;
- which anomalies receive attention;
- which anomalies are concealed behind normal operations.

For example, when “efficiency” is placed as the First Purpose, waiting, slack, suspension, explanation, and collation tend to become costs.

When “safety” is placed as the First Purpose, freedom, speed, and discretion tend to become costs.

When “growth” is placed as the First Purpose, maintenance, repair, withdrawal, and exhaustion may become less visible.

A purpose is not a neutral banner.

A purpose draws a world through sensibility, understanding, resource allocation, responsibility, evaluation, and institutions.

Therefore, a purpose must withstand the history returned from the world it has produced.

## 2. Propositional Truth and Operational Truth

This note does not oppose propositional truth and operational truth.

### 2.1 Propositional Truth

Propositional truth asks at least the following:

- Does the proposition correspond to fact?
- Is there evidence?
- Is the inference valid?
- Can it withstand counterexamples?
- Can others collate it?

### 2.2 Operational Truth

Operational truth asks what occurs after a purpose descends into reality:

- What kind of world did the purpose form?
- What became invisible in the name of the purpose?
- Who bore the burden?
- Which responsibility boundaries became ambiguous?
- Which return paths were lost?
- Did the purpose receive corrective pressure from boundaries?

A proposition may be correct while an institution that turns that proposition into a purpose is destructive.

Even a purpose with a good name may form a different world through sharing and implementation.

This note therefore treats truth through a dual condition:

```text
Propositional conditions
  fact / evidence / logic / collation
        +
Operational conditions
  purpose / sharing / world formation / Boundary CA
```

### 3.1 Inputs from Cognitive-Axis Formation

- Boundary conditions under which operation should stop or be re-examined.

Truth Management does not treat these as merely supplementary information.

It treats them as responsibility information that must be available before a purpose is selected.

## 4. First Purpose — Purpose

The First Purpose is what a system places as something to protect, increase, or refuse to lose.

The First Purpose is not a slogan.

For a purpose to function as the First Purpose, at least the following must be made explicit:

- **Object:** For whom or for what is the purpose intended?
- **Protected object:** What is treated as something that must not be lost?
- **Object of transformation:** What is received, and into what is it transformed?
- **Responsible actor:** Who assumes responsibility for outcomes and exceptions?
- **Time horizon:** How far into downstream conditions does the purpose extend?
- **Stopping conditions:** What events require the purpose to be stopped or reset?
- **Non-purposes:** What may not be justified in the name of the purpose?

There need not always be only one First Purpose.

However, where multiple First Purposes exist, their conditions of conflict must be made explicit.

Safety and freedom.  
Growth and maintenance.  
Speed and explanation.  
Individual dignity and community continuity.  
Present benefit and the possibilities of future generations.

When tensions between purposes are treated as though they do not exist, actual choices are delegated to informal power, custom, and metrics.

Setting the First Purpose does not mean eliminating conflict.

It means making explicit what is prioritized, what is suspended, and at which boundaries re-collation must occur.

## 5. Shared Purpose — Sharing

A purpose that remains only within one person remains a wish or belief.

Shared Purpose is the process by which a purpose is passed to others and transformed into a form that can be operated jointly.

Sharing includes at least the following:

- articulation;
- translation;
- education;
- roles;
- authority;
- budgets;
- evaluation metrics;
- procedures;
- exception handling;
- objection;
- audit.

Sharing is not conformity.

To share a purpose does not mean that everyone must possess the same emotions, history, or worldview.

It means that people with different cognitive axes and responsibility boundaries agree on what will be operated as a common purpose and what will remain unshared.

Typical failures of sharing include the following:

- Only the name of the purpose is shared, while its meaning is not.
- Only the metric is shared, while the First Purpose is lost.
- Authority is distributed, while responsibility does not return.
- Agreement is demanded, while ports for objection are closed.
- The translation cost borne by the operational front line is made invisible.
- During conversion into an evaluation system, the purpose drifts into a different purpose.

Shared Purpose is not a process of diluting the purpose.

It is a transformation process that allows the purpose to pass through different boundaries without destroying its core.

## 6. World Formation — The World Formed through Sharing

When a purpose is shared, the world changes.

Here, “world” does not mean the whole cosmos.

It means the operational space of cognition, institutions, action, resources, evaluation, and possibility rearranged by a purpose.

When a purpose is shared, the following change:

- what emerges as a problem;
- what is recorded as an achievement;
- who may participate in decision-making;
- which information becomes important;
- which anomalies are treated as warnings;
- which burdens are absorbed as ordinary costs;
- who receives explanations;
- who is excluded as an exception.

World Formation is not the declaration of the First Purpose.

It is the world that was actually formed.

Therefore, the First Purpose and World Formation do not necessarily coincide.

```text
Declared purpose
  !=
World formed through sharing and implementation
```

A First Purpose of “valuing people” may create a world in which people cannot say that they intend to leave.

A First Purpose of “safety” may create a world in which failures cannot be reported.

A First Purpose of “efficiency” may create a world that erodes the front line’s capacity for collation.

A First Purpose of “inclusion” may create a conformist space in which disagreement cannot be expressed.

To examine World Formation is to look not at the name of the purpose, but at the downstream conditions formed by that purpose.

## 7. Boundary CA — Return from the World to the Purpose

Check and Act are not completed solely inside the planning actor.

They occur at the boundaries of the world formed by the purpose.

This note calls the examination and correction carried out at those boundaries **Boundary CA**.

Boundary CA asks at least the following:

- Who became exhausted?
- What was made invisible?
- Where were costs transferred?
- Which exceptions increased?
- Which anomalies escaped the monitoring system?
- Which responsibility boundaries became ambiguous?
- Who was unable to object?
- Which return paths were lost?
- What remains as a residual available for re-collation?
- What has sedimented as residue in bodies, institutions, relationships, environments, and futures?

Boundary CA is not merely the confirmation of performance metrics.

It is the process of returning what was excluded from the metrics to the object of examination.

For Boundary CA to operate, the following are required:

- observability;
- historical retention;
- exception records;
- ports for objection;
- an auditing actor;
- authority to revise;
- authority to stop;
- a return path to the original purpose.

The purpose of Boundary CA is not to protect the plan.

It is to examine whether the First Purpose remains fit to retain that status.

## 8. Redefining PDCA

Conventional PDCA is represented as Plan, Do, Check, and Act.

Scientific Ontology makes explicit the world actually formed through the sharing of purpose between those stages.

```text
P  = First Purpose
D  = Sharing and Deployment / institutionalization and execution
W  = World Formation / the world formed as the Third Purpose
C  = Boundary Check / collation at the boundary
A  = Boundary Act / revision of boundary, purpose, or operation
P' = Re-collated Purpose
```

The operational cycle therefore becomes:

```text
P -> D -> W -> C/A -> P'
```

In a PDCA model that does not make W explicit, the world formed between planning and execution is difficult to see.

When only outcome metrics are returned to C, the externalization, silence, exceptions, and residues that enabled those metrics are lost.

Likewise, when A is restricted to procedural correction, the possible error of the First Purpose itself cannot be examined.

Act includes several distinct forms of revision:

1. **Procedural revision:** Change the method of execution.
2. **Boundary revision:** Change the object, responsibility, ports, or time horizon.
3. **Metric revision:** Change what is measured as achievement or failure.
4. **Sharing revision:** Change translation, authority, or procedures for objection.
5. **Purpose revision:** Change the priority or content of the First Purpose.
6. **Purpose abandonment:** Stop a purpose that has lost its fitness to remain the First Purpose.

This cycle does not guarantee automatic convergence toward a correct purpose.

Even so, it preserves greater revisability than an operation that leaves the purpose untouched and attributes failure only to inadequate execution at the front line.

## 9. Local Closure, False Closure, and Reopenability

Operation requires closure.

If everything remains undecided, neither judgment nor responsibility can be established.

However, there are different kinds of closure.

### 9.1 Local Closure

Local closure is the act of committing to a decision at a given time while making its scope, duration, responsibility, and reopening conditions explicit.

Even when provisional, local closure is not irresponsible.

It leaves a history of who decided what, on what grounds, and within what scope.

### 9.2 False Closure

False closure is the treatment of something unresolved as though it were resolved.

- Treating metric achievement as purpose achievement.
- Treating procedural completion as completion of responsibility.
- Treating obtained consent as achieved understanding.
- Treating record deletion as deletion of history.
- Treating the absence of audible opposition as agreement.

False closure does not eliminate residuals.

It makes residuals unavailable for re-collation and transfers them to other boundaries as residue.

### 9.3 Reopenability

Mature operation possesses both the capacity to close and the capacity to reopen.

Reopenability does not mean pretending that a past decision never occurred.

It means retaining history while making the purpose, boundaries, metrics, and protocols available for re-collation.

## 10. Failure Modes of Truth

### 10.1 Concealment of Purpose

A purpose other than the declared purpose governs evaluation, budgets, and the distribution of authority.

### 10.2 Exemption through the Name of the Purpose

A purpose with a morally favorable name is used to avoid examining harm and externalization in implementation.

### 10.3 Confusion of Sharing with Conformity

Disagreement, suspension, and conditional participation are rejected, while emotional identification with the purpose is demanded.

### 10.4 Substitution of the World by Metrics

Measurable indicators are treated as substitutes for the whole world that has been formed.

### 10.5 Concealment of Anomalies through Normal Operation

The fact that operations continue is treated as proof of health, causing hidden accumulation, person-dependent operation, and successfully functioning anomalies to be overlooked.

### 10.6 Ritualization of CA

Meetings, reports, and audits exist, but there is no corrective authority capable of changing the purpose, boundaries, or allocation of authority.

### 10.7 Downstreaming of Responsibility

Actors who design, authorize, and benefit fail to retain responsibility, transferring burdens to front-line workers, users, nature, and the future.

### 10.8 Immortalization of the First Purpose

Even after conditions change, the purpose is not revised, and the world is destroyed in order to protect the purpose.

## 11. Scales of Application

The structure proposed in this note can be comparatively applied at different scales.

This does not mean that those scales are identical or governed by one formula.

### 11.1 Individual

An individual sets purposes concerning what to protect, what to assume responsibility for, and where to withdraw.

Personal goals also receive Boundary CA from the body, relationships, time, and lived life.

### 11.2 AI

AI systems are given objective functions, system instructions, evaluation metrics, and contexts of use.

However, the world and responsibilities formed by AI outputs do not close inside the model.

The deploying actor, users, and operating organization must retain the responsibility boundary.

### 11.3 Organization

Within an organization, purpose is transformed into roles, authority, budgets, evaluation, ports, and audit.

The truth or falsity of an organizational purpose appears not in its mission statement, but in what the organization receives, what it transforms, and what it returns to society.

### 11.4 State

State purposes are deployed through law, taxation, education, security, currency, administration, and border management.

A state possesses a large responsibility boundary, but if it compresses internally distinct modes of existence into one purpose too aggressively, it increases exceptions and residues.

### 11.5 International Order

International orders often lack a single final responsible actor.

For this reason, not only shared purpose but also diplomacy, monitoring, objection, withdrawal, and renegotiation ports connecting distinct responsibility boundaries become important.

## 12. Reading History as a History of Purpose Operation

History is not merely a sequence of events.

What did a civilization, religion, state, corporation, or movement place as its First Purpose?

Through which language, institution, money, law, military power, education, narrative, and technology was that purpose shared?

What kind of world did that sharing form?

From which boundaries did corrective pressure return as rebellion, exhaustion, reform, collapse, creation, migration, environmental change, or technological change?

History can be read as a history of purpose operation.

However, this note does not claim that history progresses necessarily toward a single purpose.

It treats history as a branching history in which multiple First Purposes conflict and in which contingency, asymmetry, cutoff, and externalization alter the path.

The role of historical reading is not to subordinate the past to the purposes of the present.

It is to re-collate the abstractions and residues on which the present First Purpose stands.

- the target boundary;
- the responsible actor;
- history that must be retained;
- differences received as input;
- the expected transformation;
- negentropy returned to society;
- externalization that is not permitted;
- required ports;
- exception, objection, and audit paths;
- stopping conditions;
- items to be re-collated through Boundary CA.

The organizational boundary model translates these into responsibility boundaries, ports, protocols, audits, logs, diplomacy, and Boundary Updates.

Truth Management and the organizational boundary model must not be confused.

The former examines whether a purpose remains fit to function as the First Purpose.

The latter operates that purpose as a responsible communication structure.

## 14. Connection to Logical Depth

On a planar logical surface, purpose, sharing, world formation, and Boundary CA can be described as a sequence.

However, not every return is processed on the same cycle.

Some purposes succeed according to short-term metrics while remaining destructive across long-term history.

Some purposes send residue to future generations, natural environments, lost cultures, or modes of existence outside the institution, even when those effects remain invisible to current participants.

The logical-depth axis is a depth direction for retaining such unresolved issues, contradictions, branches, residuals, and semantic pressure without reducing them to before and after in external time alone.

Connection to depth in Truth Management requires at least the following distinctions:

- short-term achievement versus long-term downstream conditions;
- current beneficiaries versus future burden-bearers;
- the name of a purpose versus its historical operation;
- records marked as processed versus history that remains active;
- residuals that cannot yet be expressed in the vocabulary of the present.

This note does not own the formalization of the logical-depth axis.

It identifies where purpose operation fails to close on the planar logical surface and returns those problems to the Concept Network and Research Notes.

## 15. Non-Claim Boundaries

This note does not claim that:

- managers or holders of power may freely determine truth;
- propositional truth, scientific verification, or legal procedure can be replaced by teleology;
- conflict will automatically disappear when a purpose is shared;
- one First Purpose can consistently govern individuals, organizations, states, and international orders;
- replacing PDCA terminology with Scientific Ontology terminology guarantees good operation;
- Boundary CA can repair all harm, irreversibility, and responsibility;
- this note directly recommends a specific management method, policy, system of governance, or model of AI governance.

This note does not justify the authority to set purposes.

It is an examination model through which those who set purposes must assume responsibility for the world formed through sharing and for the returns from its boundaries.

---

## 16. Conclusion

Truth Management is not a management theory for converting truth into advertising copy.

Nor is it a theory for absolutizing organizational purpose.

It receives candidate purposes from cognitive axes and optional axioms, makes explicit what is placed as the First Purpose, and examines the world formed through the sharing of that purpose by means of Boundary CA.

```text
Cognition
  ↓
Candidate axioms and worldviews
  ↓
First Purpose
  ↓
Sharing
  ↓
World Formation
  ↓
Boundary CA
  ↓
Revision, continuation, or abandonment of the First Purpose
```

A purpose does not become truth at the moment it is declared.

The First Purpose is tested for its fitness as truth when it receives the history returned from the world formed by its sharing and asks again what must still be protected.

Management in Scientific Ontology does not mean subordinating people and the world to a purpose.

It means returning the purpose itself to a responsible boundary before the world that the purpose has formed.
