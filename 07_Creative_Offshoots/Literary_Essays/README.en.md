## 1. Directory Role

This directory takes Scientific Ontology into continuous prose that can be read before its framework is learned in sequence. It begins through encounters in life and with other people, returning to authoritative texts, research, and applications when needed. Readability alone is not the criterion of success: what becomes communicable and what falls away when an explanation changes must also be received.

*Keeping Questions Open Without Trampling Boundaries* offers four entrances through method, correctness, ledgers, and peace. Its chapter on correctness returns to the definition owner in 01. Readers are not assumed to adopt all its vocabulary.

## 2. Public Scope

The Japanese text remains the language authority, and the existing full English commensuration remains a separate file. Making an account more accessible does not increase its warrant. Metaphors do not take over the definition authority of established science, accounting, law, or politics; nor is an essay a surface for concealing disagreement with those fields.

The discussion of “correct” presents an attitude and a reading route, not the complete conditions for adjudicating correctness. The ledger asking what remains possible afterward is not a finished accounting standard, and the account of peace does not justify unlimited retaliation. These boundaries in the text must survive summary and commensuration.
