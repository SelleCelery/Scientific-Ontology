## A.0 Purpose of the audit

This appendix brings three questions -- optimization in Business, the "natural-disaster designation" in interpersonal relationships, and mental effects and provenance that may remain after bodily death -- into contact with the engineering definition in *Correctness, Individuality, Absence, and Cut*. Its purpose is to record their correspondences and unprocessed residuals.

It is not enough to explain the three questions in one direction as "concrete examples of the authoritative text." Bringing the model into an application may expose distinctions missing from the model itself. The audit therefore works in both directions. It includes not only

$$
\text{Foundational Model}
\;\longrightarrow\;
\text{Application}
$$

but also

$$
\text{Application}
\;\longrightarrow\;
\text{Residual}
\;\longrightarrow\;
\text{Foundational Re-audit}
$$

The three questions concern different phenomena. This appendix does not reduce markets and organizations, interpersonal relationships, and the inheritance of histories to a single law. The structural question they share is this: after a finite system compresses, abstracts, and provisionally closes a complex object so that it can handle it, how does it retain differences left out by that compression, and how far can it return them to its current judgment?

## A.1 Minimum understanding of the authoritative text required for this audit

This section summarizes enough of the authoritative text for a reader who has not read it beforehand to reproduce a minimum audit using this appendix. It is not a complete substitute for that text, but sets out the symbols and conditions used here.

What is reproduced here is the minimum projection needed to reproduce this audit. It is not a complete restatement or frozen duplicate of the current authoritative text. Read Q1–Q14 as findings retained by this audit record, separately from whether the current authoritative text has explicitly adopted any candidate concept. Use the current definition owner linked above for present definitions.

### A.1.1 The current logical space

Let the logical space that a system can distinguish, explore, compare, and re-collate at time $t$ be

$$
\Omega_t
$$

It includes at least the following components, represented here as

$$
\Omega_t
=
\{E_t,A_t,\Pi_t,H_t,Q_t,B_t\}
$$

where:

- $E_t$: candidates for facts that have been observed or received as input;
- $A_t$: propositions and relations currently accepted;
- $\Pi_t$: rules for inference, transformation, and search;
- $H_t$: formative history;
- $Q_t$: unprocessed differences, open ends, and residuals;
- $B_t$: available search resources.

The most important boundary condition is

$$
\Omega_t \neq \text{World}
$$

The range that the system can currently handle must not be identified with the world itself.

### A.1.2 Internal tenability

Before a state $S_t$ is treated as "correct," require at least internal tenability:

$$
I(S_t)
$$

Internal tenability includes not concealing known contradictions while leaving them unprocessed; not introducing unsupported joins; retaining paths back to support paths and formation conditions to the extent needed; not presenting unprocessed differences as resolved; and not extending conclusions beyond their scope of application.

In brief, the condition is:

> Do not treat what remains as though it were no longer there.

### A.1.3 Frontier, search trace, and defeater

Let the domain that can be constituted as a search target under the current resources $B_t$ and search operators $\Pi_t$ be

$$
\mathcal{F}_{B_t}(S_t,\Pi_t)
$$

Let the search trace that was actually executed be

$$
\tau_t
$$

Within this search, a *defeater* is something that calls for revising, suspending, or localizing the current validity judgment. This includes not only counterexamples but also invalidated support paths, unexamined alternative explanations, overlooked branches, and breaches of historical consistency.

Let the set of defeaters actually discovered be

$$
D_{B_t}(S_t,\Pi_t;\tau_t)
$$

### A.1.4 Search saturation and $D=\varnothing$ / $D=\bot$

Correctness is not certified merely because "no objection was found."

The defeater set is evaluated only when an actual search trace $\tau_t$ exists and

$$
\operatorname{Sat}(\tau_t\mid S_t,\Pi_t,B_t)=1
$$

holds: under the current search rules and resource conditions, the searches that should be performed have actually been attempted, and the process has stopped through a valid instance of search saturation.

If these conditions hold and no defeater is found, then

$$
D_{B_t}(S_t,\Pi_t)=\varnothing
$$

By contrast, when no search was conducted, resources ran out, the search mechanism failed, a port was closed, a return path was cut, or the search kept circling the same place,

$$
D_{B_t}(S_t,\Pi_t)=\bot
$$

The status is **undetermined**.

This distinction is central throughout the appendix.

### A.1.5 Return / Reopening

The authoritative text sets

$$
R(S_t)
=
\operatorname{Ingress}(S_t)
\land
\operatorname{Traceback}(S_t)
\land
\operatorname{Reopen}(S_t)
$$

Ingress means that differences capable of changing the current validity judgment can reach the audit structure.

Traceback means that one can return, to the extent needed, from the current judgment to support, transformations, formation history, input sources and observation conditions, and scope of application. What is recoverable here is the traceable support structure that established the judgment, not possession of an ultimate metaphysical ground.

Reopen means that when new differences arrive or support becomes invalid, the current provisional closure can be released and the state returned to re-examination.

### A.1.6 "Correct" in the engineering sense

The minimal form given by the authoritative text is therefore

$$
\boxed{
C_{B_t}^{(k)}(S_t)
=
I(S_t)
\land
\bigl(D_{B_t}(S_t,\Pi_t^{(k)})=\varnothing\bigr)
\land
R(S_t)
}
$$

This does not mean possessing truth in the general philosophical sense. It denotes the following engineering state:

> Under the current resources, search operators, and formative history, internal tenability is maintained; an actual attempt has been made to break the current judgment; no defeater has been found after search saturation; and when a new difference arrives, it remains possible to return to support paths and formation conditions and reopen the judgment.

### A.1.7 Individuality

Individuality is not a personality label. It is placed as a search structure:

$$
K=(s_0,\Pi,H,\rho)
$$

Here $s_0$ is the initial search state, $\Pi$ the search operators, $H$ the formative history, and $\rho$ the priority rule that allocates finite resources.

Different individualities can therefore yield

$$
\operatorname{Reach}_B(K_A)\neq \operatorname{Reach}_B(K_B)
$$

Individuality is consequently treated not as something that freely determines truth or falsity, but as a frontier generator that changes what can be reached that might break a given "correctness."

### A.1.8 Absence, cut, residual, and pressure

The authoritative text does not collapse absence into one kind. It distinguishes Pure Unknown, Expected Missing, Cut, Resource Boundary, Deliberate Seal, and related cases.

What matters is the abstract residual

$$
r(v)=\operatorname{Expected}(v)-\operatorname{Observed}(v)
$$

A difference between an expected connection and an observed state produces a residual.

A candidate search priority, obtained by collecting downstream residuals back toward upstream points, is placed as

$$
P(v)
=
\sum_{u\in Desc(v)}w(u,v)|r(u)|
$$

However,

$$
P(v)\not\Rightarrow \operatorname{Truth}(v)
$$

Pressure is not a signal that "truth is here." It is a signal to re-examine a place where the relationship between the current structure and the input world is not working well.

### A.1.9 Communication topology

View the system as

$$
G=(V,E,P,H)
$$

Here $V$ contains nodes such as states, concepts, observations, and judgments; $E$ contains edges such as support, transformation, dependency, and return; $P$ denotes external ports; and $H$ is the history of formation and change.

There are two basic forms of failure.

One is a Cut: a path that ought to connect cannot be traversed.

The other is an Unsupported Join: a connection that should not be made, such as unsupported causation, unjustified generalization, or the identification of states with different histories.

## A.2 Mapping the three questions into a shared form

The three questions share an operation: compressing an object so that it can be handled for a finite purpose.

Place the general form as

$$
X_i
\xrightarrow{\Phi_i}
M_i
\xrightarrow{\Pi_i}
Y_i
$$

where:

- $X_i$: the original object;
- $\Phi_i$: compression, abstraction, or modeling;
- $M_i$: the operational model;
- $\Pi_i$: inference, optimization, or judgment over that model;
- $Y_i$: output or result.

In Business,

$$
X_B=
\text{people, time, land, skills, relationships}
$$

is compressed by

$$
\Phi_B
$$

into operational variables such as prices, work-hours, profit, utilization rates, and unit prices.

In the natural-disaster designation,

$$
X_N=
\text{the other person as a whole}
$$

is compressed by

$$
\Phi_N
$$

into "response patterns observable in the current relationship."

In the inheritance of mental histories,

$$
X_H=
\text{lived experience, judgment, formative history}
$$

is compressed by

$$
\Phi_H
$$

into writing, lessons, institutions, and records.

The operation itself is not improper. Compression is necessary because finite systems cannot retain the entirety of their objects.

The danger begins when the system starts treating

$$
M_i \equiv X_i
$$

as the case: a model built to handle an object acquires authority to define the object as a whole.

This appendix provisionally represents that state for audit purposes as

$$
\operatorname{FC}(S)
=
\operatorname{TreatAsClosed}(S)
\land
\Bigl[
D(S)=\bot
\;\lor\;
\neg R(S)
\;\lor\;
\operatorname{ScopeOverflow}(S)
\Bigr]
$$

$\operatorname{FC}$ is an audit symbol for False Closure, not an official symbol of the authoritative model. It means:

> A state that does not meet the conditions for closing a correctness judgment, or that exceeds its scope of application, yet is operationally treated as "already correct," "already sufficient," or "no longer in need of examination."

In Business: "It is profitable, so the structure as a whole is good."

In the natural-disaster designation: "They did not change however many times I explained, so this person cannot change."

In the inheritance of histories: "This lesson has survived from long ago, so it is still correct."

Each can extend a local form of viability to the object as a whole.

## A.3 Audit target 1: Business and social return

### A.3.1 Direct correspondence with the authoritative text

Cut and Return provide the most direct correspondences in the Business discussion.

Suppose an unreasonable deadline is met. If "successful delivery" returns to the decision-maker while the resulting exhaustion, staff departures, and lost opportunities to learn do not return to the decision-making surface, then

$$
Y_{\mathrm{success}}
\rightarrow
\operatorname{Decision}
$$

holds, while

$$
Q_{\mathrm{cost}}
\nrightarrow
\operatorname{Decision}
$$

does not supply that return.

This can be read as a Cut in communication topology.

The important point is that the existence of a complaints desk or questionnaire alone does not guarantee return. Even if information arrives, there may be Ingress without effective return when that information cannot change judgment conditions or resource allocation.

### A.3.2 Residual: Actuate

The authoritative text's

$$
R
=
\operatorname{Ingress}
\land
\operatorname{Traceback}
\land
\operatorname{Reopen}
$$

is strong as an epistemic and auditing structure. But bringing it into social institutions exposes a new residual.

An objection can enter. One can return to the cause. Re-examination is possible. Yet without the authority, budget, personnel, or implementation route needed to change a decision, can one say that something has socially "returned"?

This appendix therefore places the following audit candidate:

$$
R_{\mathrm{social}}^{*}
=
\operatorname{Ingress}
\land
\operatorname{Traceback}
\land
\operatorname{Reopen}
\land
\operatorname{Actuate}
$$

Here $\operatorname{Actuate}$ refers to:

> An effecting path through which a judgment changed by re-collation can return to actual decisions, resource allocation, procedures, and implementation.

This is not an additional definition imposed on the authoritative text. Rather, it records an application residual: the following implication does not necessarily hold.

$$
R \stackrel{?}{\Longrightarrow} \text{social correction}
$$

### A.3.3 Externalization and the asymmetry of return

In Business, the actor making the decision can differ from the actor bearing its costs. Thus

$$
\operatorname{DecisionActor} \neq \operatorname{CostBearer}
$$

can hold.

When it does, even if failure occurs,

$$
Q_{\mathrm{cost}}
$$

may fail to return to the decision-maker and instead be absorbed as a third party's burden.

This residual should not simply be read as "the manager is bad." The questions are whether the following three points coincide, or are effectively connected:

$$
\text{Who decides?}
$$

$$
\text{Who receives the effect?}
$$

$$
\text{Who can reopen the decision?}
$$

## A.4 Audit target 2: Natural-disaster designation and Action Closure

### A.4.1 The most important separation

The largest residual exposed by bringing natural-disaster designation into contact with the authoritative text is

$$
\operatorname{EpistemicClosure}
\neq
\operatorname{ActionClosure}
$$

If adequate search saturation has not been reached concerning the proposition that another person could change in the future, then

$$
D_{B_t}(P_{\mathrm{other\ updateable}})=\bot
$$

This is a state in which one cannot say, "the other person cannot change."

Nevertheless, to protect one's body, time, safety, and living conditions, it can be possible to set

$$
\operatorname{Close}_{\mathrm{action}}=1
$$

Thus

$$
\operatorname{Close}_{\mathrm{action}}=1
$$

and

$$
\operatorname{Close}_{\mathrm{claim}}=0
$$

can coexist. The state is:

> I do not settle that you can never change. But I will not spend any more of my body and time on finding out.

### A.4.2 Relation to Resource Boundary / Deliberate Seal

Withdrawal after a natural-disaster designation touches Resource Boundary or Deliberate Seal in the authoritative text's classification.

That is, either

$$
B_t \leq B_{\min}
$$

has become the case, or safety and recovery are reasons for setting

$$
\operatorname{DeliberateSeal}=1
$$

Any resulting quiet must not be confused with search saturation:

$$
\operatorname{Silence}
\not\Rightarrow
D=\varnothing
$$

### A.4.3 Different observational axes arising from individuality

The authoritative text places individuality as

$$
K=(s_0,\Pi,H,\rho)
$$

Even when no updating is visible along our own observational axis $\rho_A$,

$$
\operatorname{Observed}_{\rho_A}(\operatorname{Update}_B)=0
$$

does not entail

$$
\operatorname{Update}(B)=0
$$

The possibility remains that the other person is updating under different conditions, in another relationship, or along another axis.

This residual does not invalidate the natural-disaster designation. Rather, it is a reason why the following separation is needed:

$$
\operatorname{Close}_{\mathrm{action}}=1,
\qquad
\operatorname{Close}_{\mathrm{claim}}=0
$$

### A.4.4 Evidential burdens for private withdrawal and public assertions

Publicly making a broad negative claim about another person and reducing one's own contact with them call for different strengths of grounds. It can therefore be the case that

$$
\operatorname{EvidenceThreshold}_{\mathrm{public\ claim}}
>
\operatorname{EvidenceThreshold}_{\mathrm{private\ retreat}}
$$

This does not mean that "anything goes in a private judgment." Private withdrawal primarily concerns the allocation of one's own resources, whereas a public assertion acts on another person's reputation, rights, and opportunities.

### A.4.5 Mirroring and power asymmetry

The other person may also treat us as a fixed response system. However, the existence of

$$
\operatorname{MutualModeling}
$$

and equality of

$$
\operatorname{MutualPower}
$$

are separate matters.

The same "refusal," "fixing," or "cut" has different effects for the party that can compel contact and the party that has difficulty leaving. Discovering a mirror relationship therefore does not automatically justify setting

$$
\operatorname{Responsibility}_A=\operatorname{Responsibility}_B
$$

## A.5 Audit target 3: Body, mind, provenance, and Proxy Return

### A.5.1 Separating continued effects from continued subjectivity

Writing, institutions, lessons, and criteria for judgment can continue to act after a person's body has stopped. But

$$
\operatorname{Effect}_{t>\mathrm{death}}\neq 0
$$

does not entail

$$
\operatorname{Subject}_{t>\mathrm{death}}\neq 0
$$

Continued effects and the continued subjective experience of the person must be distinguished.

### A.5.2 When return to the original subject becomes impossible

When a person dies, or an organization ceases to exist,

$$
\operatorname{Ingress}_{\mathrm{source}}=0
$$

can become the case. Residuals remain that cannot be returned to the original subject.

Consider the successor structure

$$
S_0
\rightarrow
\operatorname{Artifact}
\rightarrow
S_1
$$

Here $S_0$ is the original subject; Artifact is writing, a record, an institution, or data; and $S_1$ is a later reader, organization, or auditing subject.

A candidate for Proxy / Successor Return then appears:

$$
R_{\mathrm{successor}}
=
\operatorname{Ingress}(S_1)
\land
\operatorname{Traceback}_{\mathrm{archive}}
\land
\operatorname{Reopen}(S_1)
$$

It means:

> Instead of necessarily returning a residual to its source, connect it to a successor subject capable of judging it again.

This does not replace the authoritative text's concept of return. It is an unresolved question about how far return can be extended when the original subject no longer exists.

### A.5.3 Provenance does not guarantee validity

Being able to return to history matters, but

$$
\operatorname{Provenance}(x)
\not\Rightarrow
\operatorname{Validity}(x)
$$

Mistaken judgments also have histories. Prejudices also have formative reasons.

Provenance is therefore treated not as

> proof of correctness,

but as

> an entrance that makes re-examination possible.

### A.5.4 Irrecoverable residuals

Not all residuals can necessarily be recovered. When the original records no longer exist, the parties have died, and others' memories have been lost,

$$
\operatorname{Traceback}=0
$$

may be the case.

To think "creating a return path will solve it" can itself become false closure. Theory therefore needs to allow for

$$
\operatorname{IrrecoverableResidual}
$$

The existence of irrecoverable residuals does not make return unnecessary. It increases the importance of designing in advance what should be preserved before it is lost.

## A.7 Accumulation of residuals

Residuals are not added together into a single number. Their meaning and scope of effect differ across Business, interpersonal relationships, the inheritance of histories, and the essay itself.

They are therefore retained as a residual vector:

$$
\mathbf Q
=
(Q_1,Q_2,\ldots,Q_n)
$$

### Q1 Epistemic Correctness and Action Authorization are distinct

$$
C_{\mathrm{epistemic}}
\neq
Permit_{\mathrm{action}}
$$

Being able to judge something correct is not the same as being permitted to intervene in the world. This is the most important residual exposed by the audit.

### Q2 Action Closure and Claim Closure are distinct

Even if

$$
\operatorname{Close}_{\mathrm{action}}=1
$$

it can still be the case that

$$
\operatorname{Close}_{\mathrm{claim}}=0
$$

There are circumstances in which a person can stop contact without settling the other person's essence.

### Q3 Does social Return require Actuate?

$$
R_{\mathrm{social}}^{*}
\stackrel{?}{=}
R\land \operatorname{Actuate}
$$

If objections can enter, grounds can be traced, and re-examination can occur, but no authority path permits a change in the decision, can this be called social return?

This remains unresolved.

### Q4 Provenance of Expected

The residual is defined as

$$
r(v)=\operatorname{Expected}(v)-\operatorname{Observed}(v)
$$

If Expected itself is mistaken, however, residual pressure can misdirect the search. What is needed, therefore, includes

$$
\operatorname{Provenance}(\operatorname{Expected})
$$

and conditions under which Expected becomes invalid.

### Q5 Individuality-dependent differences in observation

$$
\operatorname{Observed}_{\rho_A}(\operatorname{Update}_B)=0
\not\Rightarrow
\operatorname{Update}(B)=0
$$

Conditions are needed to prevent updating by another individuality or search geometry from being judged zero solely through one's own observational axis.

### Q6 Proxy / Successor Return

When the original subject no longer exists,

$$
\text{Return to Source}
$$

becomes impossible. Whether the concept of return should then include

$$
\text{Return to Re-auditable Successor}
$$

remains unresolved.

### Q7 Private withdrawal can transfer residuals to third parties

Even when an individual withdraws legitimately, their burden may pass to another person:

$$
\operatorname{PrivateClosure}
\rightarrow
\operatorname{ExternalResidual}
$$

may occur. Without denying individual withdrawal, institutional arrangements must therefore audit how residual burdens are redistributed.

### Q8 Provenance and validity are not identical

$$
\operatorname{Provenance}
\neq
\operatorname{Validity}
$$

The possibility of tracing an origin does not guarantee that the origin is valid.

### Q9 The system boundary of Business

If the word "Business" simultaneously contains firms, contracts, markets, ownership, legal institutions, labor relations, and consumer choice, the boundary of

$$
G_B
$$

becomes unclear. Unless we distinguish which nodes can change which edges, responsibility-bearing subjects become mixed together.

### Q10 The audited essay has not reached saturation

$$
D_T=\bot
$$

High internal tenability must be distinguished from saturation of external exploration.

### Q11 The same compression operation does not produce the same power effects

When Business compresses others in order to manage them, and when an individual compresses an understanding of another person to reduce their own contact,

$$
\Phi_B
$$

and

$$
\Phi_N
$$

may be formally similar, yet their ranges of effect on others differ. Thus

$$
\operatorname{SameOperation}
\not\Rightarrow
\operatorname{SameResponsibility}
$$

### Q12 Irrecoverable Residual

A return path cannot necessarily be made for every residual. Theory needs to allow for

$$
Q_{\mathrm{irrecoverable}}\neq\varnothing
$$

### Q13 Stopping conditions and unlimited self-audit

Auditing the audit indefinitely produces infinite regress. Therefore,

$$
\operatorname{AuditClosure}
$$

itself needs legitimate stopping conditions. That stopping must not, however, be identified with

$$
\operatorname{Correctness}
$$

### Q14 False isomorphism across scales

Using the same notation for individuals, companies, society, and posthumous inheritance of histories creates a risk of turning structural similarity into ontological identity. It is therefore necessary to make explicit that

$$
\operatorname{StructuralSimilarity}
\neq
\operatorname{OntologicalIdentity}
$$

## A.8 The central residual: The bridge from "correctness" to "intervention"

The largest residual in this audit lies between the Correctness addressed by the authoritative text and actual Action Authorization.

The authoritative text specifies

$$
C_{B_t}^{(k)}(S_t)
$$

with substantial rigor. However, even when

$$
C_{B_t}^{(k)}(S_t)=1
$$

it is not necessarily the case that

$$
\operatorname{MayDo}(a)=1
$$

In other words,

$$
\operatorname{Know}(P)
\not\Rightarrow
\operatorname{MayDo}(A)
$$

Between the two, there may be separate conditions including at least:

$$
\operatorname{Authority}
$$

$$
\operatorname{Consent}
$$

$$
\operatorname{BoundaryRespect}
$$

$$
\operatorname{BurdenDistribution}
$$

$$
\operatorname{Safety}
$$

$$
\operatorname{Reversibility}
$$

$$
\operatorname{ThirdPartyImpact}
$$

This appendix does not establish these as a canonical formula for Action Authorization.

An audit candidate might be placed in a form such as

$$
\operatorname{Auth}_t(a)
\stackrel{?}{=}
C_{B_t}^{(k)}(S_t)
\land
\operatorname{Jurisdiction}_t(a)
\land
\operatorname{Boundary}_t(a)
\land
\operatorname{Burden}_t(a)
\land
\operatorname{Safety}_t(a)
$$

but the definitions of its terms remain unsettled. The formula itself is not the main point. The point is to retain this residual:

> Knowing the world correctly and having the right to change the world through one's own judgment are different things.

This audit treats that as a candidate for a further theoretical layer, not a small correction.

## A.9 Reproduction procedure for auditors

When using this appendix to audit another essay, institution, interpersonal relationship, or AI output, the following sequence is proposed.

First, specify the audit target $S$ and its system boundary. Do not conflate a person, an organization, an institution, and a document.

Second, describe what the target compresses in order to handle it. Place

$$
X
\xrightarrow{\Phi}
M

## A.11 Final compression of the audit record

The structure obtained by this audit can be compressed into the following minimal forms.

### Compression 1

$$
\operatorname{Model} \neq \text{World}
$$

A model for handling an object is not the object itself.

### Compression 2

$$
D=\bot \neq D=\varnothing
$$

Do not confuse an undetermined status with a state in which no defeater was found after search.

### Compression 3

$$
\operatorname{Close}_{\mathrm{action}}=1
\not\Rightarrow
\operatorname{Close}_{\mathrm{claim}}=1
$$

Stopping an action does not require closing the proposition as well.

### Compression 4

$$
\operatorname{Correctness}
\not\Rightarrow
\operatorname{Authorization}
$$

Knowing correctly does not necessarily give rise to authority to intervene.

### Compression 5

$$
\operatorname{Ingress}+\operatorname{Traceback}+\operatorname{Reopen}
\stackrel{\mathrm{social}}{\not\Rightarrow}
\operatorname{Change}
$$

Social implementation may separately require a path of authority through which a changed judgment becomes effective action.

### Compression 6

$$
\operatorname{Provenance}
\neq
\operatorname{Validity}
$$

History is not proof of correctness; it is a surface for re-examination.

### Compression 7

$$
\operatorname{Return}
\not\equiv
\text{Return to Origin}
$$

When the original subject no longer exists, connection to a successor subject capable of judging again may be needed.

### Compression 8

$$
\operatorname{StructuralSimilarity}
\neq
\operatorname{OntologicalIdentity}
$$

The fact that the same notation can be used in different domains does not prove that they have the same ontology.

### Final residual

The authoritative text concerns "how far a finite system may currently close a judgment as correct." The three questions have exposed the next boundary:

> **How far may a subject that has attained correctness intervene in the world?**

This question lies outside the authoritative text, but arises naturally from it. The audit therefore does not delete the residual. It sends it onward to a further layer:

$$
\boxed{
\operatorname{Correctness}
\;\longrightarrow\;
\operatorname{ActionBoundary}
\;\longrightarrow\;
\operatorname{Unresolved}
}
$$

This is the return path at the present point.
