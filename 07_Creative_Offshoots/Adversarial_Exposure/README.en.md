## 1. Directory Role

This case brings *Correctness, Individuality, Absence, and Cut* from 01 into contact with Business, withdrawal from interpersonal relationships, and histories of judgment that remain beyond a body. The essay does not simply explain the core: it also turns objections toward the self that models other people. The appendix records what corresponds and what the core alone did not close.

“Adversarial” does not classify people or cultures as enemies. It names the directory's function: exposure to conditions that do not adopt our explanation and questions that could break it. This case is a structural cross-collation, not a declaration that an independent external refutation test has been completed.

## 2. Public Scope

The essay, audit record, and foundational text are separate documents. The Japanese essay is authoritative for the language of that essay; the Japanese appendix is authoritative for its audit record. Neither automatically changes definitions in 01. English commensurations occupy separate files.

The appendix explicitly retains `D_T = ⊥`, lack of search saturation, and non-certification of correctness. Successful formula rendering is not successful mathematical proof, empirical validation, or independent peer review. Its phrase “comparatively strong” is the audit record's qualitative judgment, not a new S/E score or quantitative certification scale.

The “natural-disaster designation” is limited by the essay to a private change in mode of contact; it is not an official designation, medical diagnosis, or permanent personality classification. The inheritance of mind must be read with continued effects distinguished from the person's continued subjective experience. Removing dangerous words must not also erase the burdens to which they point.
