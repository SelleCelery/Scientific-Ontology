## Purpose

This release introduces **Boundary Epistemological Critique / 境界認識批判** as a new foundational layer of **Scientific Ontology / 存在境界論**. It reframes knowledge through boundary, history, internal and external heuristics, operational correspondence, collation, residual retention, residue formation, and capacity for judgment.

This release remains a public conceptual archive and navigational interface. It is not a complete formal specification, empirical scientific theory, AI runtime package, or disclosure of private core materials.

### Clarified

- Clarified that Boundary Epistemological Critique is not an observation theory designed to make everything observable. It asks how finite observing systems preserve access to what leaks out of observation, classification, operational correspondence, and collation.
- Clarified the distinction between residual and residue: residual as a re-collatable difference, residue as sedimented history or extra-institutional burden that continues to act on a field.
- Clarified that sensibility and understanding branch into formal-space theory, time theory, and boundary-noise theory.
- Clarified that the logical-depth axis is not identical with computational logical depth, but is used in SO as a depth direction for unresolved residuals, residues, contradictions, semantic pressure, and re-collation.
- Clarified that AI-era knowledge should preserve capacity for judgment and access to residuals, rather than merely producing closed answers.

- Reframed `03_Tam_Goodness/AI_Personality_as_Response_Structure.md` as the public gate and hub for personality AI documents.
- Clarified the relationship between:
  - `03_Tam_Goodness/AI_Boundary_Interface_and_Synchronous_Understanding.md`

- Clarified that Scientific Ontology does not reject the achievements or practical power of modern science, but treats science as a powerful heuristic system whose observational, objectifying, causal, temporal, and subject-object assumptions may be reopened.

- Clarified that personality AI is treated as response structure, not as tone, roleplay, character setting, or a claim of machine consciousness.
- Clarified that meaning in personality AI applications may be treated as a return orbit formed through repeated contact between human history and AI response.
- Clarified that `History_Loop_Radius_and_Return_Stability.md` is a conceptual / heuristic model, not empirical psychology, proof of AI consciousness, or a replacement for physical law.
