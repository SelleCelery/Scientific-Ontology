## Release theme

**Boundary cognition: from critique to formation, operation, and ethical implementation.**

Public Edition v4.3.0 extends the boundary-oriented movement established in v4.2.0.

Earlier high-compression roots such as AMP, ITS, and HFC made broad conceptual generation possible, but did not by themselves provide easily stated public observation conditions or falsification criteria. v4.2.0 therefore moved the public description toward boundary realism, boundary epistemological critique, claim-strength control, and explicit correspondence limits.

v4.3.0 continues that movement without changing its direction.

It connects:

1. **critique of boundary cognition** in `01_Sat_Truth`,
2. **formation of boundary cognition** in `02_Raj_Beauty`,
3. **operation of boundary cognition** in `03_Tam_Goodness`, and
4. **communication, organizational, AI, social, and peace-oriented implementation** in the Applications and Research Notes layers.

The release also documents the research dynamics behind that expansion: deeper inquiry into the conditions of cognition produces richer public descriptions at the surface. Ethical questions do not appear only after the theory is complete. The requirement to connect without erasure, stop without annihilation, retain residuals, and preserve return paths drives the increase in conceptual resolution.

### 日本語要旨

v4.3.0の主題は、**境界認識批判から、境界認識生成・境界認識運用・倫理実装へ**である。

AMP、ITS、HFCのような高深度・高圧縮の根から直接強い主張を行うのではなく、v4.2.0では、観測成立条件、境界実在性、境界認識批判、主張強度、非主張境界へ記述位置を移した。

v4.3.0は、その運動を自然に引き継いでいる。

- `01_Sat_Truth`では、認識の成立条件と捨象を批判する。
- `02_Raj_Beauty`では、差分、履歴、関心、捨象から認識方向が生成される過程を扱う。
- `03_Tam_Goodness`では、異なる認識形式を比較、通約、保留、接続、再開する規約を扱う。
- ApplicationsとResearch Notesでは、その構造を通信、組織、AI、社会設計、平和仕様へ降ろす。

根拠を探るために根を深く伸ばした結果、公開可能な表層記述が豊かになった。さらに、壊さずに接触し、差異を消去せずに運用し、残差と返路を保持するという倫理的要求が、研究の解像度上昇を駆動した。

## Relation to v4.2.0

v4.2.0 established the public boundary from which this release proceeds.

Its central work included:

- Boundary Realism Principle,
- Boundary Epistemological Critique,
- Physics Correspondence Policy,
- stronger claim-strength and publication-layer controls,
- clearer Research Notes navigation, and
- public-format checking infrastructure.

v4.3.0 does not replace that release-level interpretation. It makes the next movement explicit.

```text
Boundary cognition critique
        ↓
Boundary cognition formation
        ↓
Boundary cognition operation
        ↓
Purpose and research dynamics
        ↓
Communication and organizational implementation
        ↓
AI, social design, and peace specifications
        ↓
Residuals, objections, and results return upstream
```

This is a change in descriptive resolution rather than a change of theoretical direction.

This application connects cognitive-axis formation, existence-phase compression, boundary diplomacy, port allocation, and organizational boundaries to a peace specification.

Peace is not defined as unconditional opening, complete synchronization, or the elimination of all boundaries. It is approached through limited contact, capacity, refusal, objection, suspension, return, and possible reopening among finite history-bearing entities.

The document is a public conceptual application, not a replacement for diplomacy, international law, political science, psychology, physics, or professional policy design.

## Clarified in this release

- Boundary cognition critique, formation, and operation are distinct but connected responsibilities.
- `01_Sat_Truth`, `02_Raj_Beauty`, and `03_Tam_Goodness` are not merely topic folders. Structurally, they describe network conditions, what moves through the network and how it is read, and the rules by which communication is governed and returned.
- Research dynamics means expanding publicly describable, comparable, implementable, and re-collatable surfaces by investigating the conditions of cognition more deeply.
- Ethical requirements can drive theoretical resolution rather than appearing only as downstream applications.
- Seamless cognition does not imply boundarylessness. Finite operation requires provisional discretization, ports, capacities, stopping conditions, and return paths.
- Peace is not equated with complete openness or total synchronization.
- Public generative source, Public definition owner, Private lineage, and Operationalized in are different relations.
- The root README is the only English-first English–Japanese co-authoritative document unless the publication policy is explicitly revised.
- `ja+en` remains an asymmetric integrated format: Japanese authoritative text with an English commensurated rendering.
- Physics-adjacent correspondences remain governed by the Physics Correspondence Policy and do not establish replacement physics.
