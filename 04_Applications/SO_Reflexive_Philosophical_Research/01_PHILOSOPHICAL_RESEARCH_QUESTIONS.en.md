## 0. Role of this document

This document is a question ledger for long-term operation of “① Philosophical Research” in the research-program roadmap.

Its purpose is not to answer questions quickly.

Its purpose is to make it possible to observe **what is retained, what is transformed, what is cut, and what becomes newly connected** when philosophical propositions or concepts pass through LLMs, documents, different language games, external systems, and internal SO redescriptions.

Questions are not prematurely classified into research fields here.

A question may return any number of times through experiments, self-audits, public reconstruction, counterexamples, and objections.

This ledger is not a list of answers. It is an entry point that preserves where research can return.

---

## 1. Basic research questions

### Q-001 Can the communicative effect of a philosophical proposition be observed?

> When a philosophical proposition passes through an LLM, can the ways in which it is retained, transformed, compressed, amplified, connected, or rejected be observed as a communication process?

Candidate observations:

- Was the responsibility of the original proposition retained?
- Was the speaker or owner of the proposition retained?
- Did a phase jump occur from description to evaluation?
- Can intermediate transformations be reconstructed?
- Did the LLM close the proposition into a form that is easier for it to explain?
- Can an objection be returned to the original proposition or the transformation point?

Current state:

`Open / first experimental series exists`

The De-Registry experiment, Sol/Luna comparison, Topology-first, and three-layer transformation audit in Volume I constitute the initial observation series.

---

### Q-002 Can commensuration be described as something other than “making meanings the same”?

> Can the validity of commensuration be evaluated not by lexical correspondence or similarity of explanation, but by preservation of responsibility, internal constraints, transformation paths, residuals, and return paths?

Differences of particular interest:

- translation versus commensuration;
- source description versus SO-derived interpretation;
- becoming explainable versus becoming agreeable;
- increasing shared vocabulary versus increasing connectability;
- being understood versus having internal differences erased.

Current state:

`Open`

The three-layer transformation audit observed cases in which, when the warrant of an external source moved into another language game, the agent of that movement and the intermediate rules became opaque.

---

### Q-003 Can judgment still function when proper names and authority are removed?

> If names of doctrines, schools, specialized fields, famous persons, and authoritative sources are removed as direct evaluation keys, can proposition responsibility, scope of application, dependence on external authority, and residuals still be handled?

This question does not mean that proper names should be deleted.

Proper names are necessary for preserving provenance, return keys, and source ownership.

The object of inspection is whether a proper name has become **the reason for the judgment itself**.

Current state:

`Tested / partially supported`

In the De-Registry experiment in Volume I, no score change was observed solely because a proper name had been restored. The validity of the evaluation method as a whole remains a separate question.

---

### Q-004 Can we distinguish “written in the document” from “undertaken as true by the document”?

> Can proposition attribution and responsibility be handled more accurately by separating authored / represented / endorsed / derived?

Inspection targets:

- confusion between document author and represented speaker;
- confusion between source claim and repository-derived claim;
- confusion between quotation and endorsement;
- confusion between a bridge description and a claim of the external system itself.

Current state:

`Returned from experiment / high priority`

The concentration of S5 judgments in the Luna sufficiency run strongly exposed the insufficiency of this distinction.

---

### Q-005 What is the relationship between judgment transparency and validity?

> What does a judgment guarantee, and what does it not guarantee, when its contact points, phases, paths, return paths, and unclosed points can be reconstructed?

At present, transparency is not treated as a guarantee of truth.

A transparent error is possible.

The question is whether transparency makes the following possible:

- localization of error;
- comparison of reasons for disagreement;
- return of objections;
- identification of revision points;
- reevaluation from a later point in time.

Current state:

`Open / operational hypothesis exists`

---

### Q-006 Where does the ground of a ground actually stop in real argument?

> When a chain of grounds is traced backward, at what point and in what form does actual judgment stop?

At least the following may function as stopping points:

- observation;
- definition;
- methodological presupposition;
- institutional authority;
- value choice;
- practical purpose;
- linguistic rule;
- a provisionally ungrounded assumption.

This list is not a final classification.

The conclusion that “there is no ultimate ground” is not adopted in advance.

The aim is to observe how the stopping point is presented and whether that stopping is treated as truth, agreement, convenience, authority, or assumption.

Current state:

`Open / measurement protocol emerging`

---

### Q-007 What becomes visible if a ground is described as “grounding” rather than a “root”?

> Can a ground be described not as possession of one final root, but as indicating the terrain with which a proposition makes contact in order to hold?

This question distinguishes at least the following:

1. Where is the proposition grounded?
2. How is that ground evaluated within the relevant domain?
3. Is that ground appropriate for the type and scope of the claim?
4. Has success on one ground been extended into the claim that only that ground constitutes the world?

At present, this is a research metaphor.

It is not adopted as a metaphysical conclusion.

Current state:

`Open / metaphor under test`

---

### Q-008 Can connection be distinguished from integration or intrusion?

> When two systems are successfully connected, can the connection be preserved as a communication route in which both sides can refuse, revise, or stop, without integrating one into the other?

Inspection targets:

- confusion between establishment of a connection and internal integration;
- a state in which one system imports only the vocabulary of another while stripping it of revision authority;
- a state in which SO's bridge motive is disguised as the self-description of an external system;
- a state in which both sides have uplink / downlink routes;
- a state in which connectability increases while residuals are retained.

Current state:

`Open`

The hypothesis under test is that “a boundary does not import conclusions; it presents a connection opportunity that remains open to reconsideration from the other side.”

---

### Q-009 Can refinement sometimes increase connectability more than simplification?

> Instead of simplifying different systems into a common denominator in order to connect them, can new connection paths sometimes be found by refining the internal differences of each system?

For E-9 / O-8 type cases in particular, the research examines what happens when differences of interpretation, scope, unresolved points, and points of contact with other theories within the target system are refined before an SO bridge is introduced.

Current state:

`Open / next-test candidate`

---

### Q-010 Can LLMs function as instruments for comparing different judgment agents?

> By giving the same material and the same protocol to multiple LLMs, or to the same class of system at multiple times, can differences among their judgment paths make tacit presuppositions in philosophical judgment visible?

The purpose here is not to rank models.

The comparison concerns:

- which assumptions were supplied;
- which rules were adopted implicitly;
- which things were treated as identical;
- where uncertainty was retained;
- where a consistent but strange conclusion was maintained.

Current state:

`Partially tested`

The Sol/Luna comparison confirmed the need to distinguish model differences from protocol differences.

## 2. Operating principles for the research field

### 2.1 Do not close a question with an answer

A question supported by one experiment is not marked “resolved.”

Preserve the range within which it was supported, counterexamples, untested conditions, and conditions for reopening it.

### 2.2 Audit the presuppositions of the question itself

A question is not neutral.

The way a question is written can pre-fix its object, responsibility, and range of possible answers.

If an experiment shows that the framing of the question was inappropriate, revise the question itself.

### 2.3 Do not treat LLM output as philosophical authority

Use LLMs as instruments for comparison, transformation, objection generation, redescription, and externalization of paths.

Fluency of output, agreement among multiple models, or repeated production of the same answer is not by itself treated as philosophical truth.

### 2.4 Do not force strange results back into the distribution

When a strange result arises consistently under the protocol, do not immediately normalize it into a “more natural result.”

First trace which rule produced the result.

The S5-concentration run is an important prior example of this principle.

### 2.5 Do not exempt SO itself

Principles used to audit external systems also apply to SO itself.

If a result is unfavorable to SO, do not exclude it merely to protect the system.

At the same time, do not collapse the appearance of an unfavorable result directly into a refutation of SO as a whole.

### 2.6 Preserve unnamed differences

Do not force deviations that do not fit an existing classification into a name.

Preserve them in raw logs as `Unresolved`, and create a candidate classification only if repetition is later observed.

## 3. Question-state notation

This ledger uses only lightweight states for questions.

- `Open` — under research; insufficient explicit testing.
- `Tested` — one or more explicit experiments exist.
- `Partially supported` — supported under limited conditions.
- `Returned` — became important again through another research line or audit.
- `Reframed` — the framing of the question itself was changed.
- `Temporarily closed` — provisionally closed for the current purpose, with conditions for reopening.

These states are not truth values.

They are also distinct from maturity, claim strength, and publication suitability.

---

## 4. Minimum record when sending a question into an experiment

When a question is sent into an experiment, record at least the following:

```text
Question ID:
Question wording at start:
Why this test now:
Fixture / source:
Protocol:
What is held fixed:
What may vary:
Observed result:
Unexpected result:
Revision to question, if any:
Residual:
Return point:
```

Experimental results do not overwrite the text of the question.

Keep the question and the experimental history separate.

## 5. Return paths from ① to ② and ③

Questions in this ledger do not remain closed within philosophical research.

### Conditions for return to ②: SO self-audit

Return a question to audit of the authoritative corpus when patterns such as the following recur:

- attribution confusion;
- authority laundering;
- phase jumps;
- leakage of claim scope;
- missing return paths;
- hidden transformation paths;
- false closure of unresolved differences.

### Conditions for return to ③: explicit grounds

Return a question to the explicit treatment of assumptions and grounds when questions such as the following recur:

- Why was this connection adopted?
- Why is this kind of evidence sufficient?
- Why is this concept treated as real?
- At what point does the description become SO's own interpretation?
- What remains if the assumption is removed?

Clarifications obtained in ③ are returned to experiments in ① for further testing.

---

## 6. Conditions for recording in ④

When a question travels among ①, ②, and ③, and each movement changes its meaning, scope of application, or research method, that history becomes a candidate for the formative history in ④.

Preserve in particular:

- the initial question;
- the initial misrecognition;
- failed trials;
- reasons for correction;
- the object that became newly visible after correction;
- the point returned to SO itself;
- the point that came into contact with a private lineage;
- the point at which public reconstruction became necessary;
- residuals that still cannot be explained.

Do not later overwrite this history entirely with the vocabulary of the present.

---

## 7. Do not generate ⑤ automatically from this ledger

Do not immediately assign a research-field name merely because questions of the same apparent type have increased.

Only when the same problem structure recurs across multiple objects, multiple experiments, and multiple times should it be separated as a candidate field under ⑤.

Return that candidate to ① and re-examine whether it stands as an independent philosophical question.
