# Evidence Provenance Note

The evidence files in this directory are copied from experiment artifacts generated during 2026-08-21 to 2026-08-22.

They are preserved as historical records. Their terminology, scoring rules, and source ledgers may reflect the protocol state of that run and must not be silently normalized to the current method.

In particular:

- S/E/U/P and V* labels are historical/experimental where present.
- A model output is evidence of what that run produced, not evidence that the output is true.
- External source URLs appearing in the four-case three-layer result are preserved as recorded by that run and were not independently revalidated during this packaging step.
- Failed runs are retained because later methods were partly motivated by their failure modes.
