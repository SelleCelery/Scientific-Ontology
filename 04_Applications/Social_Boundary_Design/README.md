# 日本語正本

## 1. Directory Role / ディレクトリの位置づけ

`Social_Boundary_Design`は、AI、制度、組織、平和を、社会側の境界設計として扱う。

中心的な問いは導入可能性だけではない。誰が目的を設定し、許可、委任、監督、停止、修正を行うか、負荷と残渣が誰の境界へ現れるか、異議申立てと再照合の返路が残るかを問う。

# English Commensurated Rendering

## 0. Role

`Social_Boundary_Design` treats AI, institutions, organizations, and peace as problems of boundary design on the social side.

The central question is not only whether something can be introduced. It asks who sets the purpose; who authorizes, delegates, supervises, stops, and revises; at whose boundary burdens and residues appear; and whether return paths for objection and re-collation remain available.

## 1. Structural Position

```text
03 Tam / Goodness
Protocol, ethics, and commensuration
  +
05 Cognitive Dynamics and Communication Studies
Ports, capacity, responsibility, and organizational boundaries
  ↓
Social Boundary Design
AI adoption, institutional design, and peace specifications
```

AI-side response structure belongs to [`../AI_Adaptation/README.md`](../AI_Adaptation/README.md).

## 2. Public Scope

This README is not a replacement for standard science, law, policy, or established disciplinary definitions.

Included:

- a collation checklist for AI adoption;
- responsibility boundaries, stop conditions, and re-collatability;
- design principles for social boundaries and institutional adoption;
- peace specifications based on limited connection, ports, and reopening possibility.

Not included:

- definitive legal advice, procurement standards, or certification standards;
- suitability judgments for specific organizations;
- diplomatic or security operations;
- a universally validated empirical theory of peace;
- private operational variables.
