# English Commensurated Rendering

## 0. Role

`01_Sat_Truth` addresses **the network structure itself and the conditions under which it can exist**.

Before communication content, it asks what may count as existence, contact, observation, meaning, and knowledge; how far the inside and outside of a boundary can be described; and what cognition presupposes and abstracts away.

This is why metaphysics, axioms, Boundary Realism, conditions of meaning, and Boundary Epistemological Critique belong here.

## 1. Structural Position

```text
Private origins and highly compressed roots
  ↓ expanded into public structures
01 Sat / Truth
  ├─ Boundary Realism
  ├─ Public axioms
  ├─ Conditions of meaning
  └─ Boundary Epistemological Critique
  ↓
02 Raj / Beauty: communication content and generation of reading
  ↓
03 Tam / Goodness: communication protocols and management
```

A public foundation is not completion. It remains open to revision through applications, counterexamples, and commensuration residuals.
